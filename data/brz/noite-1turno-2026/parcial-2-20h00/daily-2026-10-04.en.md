## 1. Prediction market

In the [Polymarket winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), confirmed reading of October 4, 18:59 BRT, with the count under way, Flávio Bolsonaro rises 18.95pp, to 83.50% (USD 15.71M), and Lula falls 20.00pp, to 15.50% (USD 16.00M), against the confirmed reading of October 3, 20:23 BRT. The gap goes from 29.05pp to 68.00pp, and the move normalized by the pair sum is 19.83pp to each side. Total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 168.86M across 19 contracts, with USD 5.03M of new money since the reading of October 3. This edition keeps the last confirmed reading of the winner contract, the one of 18:59.

Flávio Bolsonaro stands 20.20pp above the recorded top of the series since April 14, 63.30% on October 1, and Lula 19.00pp below the recorded floor, 34.50% on April 25. The Renan Santos contract stands at 0.05% and remains the one with the largest accumulated volume in the presidential book, USD 17.10M.

The [second place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) and [third place books](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) of the first round resolve with the result of the count, and this edition does not publish a new price for them. ## 2. What the polling firms registered
