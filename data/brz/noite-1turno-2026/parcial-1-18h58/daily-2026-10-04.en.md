## 1. Prediction market

In the [Polymarket winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), confirmed reading of October 4, 18:59 BRT, with the count under way, Flávio Bolsonaro rises 18.95pp, to 83.50% (USD 15.71M), and Lula falls 20.00pp, to 15.50% (USD 16.00M), against the confirmed reading of October 3, 20:23 BRT. The gap goes from 29.05pp to 68.00pp, and the move normalized by the pair sum is 19.83pp to each side. Total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 168.86M across 19 contracts, with USD 5.03M of new money since the reading of October 3.

Flávio Bolsonaro stands 20.20pp above the recorded top of the series since April 14, 63.30% on October 1, and Lula 19.00pp below the recorded floor, 34.50% on April 25. The Renan Santos contract fell to 0.05% and remains the one with the largest accumulated volume in the presidential book, USD 17.10M.

The [second place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) and [third place books](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) of the first round resolve with the result of the count, and this edition does not publish a new price for them. The [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 2.60% (USD 0.09M).

## 2. What the polling firms registered

In the official TSE partial count of 18:44 BRT, with 47.26% of polling sections tallied and turnout of 79.10% in them, Flávio Bolsonaro had 50.20% of valid votes, Lula 41.63%, Augusto Cury 2.96%, Ronaldo Caiado 2.38% and Renan Santos 2.32%. A partial count is not a result: the order in which sections are tallied changes the picture over the night, and in 2022 the first round lead changed hands [with more than 70% of ballot boxes counted](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/politica/lula-virou-contra-bolsonaro-com-mais-de-70-das-urnas-apuradas-em-2022/).

In the nine national polls released on October 3, the eve of the vote, Lula was ahead in the first round in five and Flávio Bolsonaro in four. Two came out on the night of October 3, after the previous edition: AtlasIntel put [Lula at 47% and Flávio Bolsonaro at 44.1% of valid votes](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMitAFBVV95cUxNRjVZblgycmpOcmgwQ3hOX3ZPY2h1b0tQejMwM1JzTTdOLWdpaE05RVhYZENqbEQ0Qm1idi1xUWJKZGR6bndTTDU2dlgxVlM5TTAtR2MzRVJld3l5eHNZUUhpcnRNWWJXN0ZYc1o2eUNES1RobWVrYmUzUXgxcTZsUE5DSW1hQlVkblNUclBKRTNlb2RJWmd4bkIwZ0JMSjlFeU14US16MENMaGMxeG0wNnN1Wm4?oc=5), and Gerp's second round of the day put [Flávio Bolsonaro at 44% and Lula at 42%](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMimAFBVV95cUxNM1lianAtblUxZUppc043ZmtNSmt6NHBmTjhVakkzcTlCaFpEOUszYTFuWkxmbDZnZW9LbG1jUkFyYXgxWllyelJPSTFaeTd4QjlfQVQyYmFjRGlITlVUTG9QQ3dCc2FSRFdwNjZpZjFxUTBMZEJ0MkR4elpsOHh0a3R4WUhsVzBvOGV6T1RJeEJoWHp3UElFag?oc=5).
