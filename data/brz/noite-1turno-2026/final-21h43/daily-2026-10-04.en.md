## 2. What the polling firms registered

In the TSE tally of 21:44 BRT, with 99.59% of polling sections and turnout of 78.93% in them, Flávio Bolsonaro had 47.12% of valid votes, Lula 45.06%, Augusto Cury 2.89%, Renan Santos 2.25%, Ronaldo Caiado 2.19% and Romeu Zema 0.27%. The court marked the result as mathematically defined at 20:58, and the second round is [on October 25](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi2gFBVV95cUxOdGlzNzJKRG5nTmJ4dlVCN0I3dGpzZnNEV3VNTEM3MlE4eWRkM2tmaHpubzc3SWJjeVRGZ3FFa2d5YVV5SF9BNUd5YzM0c2V6YjgteGJ4cExHNFlSem1OYkU1OWYzQ2NOc1kxdWdkTk52ZGFWQVdyU3VucmlfZGRtTHlEQnJZdmR2c2VLREYwdGFtRDZFdWxxcC1EUWNLWWhQdmZ4bmtYd1h4TWJHd1ZTSHZ5bnFuaHJZYkhnMTNXdlFwTFBONUItYV9kY0ZhUnFkTGFxVmRJS1BVdw?oc=5).

In the nine national polls of the eve, Lula was ahead in the first round in five, and Flávio Bolsonaro in four: Palver, Futura and the two Gerp rounds. Their range went from 5.7 points for Lula, in valid votes in CNT/MDA, to 4 points for Flávio Bolsonaro, in Palver, and the final margin fell within it. In the partial counts of the night that margin reached 8.57 points at 18:44, with 47.26% of polling sections, and kept falling until the result.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Poll x ballot box:** five of the nine national polls of the eve put Lula ahead in the first round, and the TSE recorded Flávio Bolsonaro 2.06 points ahead in valid votes. The four that put him in the lead, Palver, Futura and the two Gerp rounds, had the same order as the result, and the final margin fell within the range of the nine.

> **Market x poll:** on the eve, with most national polls putting Lula ahead, the winner contract already paid 64.55% for Flávio Bolsonaro. With the first round result, it went to 84.25%.

> **Partial count x result:** at 18:44, with 47.26% of polling sections, Flávio Bolsonaro had 50.20% of valid votes and the margin was 8.57 points; with 99.59%, it ended at 47.12% and 2.06 points, without going above 50%.

## In summary

**There will be a second round between Flávio Bolsonaro and Lula, on October 25.** Flávio Bolsonaro finished ahead, 47.12% to 45.06% of valid votes, and the winner contract went to 84.25%.

**The polls of the eve split on the order, and the final margin fit within their range.** Four of the nine put Flávio Bolsonaro ahead.

**The PL leaves the first round with 19 of the 54 Senate seats and five governors elected.** Twenty states decided their government, and seven go to the second round.
