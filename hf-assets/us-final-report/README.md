# Final report · Relatório final · Informe final

**US 2026 Closing Report** (Relatório de fechamento EUA 2026 · Informe de cierre EE. UU. 2026), in English, Portuguese and Spanish. It puts together what AFOS recorded, side by side, from April to the November 3 election: the Polymarket prices on control of the House and the Senate, the national generic ballot polls and the press coverage, and sets those series next to the result and the reaction around the world.

**Version 1.0 uses projected results** (NBC News, 2026-11-06, 1:33 p.m. ET): states were still certifying, the Senate races in Alaska and Mississippi were uncalled and the national House vote was still being counted. Version 1.1 will add the official result and the comparison of the polling average with the national House vote.

The data series behind the report are in this dataset (`data/`, `polls/`, `press/`, `raw/`); wallets in the flow study are described without address or username. The report is also online, with light and blue themes, at https://www.afos-analytics.com/en/report/usa-2026 (PT: /pt-BR/report/usa-2026 · ES: /es/report/usa-2026). Quotations originally in another language are translated by AFOS.
