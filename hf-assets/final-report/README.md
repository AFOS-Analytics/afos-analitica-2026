# Final report · Relatório final · Informe final

**Relatório de fechamento Brasil 2026** (Brazil 2026 Closing Report · Informe de cierre Brasil 2026), in Portuguese, English and Spanish. It puts together the 186 days in which AFOS recorded, side by side, the Polymarket winner contract, the national polls registered with the TSE and the press coverage, and compares those series with the official result of both rounds.

|------|---------|

The data series behind the report are in this dataset (`data/`, `polls/`, `results/`, `election-night/`); the other sources are linked in the report itself.

The report is also online, with light and blue themes, at https://www.afos-analytics.com/en/report/brazil-2026 (PT: /pt-BR/report/brazil-2026 · ES: /es/report/brazil-2026). Quotations originally in another language are translated by AFOS.
