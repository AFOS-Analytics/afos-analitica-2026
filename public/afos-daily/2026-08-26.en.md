## 1. Prediction market

The prices on this page come from the [confirmed reading](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) of August 26, 3:19 PM BRT (6:19 PM UTC), and the total accumulated volume in the presidential book is USD 133.35M. Lula closed at 62.50% (USD 9.03M) with no change, a third straight day flat. Flávio Bolsonaro rose 0.90pp to 35.55% (USD 8.94M), the largest among the winner contracts.

The distance between the two fell from 27.85pp to 26.95pp. Checked in the database backup against the full record since April 14, it is the tightest since June 21, and it narrowed from one side only, because the leader gave up nothing. The 35.55% is also Flávio Bolsonaro's highest level since May 13, still 9.95pp below the series high, which is 45.50%, of May 6.

The largest move of the day, however, is in the third-place contract for the first round. Renan Santos gave up 6.00pp to 45.50% (USD 198,000), while Ronaldo Caiado gave up 3.00pp to 39.00% (USD 63,000). Both fell, and even so the distance between them shortened from 9.50pp to 6.50pp, because one fell twice as much as the other. In the winner contract they stood at 2.75% (USD 11.23M) and 0.45% (USD 6.59M).

A [later capture](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), confirmed at 8:08 PM BRT (11:08 PM UTC), shows the third-place contract giving part of the move back that same night: Renan Santos returned to 47.50% and Augusto Cury rose to 10.90%. The panel table follows the 3:19 PM reading.

In the [other contracts](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), almost nothing: Flávio Bolsonaro flat at 87.50% (USD 376,000) in second place, the impeachment of a Supreme Court justice at 3.40% (USD 84,000) for a third day, and the PL at 77.50% (USD 259,000) for the largest Senate caucus.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Polling vs polling:** the day's most relevant disagreement is not between market and polling, it is between two polls. Gerp and Indexa measured the same contest, in the same week, with overlapping fieldwork windows, and inverted the runoff. That is ten points between institutes, with no days in between to explain it. The panel publishes both and does not average them.

> **Price vs polling:** the market moved 0.90pp in favour of the runner-up on the very day one poll puts him ahead and another puts him five points behind. The price did not choose between the two readings, and its distance to the leader is the tightest since June 21.

> **Contract vs contract:** the largest move of the day was not in the contract on who wins, it was in the one on who finishes third, and both names in that pair fell together. Probability of winning and voting intention measure different things, and the panel does not subtract one from the other.

## In summary

1. **What disagreed today was two polls, not market and polling.** Gerp and Indexa, [registered with the TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), came out on the same day with overlapping fieldwork and inverted the runoff.

2. **The [price](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) gap is the tightest since June 21.** It settled at 26.95pp, and it narrowed from one side only, because the leader gave up nothing.

3. **The government rating has one sign and different sizes.** The week's three houses measure a negative balance, with five points of spread between them.
