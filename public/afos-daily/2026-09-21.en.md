## 1. Prediction market

The presidential outright winner contract on [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) closed the 17:01 certified reading with **Flávio Bolsonaro at 60.90%** (USD 11.37M accumulated) and **Lula at 38.50%** (USD 11.66M accumulated), a gap of **22.40pp**. Total volume in the presidential book adds up to **USD 152.91M**. Against the 00:06 certified reading the same day, Flávio rose 1.85pp and Lula gave up 3.00pp, and the gap went from 17.55pp to 22.40pp.

In the readings recorded through the day the move was larger than the certified reading shows, and the series of extremes is in this edition's closing section.

In the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) second-place contract the movement ran the other way: Flávio fell 2.00pp, to **68.50%** (USD 1.05M), and Lula gave up 0.15pp, to **29.40%** (USD 0.88M). It is the only book in which Flávio lost price on the day.

In the first-round third-place book, **Augusto Cury rose 7.70pp, to 40.25%** (USD 0.25M) and **Renan Santos fell 7.00pp, to 45.50%** (USD 0.36M), the largest rise and the largest drop on the panel. Ronaldo Caiado rose 4.00pp, to **15.50%** (USD 0.14M), and Romeu Zema gave up 0.55pp, to 0.50%. Renan Santos has already been paid 71.50% there on July 13.

The contract on [a Supreme Court justice leaving by impeachment before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at **4.30%** (USD 0.09M), 0.10pp below the 00:06 reading, in a thin book where a tenth does not separate news from noise. In the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the Liberal Party stays at 83.50% (USD 0.26M) and the MDB gave up 1.25pp, to 12.35%.

In the runoff it also tested five pairings, and [CNN Brasil](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMimgFBVV95cUxNMWxibDVlZ1dQRTlFWGlEcEl0M2lQbVBYT3pZRkxxdi1xaUx5MnlVbFZ4cU9NTHE5cE1xNi1qbTNjb2djeGtYV1NzUWphYnMyQ0xOdEUwUjRaRndaU042Y0YtLTNLUjdXbUNsRTFiZ0xlTGMwLU5sdGUtTzdLSFBJNlkwNy0tcGkyalJXNUUyOWlzMGJaUTd1cTdR?oc=5) records the result: Lula loses to Flávio 43% to 47%, to Romeu Zema 43% to 44% and to Ronaldo Caiado 43% to 44%, and beats Augusto Cury 43% to 35% and Renan Santos 43% to 34%.

## 3. What the press covered

On **September 21** the Official Gazette of the Union published the dismissal of **Eduardo Bolsonaro** from his post as a Federal Police clerk for job abandonment. The date matters: Personnel Order nº 135 was signed on **September 18** by minister Wellington César Lima e Silva, and the disciplinary administrative process was concluded by the Federal Police in **July**. What changed on September 21 was the publication, not the decision, as recorded by [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/governo-lula-demite-eduardo-bolsonaro-abandono-cargo-pf/), Poder360, Metrópoles and CartaCapital.

At the Supreme Court the picture stays where it was: the two requests to investigate justices tied to the Master case remain undated, and the request to revoke Daniel Vorcaro's detention has been with justice Edson Fachin since September 18, undecided. [Correio Braziliense](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMizAFBVV95cUxNbkZieFR2VVBlYjV4M0V1X0N3OURLaE95blVWWnRvczBQYXlNUEFxdmZqbjU5bjlKUlhZeXRUNFlLTC1CNFFXSG1JU1dNcGRobnRmdDBsSjA4cWgxTFF1bnZMa3pVSEg1ZUNySGtFSEpYV2pQemNDR3o0d2R3dVAzdEpPb042WGZnVjhtTzd3Nkx4VlB5aF91LVdEbzBlNlBicENieWJ0TUZnMTZsUmJxdVB5U1JCVURiamw5OXFDRlpHajd1THQ5UlUyZETSAdIBQVVfeXFMTVR4UUdaRTRLMHF5UWVfV3lyQTg1WkRZZjFiNDdyMVpvS3ZIRWE0WFROX2w5c25WYk5DSl9EQ3FwSG5JdHpJUnVhSlpJdEJNblNZS1VzRjNDUmlCOXhTTzV6Z2NGVDNVaGZZejBwODhfazd1WTJOUlZMMjBvSk5oSlBZMWZXQ0NjendTQmtucHd3aVJacG5DVDV1cE9aT1RLZ0E0ZWV0UXFmWlQ4VE5sand1YUtyQURCNXBUUTVsQ2VZeFVKVWtXWExNalN2cm9MQTJ3?oc=5) records the reaction to a photograph of the prosecutor-general with Vorcaro.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** the outright winner contract prices a 22.40pp distance, and the day's two polls measure a [statistical tie](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) in both rounds, with one point in the first round in each. The two instruments do not disagree by a little: they disagree on the order of magnitude of the race.

> **Poll × poll:** the divergence is larger in the third way than at the top, and the two firms measured the same days. What separates them is not time.

> **The signature of the five pairings:** in Palver, Lula scores 43% against all five opponents, and all the variation sits on the opponent's side. In BTG/Nexus he ranges from 44% to 47% depending on whom he faces. Two firms measuring the same pairings in the same week produce different response structures, and that is information about the instrument.

## In summary

**The gap in the outright winner contract opened to 22.40pp, and the day's extreme is not in the reading the panel publishes.** Flávio Bolsonaro rose 1.85pp, to 60.90%, and Lula gave up 3.00pp, to 38.50%, between 00:06 and 17:01. In the recorded readings the gap reached 23.80pp at 15:00 and Flávio 61.70% at 14:30, the two highest of the 158 days of series. Lula is not at his floor, 34.50%, from April 25.

**The third-place book moved more than the presidential one and closed almost the entire distance between the top two.** Cury rose 7.70pp, to 40.25%, Renan Santos fell 7.00pp, to 45.50%, and the gap between them shrank from 19.95pp to 5.25pp in sixteen hours. Cury has already been paid 60.30% there on September 4.

**The two national polls closed fieldwork on the same September 20 and disagree on everything they measured together.** BTG/Nexus puts Lula ahead in both rounds and Cury third, 6% against 3% for Renan Santos; Palver puts Flávio ahead in both and Renan Santos third, 11% against 2%. Eight points on the same name, between firms that measured the same days.
