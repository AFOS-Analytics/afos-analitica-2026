## 3. What the press covered

On the night of September 30, the full TSE [upheld the removal of the posts that attributed to Flávio Bolsonaro a plan to strip Our Lady of Aparecida of the title of patron saint](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/eleicoes/2026/mendonca-ajusta-voto-no-tse-para-liberar-post-de-tabet-sobre-nossa-senhora/) and cleared the post by comedian Antonio Tabet and the cases of reasonable doubt, after André Mendonça adjusted his vote, [as also reported by G1](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/30/apos-mendonca-mudar-voto-tse-decide-liberar-post-de-humorista-sobre-nossa-senhora-aparecida.ghtml). The session was agreed between Edson Fachin and Kassio Nunes Marques, and [Fachin asked that the crisis be resolved first at the TSE](https://g1.globo.com/politica/blog/camila-bomfim/post/2026/09/30/fachin-pede-a-nunes-marques-para-resolver-crise-do-caso-nossa-senhora-primeiro-no-tse.ghtml).

In the campaign, Lula [used his TV airtime to talk about betting sites and Our Lady](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/lula-usa-tv-para-falar-de-bets-e-nossa-senhora-enquanto-flavio-mira-eleitores-de-caiado-e-cury.shtml), while Flávio Bolsonaro targeted voters of Ronaldo Caiado and Augusto Cury. Allies expect that Lula [will skip the TV Globo debate of October 1](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/lula-busca-evitar-desgaste-e-deve-faltar-a-debate-da-globo-adiando-embate-com-flavio-bolsonaro.shtml), with no decision announced by the end of the day.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x poll:** of the three national polls registered for September 30, two put Lula ahead in the first round, and in the second round they range from Lula 1 point ahead, in Indexa, to Flávio Bolsonaro 5.5 points ahead, in Futura. In the winner contract the last recorded point of the day put the gap at 25.00pp in favour of Flávio Bolsonaro.

> **Firm x firm:** Futura puts Flávio Bolsonaro 2.8 points ahead in the first round, and Indexa puts Lula 5 points ahead, with fieldwork periods that overlap from September 27 to 29.

> **Third place:** the book puts Renan Santos 18.80pp ahead of Augusto Cury, and in the three national polls of the day the third-placed candidate is Ronaldo Caiado in Futura, Augusto Cury in Ideia, and Caiado and Cury tied in Indexa.

## In summary

**The gap in the winner contract closed the day at 25.00pp.** This is the last recorded point of September 30, above the 20.30pp of the previous day's confirmed reading.

**The national polls of the day disagree with each other.** Futura puts Flávio Bolsonaro ahead in both rounds, and Ideia and Indexa put Lula ahead in the first and show a statistical tie in the second.

**The Our Lady case was ruled on by the full TSE.** The court upheld the removal of the false posts about Flávio Bolsonaro and cleared Tabet's post.
