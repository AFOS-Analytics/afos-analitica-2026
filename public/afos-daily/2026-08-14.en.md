## 1. Prediction market

The confirmed reading of August 14, at 14:46 BRT, shows an almost motionless [presidential book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election). **Lula stands at 63.50% (USD 8.25M)**, the same value as the confirmed reading of August 12, and the recorded series shows 63.50% on every day from August 9 to August 14. Six days without variation in a contract of this size is information: the market did not react to anything that happened during the week. The **total cumulative volume of the presidential book is USD 122.91M**.

**Flávio Bolsonaro stands at 27.85% (USD 8.20M)**, up 0.20pp, and was the only name above 1% to rise. The distance between the top two narrowed from 35.85pp to **35.65pp**, and all of the movement came from him, not from the leader. No superlative: among the points recorded since May 16, 51 had Flávio at a level equal to or above today's, with a peak of 33.20% on June 2.

**[Renan Santos](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) fell 0.30pp, to 7.15% (USD 9.59M)**, the largest fall among names above 1%, even though he keeps the largest cumulative volume in the book in that range. **Caiado rose 0.10pp, to 1.05% (USD 5.70M)**. Zema and Haddad remain below 0.5%, the cutoff the panel uses to separate price from noise.

For the second and third place contracts, for the Senate and for the STF impeachment contract **there is no new confirmed reading on August 14**, and the values in force are those of August 12, 16:41 BRT, with the impeachment contract at 3.90% (USD 83 thousand), the thinnest among those tracked.

## 3. What the press covered

The bigger fact of the day is institutional, not electoral. On the night of August 13 the TSE **suspended the processing of new party affiliations** in the Filia system, after the improper inclusion of Flávio Bolsonaro in the Missão party and an attempt to change Lula's affiliation, [which was never completed](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/eleicoes/2026/tse-suspende-sistema-de-filiacoes-apos-falso-registro-de-flavio-e-tentativa-contra-lula/). ⚠️ The court itself stated that there was **no hacking**, but rather improper use of the tool by someone holding the party's credentials, a distinction that changes the nature of the case.

On August 13, Justice Nunes Marques [restored Flávio's PL affiliation and cleared the registration](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/08/13/nunes-marques-restabelece-filiacao-de-flavio-ao-pl-e-libera-registro-de-candidatura.ghtml), granting the requests filed. The [presidential candidacy was registered hours later](https://www.brasildefato.com.br/2026/08/14/tse-suspende-sistema-de-filiacao-partidaria-e-flavio-bolsonaro-pl-tem-candidatura-a-presidencia-registrada-apos-problema-com-o-partido-missao/). The candidate registration deadline closes on August 15.

A further electoral fact entered with no counterpart in the price: the **OAS announced on August 13** that it will send an observation mission to Brazil, headed by **José Miguel Insulza**, a former foreign minister of Chile and former Secretary General of the OAS itself. It is the fifth OAS mission in the country since 2018. International observation measures neither voting intention nor probability, and so it enters neither of the two instruments this panel crosses.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** Caiado ties with the leader in the runoff of PoderData of August 13 and is [priced at 1.05%](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) to win the election, with 4% to 5.7% in the first round depending on the house. Reaching the runoff and winning the runoff are different questions, and the panel does not add them together.

> **Price × institutional fact:** Flávio's price rose 0.20pp in the same window in which the [registry obstacle to his candidacy](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/08/13/nunes-marques-restabelece-filiacao-de-flavio-ao-pl-e-libera-registro-de-candidatura.ghtml) was removed. The panel records the order of events and **does not attribute cause**: a calendar coincidence is not a measured relationship.

> **Market × narrative:** the day carried the largest institutional fact of the window and the leader did not move 0.01pp. When the price stays put on a day like this, the information is in the absence of movement, not in it.
