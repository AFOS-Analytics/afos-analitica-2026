> **Three movements define the day.** [AtlasIntel published](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiYkFVX3lxTE5jZUJ6TUJOY05vTmxkTnhRWXhqU2oxRm1XNW8xREU5bFlBczdiaEllUnYyRGZKWjlJRjB3cXdXQWpKRjhOTHVTTENmalU2RXhfRld0b3RSTW1VUUdwTzN4djlR0gFnQVVfeXFMTnQ3NGJHZS1weENkVkhxMkVITkRyTWNhTU1CXzF3elU3Y19IeURYNlZYUUU5RmowLUVDZmdpaE8xaHprdG1aVXBIc1BzUG5yQVJHNnlEa0dqdktEZm5mTGNOeUxnV1ZSYw?oc=5) its first national poll after the audio between Flávio Bolsonaro and Daniel Vorcaro was released: Flávio drops six points in the runoff, falling to 41.8% versus 48.9% for Lula. [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) reacted materially — Lula rose to 45.50% (↑2.00pp in 26 hours) and Flávio fell to 28.15% (↓3.40pp), opening a gap of +17.35pp, the largest recorded in the May series. The inflation tail above 6.50% fell to 9.00% (↓5.05pp in 26h), and Flávio's team filed a complaint with the [TSE](/en/glossary#tse) requesting suspension of poll release.

## 1. Prediction market

In the [Polymarket presidential race](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Lula rose to **45.50%** (↑2.00pp in 26h, vs 43.50% at May 18 close) and Flávio Bolsonaro fell to **28.15%** (↓3.40pp, cycle low since inception). The gap between the two, which had narrowed to +11.95pp on May 18, reopened to **+17.35pp Lula** — a ↑5.40pp movement in 26 hours, the largest daily movement of the month. Accumulated volume in the presidential market: USD 78.85M, with the Lula contract at USD 5.54M and Flávio at USD 5.70M.

The third way reorganized. **Renan Santos** surged to **12.55%** (↑2.85pp in 26h) and consolidated the top of the third way in the presidential race, above Romeu Zema. In the 3rd place market, Renan took the lead with **35.50%** (↑4.00pp), surpassing [Zema, who collapsed to 28.50%](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) (↓8.00pp in 26h). Caiado, in the same market, fell to 13.50% (↓4.00pp). The migration is asymmetric: Flávio yields and the market prices Renan as the primary recipient, not Zema or Caiado.

In the [2nd place market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio remains at **62.50%** (↑0.50pp) — the [PL](/en/glossary#pl) electoral base holds in the runoff scenario despite the decline in the presidential race. Lula fell to 14.50% (↓1.50pp) and Renan rose to 9.50% (↑0.60pp in 26h, ↑1.80pp in 48h). Haddad recovered to 4.25% (↑1.70pp in 26h), gaining relevance as an internal [PT](/en/glossary#pt) hedge.

The [contract on STF minister impeachment before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) registered a slight recovery to **6.05%** (↑0.75pp in 24h vs the cycle low of 5.30% on May 18), but remains below the 8% pre-launch baseline. In the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) remains stable at 78.50% (clear dominance), while [Republicanos](/en/glossary#republicanos) collapsed to 4.20% (↓6.80pp vs the May 17 peak of 11.00%) — returning all recent gains.

In the [2026 inflation bands](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the high tail suffered the day's most material movement. The 7.00%+ band collapsed to **3.30%** (↓5.35pp in 26h vs 8.65% on May 18), and the combined bands above 6.50% fell to **9.00%** (↓5.05pp in 26h, ↓6.85pp vs the May 17 peak of 15.85%). Central band 5.00-5.49% remains at 28.00% (stable), and the 4.50-4.99% band rose to 21.75% (↑3.25pp). Technical reading: the market withdrew weight from the extreme fiscal scenario without moving the central band.

## 4. Day's divergences

> **Market × national survey:** [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) reacted in the direction of [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) but with different magnitude. [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) registered a gap of +12.7pp in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno); the market prices a gap of +17.35pp in implicit probability. The difference of 4.65pp between the two indicates that [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) continues more aggressive than the published survey — something of the Vorcaro impact remains priced into the market and was not fully captured by the institute.

> **Survey × survey:** [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) 1T of May 19 (+12.7pp Lula) vs [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) 1T of May 16 (+3pp Lula) = divergence between institutes of 9.7pp. Difference attributable to the time window ([Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) in field before the audio's impact reached the sample), to methodology ([AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) includes mention of the audio in the questionnaire, fact confirmed by the company itself) and to sample design. The [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) of May 22 will be the next data point capable of calibrating this divergence.

> **Market × institutional event:** Flávio's team filed with the [TSE](/en/glossary#tse) requesting suspension of survey release, but [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) continued moving in the direction unfavorable to Flávio throughout the day. Movement contrary to the narrative of institutional blocking as an effective tool for electoral protection — market prices that the information circuit has already circulated.

## In summary

1. **Published [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) confirmed part of what the market had already priced**, but the residual difference of 4.65pp between [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) and survey suggests that the Vorcaro impact remains partially outside the survey's picture. The next [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) (May 22, with Michelle scenario) is the next data point of divergence.

2. **The reorganization of the third way was asymmetric**: Renan Santos absorbed the market migration (presidential 12.55%, 3L top 35.50%); Zema gave up the 3L top for the first time (28.50%, ↓8.00pp); Caiado also lost position (3L 13.50%, ↓4.00pp). Market did not treat all of the non-Bolsonarist right the same way.

3. **The extreme fiscal tail (inflation ≥6.50%) collapsed 5.05pp in 26 hours to 9.00%**, an atypical movement for a day with electoral survey as central event. Technical reading: the inflation market appears to react to the government's institutional signaling (admission of error, legislative recalibration) in parallel to the political movement — not as a direct reflection of the survey.
