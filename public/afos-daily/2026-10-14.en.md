## 1. Prediction market

**The gap narrows again.** In the confirmed reading of October 14 at 18:12 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 85.00% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 20.12M) and 14.50% for Lula (USD 23.09M). Against the confirmed reading of October 13 at 16:14 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro falls 1.45pp and Lula rises 1.00pp; since the sum of the pair went from 99.95 to 99.50, the normalized movement is 1.07pp to each side. The gap goes from 72.95pp to 70.50pp, after having gone from 74.55pp to 72.95pp on October 13. In the 18:33 BRT re-reading, Flávio Bolsonaro was at 85.05% and Lula at 14.50%, and the total accumulated volume in the presidential book added up to USD 181.67M. The largest volume in the book is still Lula's.

**Supreme Court and inflation.** The contract on the [impeachment of an STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 2.70% (USD 0.10M) in the confirmed reading of 18:12 BRT, 0.35pp above the 2.35% of October 13. In the [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) one, the 5.00% to 5.49% range rises from 46.50% to 50.20% (USD 25 thousand) in the 18:33 BRT reading, against the October 13 one at 19:04 BRT; the 5.50% to 5.99% range goes from 16.05% to 21.05% (USD 21 thousand), and the 4.50% to 4.99% range falls from 26.90% to 18.70% (USD 32 thousand).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Intention × rejection:** in [Quaest](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirwFBVV95cUxNVUdhc1pwY1R3d3ZfMFB6NDlFWE9FQmVrNGh5c25ySzhRLTZBUE9ud21pdFpBSzBoUDAxUE1JSnVCazcyNWdVYlZmQ3piOVRhT3lDNUIwbDhORnZaZTFzMjJqWHhHNmpISzVUMXZvZ1VIdDJaUEd0NXd0aXV6V2RmTVpPRTljLWd4RDFGNkl6a1hJel9HNFoxOHBzalI4bm5nODhOWmpCaC0yOXBEM3Y4?oc=5), Flávio Bolsonaro leads in voting intention by 46% to 44% and has 54% rejection against 53% for Lula, a difference within the 2-point margin. The two numbers come from the same sample and measure different questions.

> **Poll × poll:** with fieldwork overlapping on October 12, Quaest measures 2 points for Flávio Bolsonaro and [Gerp](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMipwFBVV95cUxPckM3QjZDUWdJT3NpaXhJR0wxNm1BcTVMcEhSQTRZSUUtdFdTZGtSektnWG9ET2VSTlRMbU5RX0dWV3BoZWFPeTlLeVR3MlpZVkZmUFlIR1dtUU5KbmtZeVRyeW5GQlRLXzlWVTB0eWt6M2lfOW15WlhYaWdBbnJPclNvd3B4S3Z3QmUyMnIyYy1zVGdhRzJlUEtLSklwdVl3RVVjZl9FRQ?oc=5), 9. The margin among the eleven post-vote national polls ranges from 1.5 points for Lula to 9 for Flávio Bolsonaro.

> **Market × poll:** on the day Quaest measured a statistical tie, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) paid 85.00% for Flávio Bolsonaro, with the gap narrowing 2.45pp against October 13. Price measures the probability of winning on October 25, and the poll measures the week's voting intention.

## In summary

1. **Quaest measures 46% to 44% for Flávio Bolsonaro**, a statistical tie, and ten of the eleven post-vote national polls put him ahead.
2. **The gap in the winner contract narrows for the second day**, to 70.50pp, with Lula at 14.50% and Flávio Bolsonaro at 85.00%.
3. **Flávio Bolsonaro reverses course and will attend the Band debate**, on October 18, the first of the second round.
