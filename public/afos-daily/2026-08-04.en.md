## 1. Prediction market

This edition publishes prices from **Aug 3, 16:11 BRT**, and marks that on every figure. There is no fresh market reading on Aug 4. Total accumulated volume in the presidential race adds up to **USD 118.32M**, and in that reading the [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) had **Lula at 65.50% (USD 7.92M)** against **Flávio at 25.45% (USD 7.86M)**, a gap of **+40.05pp**.

Behind them, **[Renan Santos](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) at 7.45% (USD 8.86M)**, the name with the most money traded in the field despite the low price, **Caiado at 1.15% (USD 5.30M)**, **Zema at 0.25% (USD 4.66M)** and **Haddad at 0.15% (USD 6.64M)**, who is still not a candidate and whose contract prices a replacement scenario.

Among the side markets, the contract on [an STF justice being removed by impeachment before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stood at **3.10% (USD 83 thousand)**, the thinnest one the panel publishes. ## 2. What the institutes recorded

Today's [TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) sweep returned **537 filings and no new poll**. The day's data point came from yesterday's round: BTG/Nexus published, in the Aug 4 coverage and from the same filing **BR-02874/2026** (n=2,002, fieldwork Jul 31 to Aug 2), [**government approval at 47% against 48% disapproval**](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirwFBVV95cUxPTG1IZ1l2RVc4VWhsYlpVVFczMUw4dzJOSkNwQlEzY0lTU3B4eHZ6U3dCY0N0ZUlwVHBkM0pZSlpkZEY1LTdVc0czREhESl9FbEFRMHpndVhqclI5cFVjNmhOSGoyTms2QlFEaDY2VEVMWGNKVUdXZG1JNm13a2ZZeGZYcU8zS1l1V2IwNU1YYTZHZDhmQlBZYklSeElIai01cUlzN3M3LW84SEFkVXpn?oc=5).

The finding is not the level, it is the stillness. Inside the house itself, approval stands at **47% across the last three rounds**: 47% x 47% on Jul 13, 47% x 49% on Jul 27 and 47% x 48% now. What oscillated was disapproval, 2pp up and 1pp down, inside the margin both times. Administration ratings tell the same story: [**37% excellent or good against 43% poor or terrible**](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMilAJBVV95cUxORjdrNERsN2hXd3VaVzFrUFhjOGZxMmh1YnBxQ001ZU4tTmVJSTdMUEVPWV9QckFCeDZhMEJJWkNsTlJfUVExaG9oQmZ3SjFBeTV1WlZMLXRMWXVQb2FjSWx2M0l1aXN0UFA0QkEtOWZNSVVEcVdJZzVrTFRKUTJaeVJVOGt4dVJfb2RlamRiWW1zQkJ5eEF6S1FyM3RpaXVCSU9PVWtRYV82YmVMQmExc2FNN3lHd2FaVnRnRlNnN1pWSzdFMkhPTHhqNEZFaDM4ZkthcFF2YXY0cmZYZ09wcDJiRW1henZaMmJPeHhCRkZqUEhza19VWU9aYWZtWExpQTE1WEUyeS1qVmVnMHJGbWV1Rjc?oc=5), versus 36% and 43% on Jul 27. PoderData/Aya of Jul 30 still gives a considerably harsher picture, 43% x 49%, and the two houses keep pointing different ways.

## 3. What the press covered

The cleanest move of the day was Zema's. Folha de S.Paulo recorded that [Senator Eduardo Girão, of the Novo party, was announced as his running mate](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/08/senador-eduardo-girao-do-partido-novo-e-anunciado-como-vice-de-zema.shtml). He is the first of the three third-way names to close a ticket, and he closes it inside his own party, without depending on an alliance.

On the other side, Flávio had the reverse. Folha recorded that [Republicanos confirmed neutrality and refused to join the ticket](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/08/republicanos-confirma-neutralidade-na-eleicao-e-amplia-isolamento-de-flavio.shtml), and then that [Podemos also refused support and decided to stay neutral](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/08/podemos-recusa-apoio-a-flavio-bolsonaro-e-tambem-decide-ficar-neutro-na-eleicao.shtml), after resisting the nomination of its own president, Renata Abreu. O Globo reported [which figure led Ciro Nogueira to reject the coalition](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/malu-gaspar/post/2026/08/o-numero-que-levou-ciro-nogueira-a-rejeitar-coligacao-com-flavio-bolsonaro.ghtml) and that [Michelle Bolsonaro called him to press for Tereza Cristina](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/bela-megale/post/2026/08/michelle-bolsonaro-liga-para-ciro-nogueira-e-intercede-por-tereza-cristina-na-vice-de-flavio.ghtml). [Daniella Marques, without Republicanos' approval, will stay on the campaign as an adviser](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/08/04/sem-aval-do-republicanos-para-ser-vice-daniella-marques-seguira-na-campanha-como-conselheira-de-flavio.ghtml). Flávio told G1 that [the running-mate decision will come by August 15](https://g1.globo.com/politica/eleicoes/2026/noticia/2026/08/04/flavio-bolsonaro-diz-que-definicao-sobre-vice-saira-ate-o-dia-15-de-agosto.ghtml), which gives eleven days.

On the judicial axis, yesterday's request became today's decision: [Dino authorised the third inquiry into Lulinha and ordered an investigation into leaks](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/08/04/dino-autoriza-terceiro-inquerito-para-apurar-suspeitas-sobre-atuacao-de-lulinha-e-manda-investigar-vazamentos-sobre-caso.ghtml). The panel recorded yesterday that the Federal Police had **requested** it, and keeps the distinction between requesting and opening.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Polling × board:** Flávio is at once the name that gained most in the week's polling, from 33% to 37% in the first round of the Nexus poll, and the only one of the three who [lost a party today](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/08/republicanos-confirma-neutralidade-na-eleicao-e-amplia-isolamento-de-flavio.shtml). Both belong to the same seven-day window and point opposite ways.

> **Half the crossing:** the polling side of this edition is from today, the [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) side is from Aug 3. Without two readings that agree there is no price, and without a price there is no crossing. The panel says which half it published instead of estimating the other.

> **Approval × voting intention:** [government approval](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMivwFBVV95cUxNS21kdmFJbldSaExqcm90TlZuQlBINU8zQktCRVNOdHBwMDI1aWl0cWtvcmhaYkVvempQTlRjeC1RMVp0VEQ2V3JNdzM1QXp3cWt2Njd1X2tub3ZiU0dpZzBkWVNkMV92cDRoekxMcTVQR1lVRFhpM1FOaHIzOUhaZjZ6YnA3eEVNWHJQaUZrV1lDTGlIbnhPOUhELVdoZVE1ZHNLX0ZrM0Q5WDJtQ0l2aGZoeTJ4LThDMXF4UktKNA?oc=5) has not moved for three Nexus rounds, while first-round voting intention shifted 4pp in the same house. They are different questions and do not add up, but the distance between them is the finding of the week.

## In summary

1. **The stillness is the data.** Three rounds from the same house at [47% approval](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirwFBVV95cUxPTG1IZ1l2RVc4VWhsYlpVVFczMUw4dzJOSkNwQlEzY0lTU3B4eHZ6U3dCY0N0ZUlwVHBkM0pZSlpkZEY1LTdVc0czREhESl9FbEFRMHpndVhqclI5cFVjNmhOSGoyTms2QlFEaDY2VEVMWGNKVUdXZG1JNm13a2ZZeGZYcU8zS1l1V2IwNU1YYTZHZDhmQlBZYklSeElIai01cUlzN3M3LW84SEFkVXpn?oc=5) describe a divided and stable electorate, not a trend.
2. **The third way gained party definition.** [Zema is the first to close a ticket](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/08/senador-eduardo-girao-do-partido-novo-e-anunciado-como-vice-de-zema.shtml), and he does it without depending on a coalition.
3. **Flávio's isolation is a recorded fact, not a reading.** Two parties [refused on the same day](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/08/podemos-recusa-apoio-a-flavio-bolsonaro-e-tambem-decide-ficar-neutro-na-eleicao.shtml), and the deadline he himself declared is [August 15](https://g1.globo.com/politica/eleicoes/2026/noticia/2026/08/04/flavio-bolsonaro-diz-que-definicao-sobre-vice-saira-ate-o-dia-15-de-agosto.ghtml).
