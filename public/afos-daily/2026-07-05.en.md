## 1. Prediction market

At 91 days until the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) spent a Sunday practically flat. **Lula remained stable at 60.50% (USD 7.2M)** and **Flávio Bolsonaro declined slightly to 22.30% (↓0.10pp, USD 7.2M)**, so the presidential gap rose marginally to **+38.20pp (↑0.10pp)**, still below the peak of +39.55pp recorded on July 3. The total accumulated volume in the presidential market exceeds USD 110M, in a net book where the absence of movement reflects the lack of new electoral data (Section 2), not lack of business.

The most visible shift of the day occurred in a sub-market. In the [2nd place of the 1º turno](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), **Flávio fell to 72.5% (↓6.0pp, USD 142k)**, while **Renan Santos rose to 11.4%** in that same market, inheriting part of what the senator ceded. Flávio continues with a comfortable lead as Lula's certain challenger, but the share lost is the day's record. In the winner's market, **Renan fell to 9.85% (↓0.10pp, USD 7.8M)**, below 10%, still with the highest accumulated volume in the presidential race, and maintained favoritism for [3rd place in the 1º turno](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) at 62.5%, ahead of Caiado (12.5%) and Zema (6.5%, ↓1.0pp).

At the bottom of the race, **Michelle Bolsonaro stood at 1.65% (USD 8.6M)**, **Caiado at 1.05% (USD 4.6M)**, **Zema at 1.05% (USD 4.2M)**, **Camilo Santana rose to 0.95% (USD 3.8M)** and **Haddad stood at 0.80% (USD 6.1M)**. In the [Senate market by number of seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) remained at the top at 87.5% (USD 250k), and the [institutional risk market](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) **STF impeachment remained stable at 2.75% (USD 82k)**, at the floor: real money keeps the probability of removing a minister by impeachment before 2027 low.

In the [2026 annual inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the 5.00% to 5.49% band leads with 37.80% (USD 8k), followed by the 4.50% to 4.99% band with 28.40%; together, the two central ranges account for 66.20% of the probability, keeping the macro scenario priced within the 4.50% to 5.49% range.

## 2. What the institutes recorded

The day brought no new national presidential survey: the latest national remains [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel)/Bloomberg from July 1 (n=5,000, field from June 25 to 30), which showed [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) Lula 46.3% to Flávio 36.6% and [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) Lula 48.8% to Flávio 42.3%. ## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × survey (level):** with the market stalled, Lula's large lead (+38.20pp) continues without counterpart in surveys. The latest national ([AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) 01/Jul) shows +6.5pp in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno), and [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) fieldwork 17-19/Jun shows [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) (47% x 43%). No new national survey entered the day to arbitrate the divergence.

> **Market × market (2nd place):** the only movement of the day was internal to the sub-markets book: Flávio lost 6.0pp from 2nd place in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) (72.5%) while Renan rose to 11.4% there, without the winner market moving. It is probability reallocation among opposition names, not a change in the Lula x bolsonarista field reading.

> **Market × survey (Renan):** the largest divergence on the dashboard persists, with Renan below 10% in the winner market (9.85%) and favorite for 3rd place in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) (62.5%), while nationals measure him at around 4% in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno). The market treats him as a probable 3rd finisher, not as a competitive candidate for victory.
