## 1. Prediction market

**The gap widens by half a point.** In the confirmed reading of October 17 at 16:59 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 76.95% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 21.50M) and 23.50% for Lula (USD 25.39M). Against the confirmed reading of October 16 at 17:01 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro rises 0.50pp and Lula holds steady; since the pair's sum went from 99.95 to 100.45, the normalized move is 0.12pp for each side. The gap goes from 52.95pp to 53.45pp, after four straight days of narrowing that took it from 72.95pp on October 13 to 52.95pp on October 16. Total cumulative volume in the presidential market comes to USD 185.34M, and the largest volume in the book is still Lula's.

**Supreme Court and inflation.** The contract on the [impeachment of an STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 2.70% (USD 0.10M), the same as the October 16 reading. In the [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) contract, in the 17:25 BRT reading against the October 16 reading at 17:02 BRT, the 5.00% to 5.49% band goes from 60.80% to 60.15% (USD 27 thousand), the 5.50% to 5.99% band from 18.40% to 16.35% (USD 21 thousand) and the 4.50% to 4.99% band from 17.00% to 16.70% (USD 32 thousand).
