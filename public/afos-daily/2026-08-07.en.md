## 1. Prediction market

This edition publishes the reading of **Aug 7, 16:44 BRT**, confirmed by two independent readings. The [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) has **Lula at 64.50% (USD 8.11M)** against **Flávio at 26.95% (USD 8.08M)**, a gap of **+37.55pp**. Total accumulated volume in the presidential market is **USD 120.64M**.

**The gap narrowed for the sixth day running, and the cause sits at one end only.** In the [AFOS series](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), the sequence since the high of **+41.80pp on Aug 1** ran through +40.90pp, +38.90pp, +38.60pp, +38.50pp and +37.90pp. Over that stretch Flávio went from 24.70% to 26.95%, while Lula has been flat at 64.50% since Aug 4. In the first half of the move it was Lula who shifted, from 66.50% to 64.50%. With no event in the window, this is momentum, not a reaction to news.

**The bigger move of the day was not in the winner contract.** In the [first round runner-up book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio **fell 4.50pp, from 87.50% to 83.00% (USD 237 thousand)**, on the same day his winner price rose. There is no arithmetic contradiction, because gaining a chance of winning takes away a chance of finishing second, but it is the biggest move on the panel since Aug 6.

In [third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) there was a mirror swap: **Renan Santos rose 2.50pp, to 58.50% (USD 176 thousand)**, and **Caiado fell 2.00pp, to 33.50% (USD 42 thousand)**. On Aug 6 the move had been the reverse.

In the presidential book, **Renan Santos sits at 7.25% (USD 9.19M)**, still with more money traded than Lula, **Caiado at 1.55% (USD 5.58M)** and **Zema at 0.45% (USD 4.83M)**. Among the side markets, [the impeachment of an STF justice](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) rose to **3.10% (USD 83 thousand)**, and the **PL is at 73.00% (USD 259 thousand)** in the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Book × book:** Flávio's winner price **rose 0.10pp** and his [runner-up contract **fell 4.50pp**](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) on the same day. The two contracts speak about the same candidate and disagree on where he lands. AFOS records both instead of picking the one that confirms a thesis.

> **Market × poll:** the [gap](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) tightened for **six straight days** and the polling was not measured once in the period. This is not disagreement: one of the two measurements does not exist on those days. The five national polls of Aug 10 and Aug 11 are the first chance to compare.

> **News × price:** both front runners took hits on the judicial axis this Friday and the winner contract moved **0.10pp**. The panel records the coincidence of dates, not the cause.

## In summary

1. **The [gap](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) closed at +37.55pp, the sixth day of tightening**, all of it from Flávio rising, not Lula falling.
2. **The [runner-up contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) fell 4.50pp for Flávio** on the same day his winner price rose, the biggest move on the panel since Aug 6.
3. **Five national polls are registered with the [TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) for Aug 10 and Aug 11**, one of them with n=5,000, and four were still in the field on Friday.
