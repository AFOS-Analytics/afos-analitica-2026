## 1. Prediction market

O [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) fez uma reversão parcial após o gap recorde da véspera. **Lula** recuou a **56.50% (USD 6.9M)**, queda de 1.00pp ante o pico de ontem, enquanto **Flávio Bolsonaro** subiu a **23.45% (USD 7.0M)**, alta de 0.55pp, de modo que o gap entre os dois fechou em **+33.05pp**, recuando do recorde de +34.60pp registrado em June 24. O movimento devolveu parte do prêmio esticado na véspera, mas mantém Lula em posição dominante no topo. O volume total acumulado no mercado presidencial segue acima de **USD 105M**, reforçando a leitura de dinheiro real ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election)).

Na terceira via e nos nomes seguintes, **Renan Santos** recuou a **11.45% (USD 7.5M)**, queda de 0.90pp, e seu favoritismo ao 3º lugar do [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) caiu de 58.5% para **44%** no sub-mercado, com Caiado (12.5%) e Zema ganhando terreno. **Michelle Bolsonaro** entrou no book a **2.65% (USD 8.2M)** em meio ao episódio do dia. O grupo da terceira via subiu de leve do piso: **Caiado** a **1.65% (USD 4.4M)**, alta de 0.20pp, **Zema** a **1.15% (USD 3.9M)**, alta de 0.20pp, e **Haddad** a **1.25% (USD 5.9M)**, queda de 0.20pp, na faixa de Camilo Santana (1.15%).

Nos sub-mercados, Flávio mantém isolado o **[2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) do [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) (78.5%)**, e o **[PL](/en/glossary#pl) lidera o Senado por número de cadeiras (76.5%)**, à frente de [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) (11.2%) e [União Brasil](/en/glossary#uniao-brasil) (4.95%). O mercado de **impeachment no [STF](/en/glossary#stf)** seguiu no piso, a **2.55% (USD 82 mil)**, sinal de risco institucional baixo ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027)).

No mercado de **inflação de 2026**, a expectativa cedeu mais: a banda mais baixa, **4.50% a 4.99%, passou a liderar a 44.85%** (vinha de 32.85% em June 24), à frente da faixa 5.00% a 5.49% (37.9%) e da 5.50% a 5.99% (16.9%). É um pano de fundo macro de inflação esperada menor.

## 3. What the press covered

In the opposition field, the day was marked by a personal episode. **Flávio Bolsonaro apologized to Michelle Bolsonaro** after she stated, in videos, that she had been disrespected amid disagreements over [PL](/en/glossary#pl) alliances in Ceará. Flávio denied having humiliated the former First Lady, said he had invited her personally, and stated he was not reciprocated, in an episode that exposed the split in the Bolsonaro family ([Brasil de Fato](https://www.brasildefato.com.br/2026/06/25/flavio-pede-desculpas-a-michelle-apos-video-que-expoe-racha-na-familia-bolsonaro/), [Jornal de Brasília](https://jornaldebrasilia.com.br/noticias/politica-e-poder/flavio-bolsonaro-nega-humilhacao-pede-desculpas-e-diz-nao-ser-correspondido-por-michelle/)). ## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market vs. research:** [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) maintains Lula dominant at 56.50%, with a gap of +33.05pp over Flávio, while PoderData/Aya, the second consecutive national poll, points to a [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) in [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) (46% x 43%). The market corrected yesterday's overstatement, but continues pricing in the Tier 1 consensus (Indexa 2T 47% x 40%, [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) 2T 47% x 43%), which gives Lula greater cushion than today's poll.

> **Research vs. research:** PoderData brought the lowest approval in the recent sample (43% x 52%), against the 48% level from [Nexus/BTG Pactual](/en/glossary#nexus-btg) and [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) rounds. The divergence between institutes on government evaluation is, in itself, a signal to monitor.

> **Market vs. market:** Renan fell both in the winner market (11.45%) and in the third-place sub-market (from 58.5% to 44%), with Caiado and Zema advancing. The space of the non-Flávio right, formerly concentrated in Renan, became more contested in the same trading session.

## In summary

1. The market returned part of yesterday's record (Lula 56.50%, gap +33.05pp) on the day when PoderData/Aya tightened the [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) to [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) (46% x 43%), the second consecutive national poll in that direction.
2. The political field was dominated by ticket movements and personal episodes: Haddad confirmed França as running mate in SP, Flávio apologized to Michelle and Zema denied alliances.
3. Expected inflation eased further (the 4.50% to 4.99% band took the lead at 44.85%) and impeachment risk at [STF](/en/glossary#stf) remained at floor (2.55%), on a day with no acute institutional trigger.—
