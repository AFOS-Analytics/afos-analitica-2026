## 1. Prediction market

The prices in this synthesis are from the confirmed reading of August 31, 1:43 PM BRT, and the total accumulated volume adds up to **USD 140.52M** in the [presidential book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election). Lula sits at **55.50% (USD 9.53M)** and has not left that number for a third day running, across 19 captures of the series since the afternoon of August 29. Flávio Bolsonaro gave up **2.05pp, to 39.05% (USD 9.37M)**, the largest move on the panel this round.

The [distance between the two](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) widened to **16.45pp**, against 14.40pp on August 30, and the widening came entirely from one side. The fall undoes much of the four-close rise that took the runner-up from 35.50% on August 27 to 41.20% on August 30, and with no isolable trigger event in the window it is a band exit by momentum.

In the placement contracts, [second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) keeps Flávio Bolsonaro at **85.50% (USD 0.44M)**, unchanged: the market still treats his reaching the runoff as near certain, and what moved was the expectation about the runoff. This round publishes no new number for the third-place contract.

In the [outright-winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Renan Santos sits at **1.85% (USD 11.95M)**, down 0.40pp, and his volume is the largest in the book among the names below the top two. Ronaldo Caiado, at **0.15% (USD 6.97M)**, and Romeu Zema, at **0.05% (USD 6.37M)**, remain below the 0.5% floor of the double reading, and this round publishes no new price for them.

In the institutional markets, the contract on a [Supreme Court justice leaving by impeachment before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) sits at **3.60% (USD 84 thousand)**, unchanged, the smallest book on the panel. In the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the PL is at **79.00% (USD 0.26M)** to elect the largest bench, with the MDB at 14.30%. In [2026 annual inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the 5.00% to 5.49% band leads with **46.40%**.

## 2. What the pollsters registered

Two national polls were released on August 31, the first since Vox Brasil of August 29. **AtlasIntel/Bloomberg** (BR-07972/2026, fieldwork August 25 to 30, n=5,014, electronic form, margin of 1pp) puts the first round at **43.4% to 33.7%** and the runoff at **47.1% to 42.6%**. **BTG/Nexus** (BR-08900/2026, fieldwork August 28 to 30, n=2,005, phone, margin of 2pp) puts them at **39% to 33%** and **46% to 45%**. The two agree on who is ahead and disagree on the size: 9.7pp against 6pp in the first round, and 4.5pp against 1pp in the runoff. Fieldwork for both closed on August 30, already after the free electoral airtime debuted on August 28.

The move both register is the third-placed name's. ## 3. What the press covered

The Electoral Court acted against both leading campaigns on the same Sunday, August 30, and both rulings became public on August 31. Dias Toffoli, in a monocratic and precautionary decision, [suspended the Renan Santos ticket's digital advertising, its transfer of electoral fund resources and its right to take part in debates](https://www.metropoles.com/colunas/andreza-matais/exclusivo-toffoli-nega-registro-de-candidatura-de-renan-santos). The reason is the late reporting of the social media profiles: the registration was filed on August 7 and the profiles only on August 19. The [decision does not deny the registration](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/politica/tse-suspende-campanha-de-renan-santos/), it suspends the campaign, and the ticket has 24 hours to provide information. The candidate [denies any irregularity](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi7AFBVV95cUxPSExNS1BzS2xRMzNvNGg5SlJIR3lyRDBsNjJSRzN4eXBPclVid1k3RmVMVmNQZ2FOTWJCblpOUGR5RjFSV0JwdzBaTGc3eDhpLUhVU0xTSS1PMjBUS1RkWGw2NkZDS2wzZ0d5N19wYWlYcDFVcmtrUGZlRk9nUkotUTZQajZVQTN1emRGX2plamRBM2VaRVpaX2Rlclp4QkI0N1oxbWlfSHBvY2psMEZjYmRwQnhybHZ5UV9tSVcwTF9DWVV5NFUyajBidkdCcHZ2NC0td1RFaUs0ZjhiajhBeEplVjF4LVMxR1k1Uw?oc=5).

On the other side, Justice Estela Aranha [suspended the Lula campaign spot titled "Conheça o currículo de Flávio Bolsonaro"](https://jornaldebrasilia.com.br/noticias/politica-e-poder/tse-suspende-propaganda-de-lula-que-lista-acusacoes-contra-flavio-bolsonaro/), which cited Banco Master and the payroll kickback scheme. The ruling held that the content goes beyond the limits of admissible criticism by presenting the senator as charged in proceedings whose complaints were dismissed. The case goes to the full panel, and the opposing campaign [asked for a right of reply](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiwwFBVV95cUxPcVZkejhyY3FTMDNVWHU5bFNYaUs5Q0p4Rzd1MzlJd3Z2bUVUcmlMTDJheWZpc0JvaExOTlhxUUhCVjdVQ3c4YVc5LWtTVTl2dmp0by1QX1VRQzhKMFNyQ1lNaGc5a0ZoWURZRUJKV21oaW9EQ041czlWOUQ5YVFjUVJLQzgzUWFFOHhYZFFSRFd5MmlrZnRFSkU3QjVfcHR2TmxadXRYODJ4M0p5clB2cldJODd1LTJPeEdPTUtaVFZ3R00?oc=5).

The top two names spoke about the Supreme Court on the same day: after the defeat in the Senate, Lula said he will [nominate Messias to the court again](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMisgFBVV95cUxOX1JCRll5ZGJoZHJNZDVSclpHZ2VFU0JmODFaWHUtc2dEbGxUdk90ek43dEVZNm9Zd1kyRlVCcGNnb2ZSZXhPcVp3UEEzY21aS3pza1dGYmduekUxSE1VLUNfcXJFTXd4SkI4MGpvalZCSTJIVUlYNmxrMVBXX0RsMTZjRTJmYkFUaXJwWl9MSDl2M25kZHNyRlotTjdnOV9WYzJ2d0c1Y1J3U2s1c281NEFR?oc=5), and Flávio Bolsonaro [is betting on changes at the court](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/eleicoes/2026/flavio-aposta-em-mudancas-no-stf-para-recuperar-credibilidade-da-corte/).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market and polls, in the first round:** for the first time this week the two move the same way. The runner-up gives up 4 points in Nexus against the same house and gives up 2.05pp in the price, on the same day. It is not proof of cause: it is a coincidence of direction, and the panel records both measurements without arbitrating between them.

> **Poll and poll, on the size:** the two disagree by 3.7 points on the first-round lead and by 3.5 on the runoff, with different methods and samples, 5,014 against 2,005. Disagreement on size between pollsters is instrument behaviour, not one of them being wrong.

> **Ballot and price, on the third-placed name:** Augusto Cury multiplies by five in Nexus and reaches double digits, and this round publishes no new price for him. His contract opened on August 27 and the series has 68 captures, too short for a superlative.

## In summary

1. **The two measurements moved together in the first round, and it is the first time this week.** The runner-up gave ground in the declared ballot and in the price, and the distance to the leader widened by 2.05pp.
2. **The third-placed name's jump exists on one side only.** Eleven percent in Nexus against 2% from the same house a week earlier, with no new price to check it against.
3. **The Electoral Court hit both leading campaigns on the same Sunday**, in monocratic rulings that became public on Monday.
