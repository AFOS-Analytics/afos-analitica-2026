## 1. Prediction market

In the [Polymarket outright winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), in the reading confirmed on September 24, 22:45 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro stands at 55.55% (USD 11.79M) and Lula at 43.50% (USD 12.18M), with the gap at 12.05pp against 18.35pp in the certified reading of September 23. Lula rises 3.00pp raw and 3.15pp normalized, and Flávio Bolsonaro gives up 3.30pp and 3.15pp, neither of them at an extreme of the series recorded since April 14. Total accumulated volume in the presidential book is USD 154.90M across 19 contracts.

In the [first round runner-up contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) he goes the other way: up 4.00pp raw, to 75.50% (USD 1.14M), while Lula gives up 1.35pp, to 24.50% (USD 0.98M). There the raw figure overstates it, and normalized the move is 2.05pp.

In the [third place book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) almost nothing moved: Renan Santos stays at the same 44.00% (USD 0.41M), Augusto Cury rises 0.10pp, to 39.95% (USD 0.27M), the distance between them closes to 4.05pp and Ronaldo Caiado gives up 0.50pp, to 14.00%. Renan Santos leads volume in both books without leading the price, USD 14.39M and USD 1.36M. The [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) gives up 0.95pp, to 3.35% (USD 0.09M), and the Senate book has no new reading.

## 3. What the press covered

Estadão published on September 24 messages from the phone of Daniel Vorcaro, in custody since November 17, 2025. They show [four attempts by Flávio Bolsonaro to contact the banker between November 13 and 16, 2025](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/flavio-bolsonaro-tentou-ligar-vorcaro-4-dias-antes-prisao-banqueiro/), confirmed by Gazeta do Povo with its own sources, and part of them concerns the financing of the film Dark Horse. The [Globo group carries the same material](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/24/flavio-bolsonaro-tentou-falar-com-vorcaro-na-vespera-da-prisao-do-banqueiro-mostram-mensagens.ghtml).

On the other side, it brings [a lawyer's account of thanks for whisky sent by the banker](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/advogado-relata-agradecimento-de-lula-por-whisky-enviado-por-vorcaro/) and the effort to get closer to the government. In Rio, the president replied that [a president's friend is the people and not Vorcaro](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/24/lula-diz-que-amigo-de-presidente-e-o-povo-nao-e-vorcaro-dono-do-banco-master.ghtml), and Flávio Bolsonaro [asked the Supreme Court to investigate the relationship between the two](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/flavio-bolsonaro-investigado-no-caso-dark-horse-pede-que-mendonca-apure-relacao-entre-lula-e-vorcaro.shtml).

On the institutional front, [Galípolo confirmed a Federal Police summons](https://www.estadao.com.br/economia/galipolo-intimacao-pf-depor-outubro-master/) and is due to testify in early October about the supervision of Banco Master, with Roberto Campos Neto and André Esteves summoned as witnesses. [Gilmar Mendes took to the full bench of the Supreme Court](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/economia/gilmar-envia-ao-plenario-do-stf-disputa-bilionaria-ligada-ao-caso-master-e-turma-do-kn/) a dispute over power plant court-ordered debts of R$ 103 billion, after messages from the same material showed interest in one justice's vote. And Vorcaro's testimony set for the day did not happen: [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/pf-adia-novamente-depoimento-vorcaro-marcado-para-esta-quinta/), the only source for the point, reports a postponement by the PF at the request of the defence.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x poll:** the market moved 3.15pp of implied probability towards Lula on the day the six national polls, each measured against its own previous wave, came out 3 to 3. The median of the six [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) readings puts Flávio Bolsonaro one point ahead, and the price closed the gap. The panel measures both instruments and does not attribute the movement of one to the other.

> **Book x book:** in the same instant of the same reading, Flávio Bolsonaro gives up 3.30pp in the winner contract and rises 4.00pp in the runner-up one. They are different questions, and there the raw figure overstates his gain, because the sum of that pair inflated from 97.35 to 100.00.

> **Poll x poll:** Renan Santos runs from 8% at Palver to 2.4% at Futura, and because the six came out on the same day that distance cannot be put down to the calendar.

## In summary

**The market moved more in one step than in any other since this record began.** The gap closed from 18.35pp to 12.05pp between the certified readings of September 23 and 24, 6.30pp at once, almost twice the second largest, the 3.35pp from September 21 to 22. It is the third straight day of closing, and this time the raw and the normalized figures say the same thing: the sum of the pair stood at 99.05 against 99.35, and when it barely moves there is no overpricing left to redistribute.

**Six national polls on the same day did not produce an answer, they produced a spread.** In the first round it runs from Lula 40% to 33% at Alfa to Flávio Bolsonaro 40.4% to 38.4% at Futura, and in the second from Lula 47% to 45% at Datafolha to Flávio Bolsonaro 49.4% to 43.7% at the same Futura, the only one of the six outside the poll's own margin. Measured firm by firm against its own previous wave, the direction comes out 3 to 3, and the third way splits more than the front pair: Renan Santos runs from 8% at Palver to 2.4% at Futura.

**The Master case moved through the release of material, not through a decision, and the material did not pick a side.** The messages published by Estadão show four attempts by Flávio Bolsonaro to contact the banker between November 13 and 16, 2025, and the effort by a lawyer to bring him closer to the Lula government. Nothing was decided: Vorcaro's testimony was postponed, Galípolo's is set for early October, and the Supreme Court impeachment contract gave up 0.95pp, to 3.35%, with the panel linking neither to the other.
