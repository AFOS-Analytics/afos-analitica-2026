## 1. Prediction market

With 66 days to the first round, total accumulated volume in the presidential market is around **USD 116.58M** ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) via the AFOS proxy, captured July 30 at 5:08pm BRT). **Lula** rose 1.00pp and returned to **63.50% (USD 7.74M)**. **Flávio Bolsonaro** was **flat** at **23.95% (USD 7.71M)**, and the gap between them widened from +38.55pp to **+39.55pp**.

The 63.50% needs a line of context before any reading: the value **ties** the top of the AFOS series for the third time, set on July 26, July 28 and now July 30, and **does not exceed it** on any of them. The gap sits **0.25pp below** the series maximum, the +39.80pp of July 26. And the distinction that organizes the day is about who moved: the gap widened **by movement on one side only**, so there was no repricing of Flávio's candidacy. His run of four daily closes on the rise was broken **without a fall**.

In the [chasing pack](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), **Renan Santos** fell 0.25pp, to **8.45% (USD 8.59M)**, below every daily close of the past week. **Ronaldo Caiado** rose 0.20pp, to **2.55% (USD 5.20M)**, adding up to 0.65pp over two sessions from the 1.90% of July 28. **Jair Bolsonaro** stayed at **1.05% (USD 5.26M)**, **Romeu Zema** fell 0.20pp to **0.55% (USD 4.57M)**, **Fernando Haddad** fell 0.10pp to **0.15% (USD 6.38M)** and **Tarcísio de Freitas** remains at **0.15% (USD 13.66M)**, still the highest accumulated volume in the book, a legacy anomaly that measures traded history and not current conviction.

The strongest move of the day is not in the winner contract. In [first-round second place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), **Renan Santos collapsed from 11.70% to 6.10%**, a fall of 5.60pp in a contract of **USD 1.09M**, while Flávio rose 0.50pp and reached **79.00%**. In [third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), in a book of **USD 484,000**, Renan **rose** 0.50pp, to **62.00%**, and Caiado stayed put at **25.50%**. Added together, the three moves say the same thing: the money did not take Renan out of the election, it **reclassified** where he stands.

In the other markets, [impeachment of an STF justice](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) **fell** 0.25pp, to **3.10% (USD 83,000)**, undoing part of the previous day's 0.55pp rise. In the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the **PL** fell 5.50pp, to **65.50% (USD 258,000)**, and the MDB rose 0.60pp, to **18.20%**. In [inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the modal band is still 5.00% to 5.49%, at **38.30%**, and the 4.50% to 4.99% band fell 2.40pp, to **32.50%**, in a book of **USD 81,000** that is not covered by the double-reading capture guard. A large move in a small book is a record, not a signal.

A note on capture, and this time it is short: the double-reading capture guard ran **once and approved**, with no divergence at all across the five books it covers. It had blocked on both of the previous rounds. Every price goes in firm, with no declared range.

## 3. What the press covered

The day's institutional axis changed author. After two days in which the dispute over artificial intelligence in campaigning ran between the STF and a defence team, on Thursday a **party** became the petitioner: the Brazil of Hope Federation, which brings together PT, PCdoB and PV, filed at the TSE a petition of more than 200 pages against a network of profiles that, **according to the federation's claim**, uses AI to attack Lula. The petition's figures, all attributed to the federation, are **1,464,245 combined followers** and **23,828 posts**, and the reach of more than 10 million cited in the reporting is the total **added to the followings of the politicians named**, not the network's own. The profile count is 31 in most of the coverage and "at least 32" in one of them. Named as amplifiers are senator Flávio Bolsonaro, deputies Gustavo Gayer, Mario Frias and Gilvan Costa, pre-candidate André Marinho and Leonardo Rodrigues de Jesus, known as Léo Índio. The requests are for content removal, identification of those responsible and suspension of monetization ([Folha de S.Paulo](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/07/pt-entra-com-acao-contra-flavio-bolsonaro-por-amplificacao-de-posts-de-ia-com-ataques-a-lula.shtml), [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/07/30/pt-aciona-tse-contra-flavio-bolsonaro-mario-frias-e-gayer-por-rede-de-videos-com-ia-contra-lula.ghtml), [O Tempo](https://www.otempo.com.br/eleicoes/2026/presidentes/2026/7/30/pt-acusa-flavio-de-colaborar-com-rede-orquestrada-de-perfis-que-usam-ia-para-atacar-lula)). The rapporteur drawn was **Kassio Nunes Marques** ([Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/colunas/manoela-alcantara/kassio-vai-relatar-acao-do-pt-sobre-ia-para-desinformacao-contra-lula)), the same justice in whose chambers the Banco Master CPI writ has sat for more than four months.

And the previous day's framing needs correcting. On July 29 this synthesis recorded that TSE justices saw **abuse** in the AI video and wanted to curb use of the technology. Thursday's reporting shows something else: TSE justices see **interference by Moraes**, hold that the discussion belongs to their own court and not to the STF, and, according to the reporting, none of the internal groups intends to apply a severe sanction to Flávio ([Folha de S.Paulo](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/07/ministros-do-tse-veem-interferencia-de-moraes-em-cobranca-sobre-ia-de-bolsonaro.shtml), [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/eleicoes/tse-ve-interferencia-de-moraes-e-ordem-sobre-ia-de-bolsonaro-gera-mal-estar/)). It stopped being a case about AI and became friction between two courts.

In the Master case, Thursday brought the furthest-reaching development since the inquiry was opened. The **Federal Police is considering** asking Interpol to include Flávio Bolsonaro and Daniel Vorcaro in what is known as a **silver notice**, an instrument that locates assets and funds abroad and which is **not an arrest order**, unlike a red notice. The target is to trace the **R$ 61 million** from Banco Master said to have financed the film "Dark Horse", about Jair Bolsonaro's career. The verb here is decisive and this synthesis does not force it: the Federal Police is **considering**, it has not filed the request, and Interpol has decided nothing ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/07/30/pf-avalia-incluir-flavio-bolsonaro-em-lista-da-interpol-para-rastrear-dinheiro-do-filme-dark-horse-no-exterior.ghtml), [InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/politica/pf-avalia-incluir-flavio-em-lista-da-interpol-para-rastrear-dinheiro-de-dark-horse/), [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/flavio-lista-interpol-rastrear-recursos-dark-horse/)). ## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** the favourite's price rose 1.00pp and the day's polling, read inside the house itself, is flat. At PoderData the first-round gap is 6pp, exactly the same as on July 16. It is the **third time in seven days** that the market moves a full point on a day with no matching polling, and this synthesis records the pattern without asserting cause, because there is no clear triggering event to attribute it to.

> **Yesterday's convergence undid itself:** on July 29 this synthesis recorded the distance between **Renan Santos's** price and the polling falling to 0.90pp, based on AtlasIntel's 7.8%. PoderData gives **4%**, and the distance **reopens to 4.45pp**. Inside PoderData itself he falls from 6% to 4%. Convergence measured against a single survey is not a trend, and the market went the same way: it halved his chance of reaching the runoff and settled him into third.

> **Institutional pile-up × price:** it was the week's densest day in institutional developments, with a party filing at the TSE, the Federal Police weighing a request to Interpol and open friction between the STF and the TSE. And the only market touching that axis, impeachment of an STF justice, moved in the **opposite direction**, falling 0.25pp. In a book of USD 83,000 that is noise, not signal, and the honest reading is that the contract is not big enough to respond to the news.

## In summary

1. **The price rose and the polling did not move.** Lula returned to 63.50%, tied the series top for the third time without exceeding it, and the gap widened to +39.55pp by movement on one side only. PoderData keeps its own house gap at 6pp since July 16.
2. **Rejection tied.** Lula and Flávio both come in at 49% at PoderData, when AtlasIntel of July 29 gave 49.4% x 52.9%. One reading does not undo the series, but the asymmetry the panel had been recording did not appear in this one.
3. **The Master case reached the campaign.** The Federal Police is considering asking Interpol to trace abroad the R$ 61 million from Banco Master said to have financed a film about Jair Bolsonaro, with Flávio Bolsonaro among the names under consideration. The Federal Police is considering, it has not filed the request.
