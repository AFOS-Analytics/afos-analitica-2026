## 1. Prediction market

The [presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), whose cumulative total volume stands at **USD 113.8M**, pushed the gap to its widest point of the cycle. Flávio Bolsonaro retreated 0.85pp to **25.65% (USD 7.5M)**, his largest daily decline of the week and his third consecutive session of losses, while Lula remained flat at **60.50% (USD 7.5M)** for the second straight trading day. The distance between the two widened from +34.00pp to **+34.85pp**.

It is worth separating what this number says from what it does not. The gap widened because the challenger fell, not because the favorite rose: Lula's price did not move a hundredth of a point over two trading days. A gap that grows through loss of conviction in one side is different information from a gap that grows through gain in the other, and absent new national polling or a ballot event, Flávio's retreat is [market momentum](https://www.afos-analytics.com/pt-BR/how-it-works), not a reaction to a dated fact.

In the [third way](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan Santos gave up 0.15pp in the winner, to **8.15% (USD 8.2M)**, but delivered the largest isolated variation in the panel's sub-market: the 3rd place in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) fell 4.00pp, from 68.50% to **64.50% (USD 144 thousand)**. Ronaldo Caiado retreated to **1.35% (USD 5.0M)** and gave way to **16.00%** in the same sub-market. Michelle Bolsonaro remained stable at **1.85% (USD 9.1M)**, Romeu Zema rose to **0.65% (USD 4.4M)** and Fernando Haddad to **0.55% (USD 6.3M)**, returning part of the previous day's decline without anything occurring between the two endpoints-the typical behavior of residual names in a thin book.

In the [second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio yielded to **81.00% (USD 181 thousand)**, where he nonetheless remains comfortably ahead, with Renan in second at 12.40%. In the [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) remains comfortably at **85.50% (USD 252 thousand)**, ahead of [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) (12.80%). Note the permanent anomaly of the panel: Tarcísio de Freitas is worth **0.15%** in the presidential race with **USD 13.5M** in cumulative volume, the largest in the book-a reminder that volume measures negotiated history, not current conviction.

The [impeachment of STF minister](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) market rose 0.35pp, to **3.75% (USD 83 thousand)**, the largest relative gain in the panel, but remains within the noise range and without any new individual minister action that would explain the move. The methodological caveat is decisive here: with cumulative volume orders of magnitude below the USD 7.5M of the presidential race, a variation of this scale does not sustain narrative. In 2026 inflation, the modal range remains 5.00% to 5.49%, now at **33.70% (USD 8.6 thousand)**, versus 39.60% in the previous reading-greater dispersion around the Treasury's official projection (5.1%), above the Central Bank's target.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × narrative (Renan):** on the same trading session in which Ciro Gomes mentioned him by name as a voting possibility, Renan Santos **lost 4.00pp in 3rd place in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno)**, from 68.50% to **64.50%**, the largest isolated variation on the panel. Political gesture on one side, real money reducing the bet on his placement on the other. AFOS does not draw a causal link between the two events; there is no evidence that the market reacted to Ciro's gesture and the most likely scenario is that they are independent on the same day. The record matters because narrative and price pointed in opposite directions.

> **Market × narrative (Caiado):** Ciro's gesture toward Caiado was the most explicit political signal he has received in the cycle, and **his price did not move in either direction**, remaining at 1.35%. The honest reading is that the market does not treat a declaration of support as a priceable fact at 77 days from the election, not that the gesture was politically irrelevant.

> **Market × polling (Renan):** Renan trades at **8.15% in the winner market** against **6% in the best poll** he has received ([PoderData](/en/glossary#podedata) July 16) and **3% in [Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest)** (July 15, the most reliable one). He remains the largest relative distortion on the panel, and the two firms still disagree on who leads the third way.

> **Gap composition:** the Lula × Flávio gap reached **+34.85pp**, the widest of the cycle, but **grew entirely through the opponent's decline**: Lula did not move in two sessions. A gap that widens through loss of conviction on one side is not the same information as a gap that widens through gain on the other.

No poll or ballot event explains the movement.
