## AFOS Daily · Daily Briefing

September 19, 2026

## 1. Prediction market

The [Polymarket presidential contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) closed the reading confirmed at 19:20 BRT with Flávio Bolsonaro at 56.75% (USD 11.03M) and Lula at 40.50% (USD 11.41M). The spread went from 14.00pp to 16.25pp, and it is the largest lead ever recorded for Flávio over Lula since April 14, across 633 readings from the database backup. The opposite extreme of the same series is 41.80pp in Lula's favour, on August 1. Total accumulated volume in the presidential book stands at USD 151.75M.

Who moved changes the sentence: Flávio rose 0.25pp and Lula fell 2.00pp, and the widening of the spread belongs entirely to the side that retreated. And the [56.75%](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) is not the top of his series since April 14: the highest value ever recorded is 56.80%, and today's price sits 0.05pp below it.

The day's strong move is in the secondary books. In the [first-round runner-up contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Flávio rose 5.00pp, to 74.50% (USD 0.98M), and Lula fell 4.70pp, to 24.65% (USD 0.83M), leaving the top of his series in that book, recorded the previous day at 29.40%. That is twenty times what Flávio moved in the winner contract. One pays for winning the election, the other for finishing the first round in second place.

In the third way, the [third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pulled the top two apart: Renan Santos rose 2.00pp, to 51.00% (USD 349 thousand), Augusto Cury fell 4.65pp, to 36.35% (USD 230 thousand), the panel's largest fall in the round, and Ronaldo Caiado rose 1.00pp, to 11.50% (USD 138 thousand). The distance between Renan and Cury went from 8.00pp to 14.65pp in one day.

The [contract on a Supreme Court justice leaving](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) by impeachment before 2027 fell 0.05pp, to 4.55% (USD 92 thousand), the smallest move on the panel, and the Senate majority one received no new price. Against intuition, the largest accumulated volume in the presidential book does not belong to the leader: it belongs to Tarcísio de Freitas, USD 14.08M, trading today at 0.05%. Volume measures the contract's past, not conviction now.

## 2. What the polling firms registered

No national poll was released on September 18 or 19, and the panel's benchmark is still the September 17 pair, which disagrees with itself. [Datafolha](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), face to face, fieldwork September 15 to 16, 2,002 interviews and a 2.0pp margin, gives the runoff to Lula 46% to 44%. [Futura Inteligência, commissioned by 100% Cidades](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), by telephone, fieldwork September 11 to 15, 2,000 interviews and a 2.2pp margin, gives it to Flávio 48.1% to 43.7%. That is 6.4 points on the same pair, with the sign reversed. In the first round both put Lula ahead, by 3 points and by 0.6 point.

The [TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) register grew from 968 to 997 presidential polls, and two left the official archive, with AFOS's two independent counts agreeing. One of them was national, with publication set for September 23 and therefore not yet due: protocol BR-00548/2026, from Real Time Big Data, which the September 18 briefing listed in this same table with a scope caveat. Today's calendar goes out without it.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x polls:** the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 56.75% on Flávio Bolsonaro, while the two most recent national polls, both from September 17, put Lula ahead in the first round, and one of them in the runoff as well. The price moved on two consecutive days without any new poll entering, which is book momentum, not a reaction to information from the ballot.

> **Book x book:** the same candidate rose 0.25pp in the winner contract and 5.00pp in the first-round runner-up one. Whoever reads only the presidential headline sees an almost flat day; whoever reads both books sees the panel's largest move.

> **Book x ballot in the third way:** the [third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 51.00% on Renan Santos against 36.35% on Augusto Cury, and the two polls do the opposite, with Cury at 6% and 6.5% against Renan's 3% and 3.4%. The distance between them in the book nearly doubled in one day, while the declared order at the ballot did not change.
