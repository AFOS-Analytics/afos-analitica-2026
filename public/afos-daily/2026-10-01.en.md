## 1. Prediction market

In the [Polymarket winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), confirmed reading of October 1, 19:29 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro stands at 61.05% (USD 12.79M) and Lula at 37.50% (USD 13.00M). Against the confirmed reading of September 29, 11:58 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro rises 1.25pp and Lula falls 2.00pp, and the gap goes from 20.30pp to 23.55pp, the fifth widening in a row between confirmed readings. The pair sum fell from 99.30 to 98.55, and the normalized move is 1.73pp to each side. Total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 159.29M across 19 contracts.

In the points recorded on October 1, Flávio Bolsonaro's price ranged from 61.05% to 63.30%, and that 63.30%, at 04:00 BRT, is the top of the series recorded since April 14. Lula's ranged from 36.50% to 37.50%, 2.00pp from the floor of the series, 34.50% on April 25. The Renan Santos contract stands at 0.25% (USD 15.12M) and remains the one with the largest accumulated volume in the presidential book.

In the [second place book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio Bolsonaro falls 5.00pp, to 71.50% (USD 1.42M), and Lula rises 4.65pp, to 28.25% (USD 1.20M): the market pays less for the chance of Flávio Bolsonaro finishing the first round in second place. In the [third place one](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan Santos falls 3.00pp, to 48.50% (USD 0.52M), Augusto Cury rises 0.85pp, to 36.70% (USD 0.34M), and the distance between the two narrows from 15.65pp to 11.80pp. Ronaldo Caiado rises 2.50pp, to 16.00%.

The [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) falls 0.30pp, to 2.60% (USD 0.09M). In the [2026 inflation one](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the 5.00% to 5.49% band leads with 49.65% (USD 0.02M), in the 12:07 BRT reading.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x poll:** the two national polls of the day put Lula 4 points ahead in the first round, and in the second Datafolha puts him 3 points ahead and Real Time 1 point behind. In the confirmed reading of 19:29 BRT the gap in the winner contract stood at 23.55pp in favour of Flávio Bolsonaro.

> **Firm x firm in the second round:** with fieldwork overlapping from September 28 to 30, Datafolha puts Lula ahead 48% to 45% and Real Time puts Flávio Bolsonaro ahead 46% to 45%, both within the margin of 2 points.

> **Third place:** the book puts Renan Santos 11.80pp ahead of Augusto Cury, and in the two national polls of the day the third-placed candidate is Cury in Datafolha, at 4%, and Renan Santos in Real Time, at 4%.
