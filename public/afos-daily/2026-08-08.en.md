## 1. Prediction market

This edition publishes the reading of **Aug 8, 14:32 BRT**, confirmed by two readings. The [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) has **Lula at 64.50% (USD 8.12M)** against **Flávio at 26.95% (USD 8.08M)**, a gap of **+37.55pp**. Total accumulated volume is **USD 121.06M**.

**The six-day run of narrowing in the gap did not continue.** In the [AFOS series](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) it came from **+41.80pp on Aug 1** and fell every day through Aug 7. Today it held where it was, with Lula flat for the **fifth day running**. **Neither gave back ground**, and that is information too: the runner-up's new level held without a poll to confirm it.

**The move of the day is in the placement books, and it contradicts yesterday's reading.** In the [first round runner-up book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio **fell for the second day running**, from 87.50% on Aug 6 to 83.00% on Aug 7 and **81.50% (USD 237 thousand)** today, adding up to 6.00pp in two days. Yesterday it coexisted with a rise in the winner contract and could be called a swap between books. Today the winner held still, so the fall is isolated.

In [third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), **Caiado fell 2.50pp, to 31.00% (USD 42 thousand)**, and **Renan Santos did not rise alongside: he held at 58.50% (USD 176 thousand)**.

Behind them, **Renan Santos at 7.65% (USD 9.23M)**, still with more money traded than Lula, **Caiado at 1.35% (USD 5.59M)** and **Zema at 0.55% (USD 5.01M)**. [STF impeachment](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) held at **3.10% (USD 83 thousand)** and the **PL is at 73.50% (USD 259 thousand)** in the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won).
