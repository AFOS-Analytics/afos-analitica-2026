## 1. Prediction market

[Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) made a partial reversal on Tuesday of yesterday's jump. After the presidential gap hit the cycle record on June 22, the market gave back part of the move: **Lula fell to 53.50%** (down 2.00pp, USD 6.7M accumulated volume) and **Flávio Bolsonaro recovered to 25.55%** (up 1.80pp, USD 6.9M), so the distance between the two closed from +31.75pp to **+27.95pp**. The total volume traded in the [presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) totals around USD 104.1M accumulated, reinforcing that this is real money, not polling data.

**Renan Santos** fell slightly to 13.35% (down 0.35pp) and maintains the highest accumulated volume in the presidential market, USD 7.4M. The third way remained at the floor and practically stable: **Ronaldo Caiado** rose to 1.65% (up 0.10pp, USD 4.3M), **Fernando Haddad** fell to 1.55% (down 0.20pp, USD 5.9M) and **Romeu Zema** remained stable at 1.15% (USD 3.8M), in the range of Camilo Santana (1.65%).

In sub-markets, the structure did not change: Flávio remains isolated as the favorite for 2nd place in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) (71.5%), Renan cemented in 3rd place (60.5%) and [PL](/en/glossary#pl) leading the Senate by number of seats (76.5%). The market for impeachment of [STF](/en/glossary#stf) minister rose to 4.30% (up 0.55pp), in low volume and at a level that continues to price in very low institutional odds. In the 2026 inflation market, the band of 5.00% to 5.49% leads with 32.4%, followed by the range of 5.50% to 5.99% (23.5%) and 6.00% to 6.49% (19.8%), a distribution pointing to persistent macro uncertainty.

The technical reading for the day is a correction of an overextended gap, not a trend reversal: the market continues to cement Lula's favoritism well above the runoff measured at the polls.

## 2. What polling institutes recorded

The highlight was the national **Indexa** survey, published on June 23, which widened Lula's advantage. In the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), [Lula has 42% against 31% of Flávio](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirgFBVV95cUxQUEUyRnoxUXllOWRwZldSeG5KR3VHMU9zQTRjUHJoZm5RRHd1Q3FpVUVYbEc5Q3E1NWVIdHZOTnhSMkhXWEowaTlFeVRJNEZmbjEwbU1KSi1Rd3BSaWRHY25DcC13ckQ2aTAyYmxFc0tGeDhxRzlEVy1oamZndWJ2dDdyWTZoUkltQ1VrQTFjNlRRVnF2Z0lrRjBwdWdNM01tN2p6N3J5SGdZNEtCTUE?oc=5) (gap of +11pp), with Lula at 48.8% of valid votes. The field was June 18 to 20, with 2,000 telephone interviews and a margin of 2.2pp. The institute itself summarizes that [Lula improves, but rejection grows](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiogFBVV95cUxQeGFJcENvRGRLbVU4ZFZzZGU1aVh2bjRKZllhTVFUYW5PYVZzVWt6Unc5azJ1Sk5ENnB4UjhWenJfR1BwWmZrY1pyR0ZXSHlrN09YVXExNzc3c1RvOVZ0NmRfeE9HcTU0aTg1aXFEbHU2YmtEdzNBZklfMTJZa3pTZ2hNVnRlV29xUE1xaWhfTkJQbHo4UGVCaUdWTmlDODhzSXc?oc=5): Lula and Flávio are rejected by 49% each, in a polarization scenario that consolidates through loyal voting.

Indexa reinforces the direction of recent nationals. [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) on June 20 (n=2.004, BR-09956/2026) gave [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) 41% to 31% and runoff 47% to 43%; CNT/MDA on June 16 gave runoff 49.3% to 36.8%, the largest gap in the sample; and [Nexus/BTG Pactual](/en/glossary#nexus-btg) on June 15 gave 49% to 43%, with government approval at 48% against 47%. Five consecutive national polls with Lula ahead.

On the state level, the contrarian reading came from Rio Grande do Sul: Real Time Big Data gave [Flávio with 51% against 42% of Lula in the runoff](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMingFBVV95cUxONjFjS0dIdU9TdEVvZFhfbG1mQnJWZnJaUjEyY2hhT09ueTB4WTNtS01SdkJINlh2QXozbXF5N3JZRW8wdlJvdnBUTHJ4ZXJwRWwtaVBVM2p1bkVxRzkyOWQzVHIwd2RvZGY0SzJtRGI3Unk5OFl1R2RnVTB3US10Vm8xcEJXQk5ySVd0Ti1yOWIxcl9iSFEySE1sSHZ0Zw?oc=5) and [42% to 39% in the first round](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMizwFBVV95cUxQb2FlQmhaRWY3cU1nR1ZvOG9oVHRYWmZWekVtQ3JUNHc0LUExTXdsbXdmSXQxZnVKVGxSdm9kRHNhMnh3M3F2RWRERzN2U256d3dzTDBCeWs5Z1BiQ1l5SDV4VFBwbF9WOGpkaVFJT2VGelkwazJjeUJnak9YNVlRZS1PVC1sbHo2emFkcDlUWFFHcGV4SG85R093cnlsTWxuMnF0MXlGR25zanV5enVDeExyRmRzeXRYTHdXLTBGbXgydW1DUEdVYlE5SkFpa0k?oc=5), with a field from June 20 to 22 and 1,600 interviews. The state signal sustains Flávio's recovery in the book, but does not translate into the national aggregate.
