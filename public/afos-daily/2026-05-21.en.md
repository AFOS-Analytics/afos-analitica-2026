## 1. Prediction market

The [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) markets of the Brazil 2026 cycle total **USD 87.90M accumulated since opening** (snapshot May 21 19:00 BRT), distributed among presidential USD 83.66M, 2nd place USD 3.55M, 3rd place USD 301k, STF impeachment USD 76k, Senate USD 254k and 2026 inflation bands USD 60k. The presidential market concentrates more than 95% of total volume — where the signal is most robust.

In the [presidential Polymarket market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Lula closed the day at **45.50% (USD 5.70M)**, stable for more than 60 hours since [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) on May 19, while Flávio Bolsonaro closed at **24.75% (USD 5.88M)** — partial recovery vs the absolute minimum of 23.35% registered in the morning (↑1.40pp in 8h). The Lula × Flávio gap closed at **+20.75pp** (↓1.45pp vs the morning, because Flávio recovered).

The third way reorganized asymmetrically and reversed the morning spike. **Renan Santos closed at 13.05% (USD 5.57M)** — corrected downward the peak of 15.60% registered in the morning, still consolidating the SECOND position in the presidential Poly above Zema (**5.35%, USD 2.72M**) and Haddad (**5.05%, USD 4.84M**). **Michelle Bolsonaro at 3.55% (USD 6.47M)** — the second largest position in volume among third-way candidates, signaling that the narrative of substitution on the [PL](/en/glossary#pl) slate continues to attract hedge capital. [In the 3rd place market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan remained at the top with **34.00%**, Zema at 24.50%, Caiado at 15.50%, and **Flávio at 8.15%** — the market continues pricing Flávio with material risk of finishing in third.

[In the 2nd place market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio maintained **55.00%** at the top of the runoff. Renan rose to **14.50% (USD 1.0M)** — only candidate with volume above USD 1M in 2nd place, conferring real weight to the signal that the market sees Renan as an ascending hypothesis for 2nd place. Lula at 12.50%, Haddad at 4.10%, Michelle at 3.35%, Zema at 3.25%.

The most relevant movement of the day came after close: the [contract for impeachment of STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) **fell to 4.35% (USD 75k accumulated)** — decline of ↓3.20pp in 90 minutes against 7.55% at 17:30 close. Movement in sub-market of low aggregated volume — monitor over the next 24h before consolidating reading on the signal.

[In the Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won) — sub-market with very reduced volumes —, [PL](/en/glossary#pl) closed at **75.00% (USD 243k)** confirming first decline of the cycle. [PSB](/en/glossary#psb) at 8.05%, [PSD](/en/glossary#psd) at 6.30%, [União Brasil](/en/glossary#uniao-brasil) at 4.90%, [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) at 4.80%. [Republicanos](/en/glossary#republicanos) partially recovered to **4.35%** after this week's collapse. [In the 2026 inflation bands](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the central band 4.50-4.99% rose to **30.20%** (↑2.85pp in 8h signaling priced fiscal moderation); band 5.00-5.49% at 23.70%; high tail (≥6.50%) fell to 7.75%.

## 4. Day's divergences

> **Market × research:** [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) continues more aggressive than any published national poll. Gap of +22.15pp Lula × Flávio in the market vs +12.7pp in [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) (May 19), +8.7pp in Vox (May 20) and +3pp in [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) (May 16). The market × average-of-polls divergence is approximately 14pp — the market prices not only what polls have already captured but also the cumulative effect of subsequent events (PSDB-Aécio, Caiado/Zema positioning, Flávio changing strategist) that no poll in the field the previous week could measure.

> **Research × research:** [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) (47% × 34.3% gap +12.7pp) vs Vox [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) (46.8% × 38.1% gap +8.7pp) vs [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) (38% × 35% gap +3pp). The [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel)-Vox convergence in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) is significant (gap between the two of only 1.6pp in Lula's advantage over Flávio). The larger discrepancy remains between [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel)/Vox and [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) — explainable by the time window ([Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) in the field before Vorcaro repercussion reached the sample) and methodology ([AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) included mention of the audio in the questionnaire, confirmed by the company itself on May 19).

> **Market × institutional field:** [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) priced Flávio's continued collapse in 32 hours (drop of ↓4.80pp) without any single explosive institutional event. The movement appears to reflect accumulation: PSDB-Aécio + Caiado/Zema positioning + Michelle/Salles + Flávio changing strategist + [PF](/en/glossary#pf) investigating amendment. The market integrates cumulative signals that would not make headlines individually.

## In summary

1. **Vox Brasil confirmed Flávio's deterioration captured by [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel)**, with convergence between the two institutes around +7-9pp Lula in the runoff. The residual market × research divergence of ~14pp points to pricing of post-poll events that [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) on May 22 may validate or refute.

2. **The non-Bolsonaro right entered a phase of explicit movement**: PSDB discusses Aécio, Caiado delivers speech about being "contaminated by Vorcaro", Zema speaks of "credibility", Michelle criticizes "alliance with evil". [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) priced first material traction of the replacement narrative (Michelle 2L at 3.25%, ↑1.70pp) and Renan consolidating second place in the presidential race.

3. **[PL](/en/glossary#pl) yielded for the first time in the cycle**: Senate at 76.00% (↓2.50pp), 2L Flávio at 56.00% (↓6.50pp, [PL](/en/glossary#pl) base yields), [Republicanos](/en/glossary#republicanos) collapses to 2.50%. The technical reading: the market began to reprice [PL](/en/glossary#pl) hegemony in the Senate in parallel with Flávio's individual collapse — sign that the crisis may have consequences beyond the presidential ticket.—
