## 1. Prediction market

The [presidential election winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) widened the spread for the third day, and this time both sides moved. In the confirmed reading of September 18, at 18:59 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro stands at 56.50% (USD 11.02M), 1.80pp above the 54.70% of September 17, and Lula fell 2.00pp, to 42.50% (USD 11.36M). The distance went from 10.20pp to 14.00pp, with total presidential volume at USD 151.46M accumulated.

Flávio's 56.50% stands above the highest value in the recorded readings of [his series](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), 56.40% earlier the same day, in a series that begins on April 14. Lula stands 25.00pp below the top of his series, 67.50% on August 16. Renan Santos gave up 0.20pp, to 0.95%, below the lowest value ever recorded.

The largest move of the day is not in the winner contract. In the [first-round runner-up contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) Flávio fell 7.00pp, to 69.50% (USD 953 thousand), and Lula rose 6.70pp, to 29.35% (USD 807 thousand). Whoever becomes more likely to win becomes less likely to finish second, and that book moved almost four times more than the winner one.

In the [third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan Santos rose 5.00pp, to 49.00% (USD 347 thousand), and opened 8.00pp over Augusto Cury, who fell 1.90pp, to 41.00% (USD 226 thousand). Ronaldo Caiado gave up 2.00pp, to 10.50% (USD 137 thousand). The [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 4.60% (USD 92 thousand), 0.45pp below the previous day, and in the [Senate contract](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won) the Liberal Party stands at 83.50% (USD 261 thousand).

## 3. What the press covered

[BBC News Brasil](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiY0FVX3lxTE53UWJUWUFHUlJ5UkcwQURPX19DQ1dKeldIWVpjaXp0bktWdldwUWdkYTBQYXJBVTdXalo0bk1MVldEUU9nR0M5UWU5Uzd1Q1BDa3ppcnM2Z2JQVVVRa0dYcFRtWdIBaEFVX3lxTE9kM3lzcTkzVThQOXZrU3FScXJsbFctc2MtMXN1b05vSjM3bzZtcWxtSGgzdGt4OXVkTXZuU0tlM3VlX0s4QU14WXE5ZmItcVdDWUF6bXRhSnFCMnY2UkVpTUlXd1lOU3dt?oc=5) recorded the announcement the same day.

The measure went to the courts in two moves. On September 17, at the Superior Electoral Court, André Mendonça [rejected a federal deputy's filing against the increase](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/17/medonca-extingue-processo-que-questiona-reajuste-do-bolsa-familia-sem-analisar-a-legalidade-da-medida.ghtml) for lack of standing, and wrote in the decision that this is no pronouncement on the legality or constitutionality of the measure. That same night, [Renan Santos filed his own petition](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/17/renan-santos-aciona-tse-contra-reajuste-do-bolsa-familia.ghtml), and [Mendonça was drawn as rapporteur again](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/eleicoes/mendonca-vai-relatar-acao-de-renan-santos-contra-reajuste-do-bolsa-familia-feito-por-lula/), according to Estadão. On September 18, the Solicitor General's Office [asked the Supreme Court to recognise the decree as constitutional](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/justica/agu-pede-que-stf-reconheca-constitucionalidade-do-reajuste-do-bolsa-familia/), according to CartaCapital and [Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/brasil/agu-pede-que-stf-reconheca-validade-de-reajuste-do-bolsa-familia). Flávio Bolsonaro [disavowed his ally's filing](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/18/flavio-bolsonaro-desautoriza-ze-trovao-por-acao-que-questionou-reajuste-de-lula-no-bolsa-familia-nao-pedi-isso.ghtml) and [promised to keep the increase](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiugFBVV95cUxNTV9KRGttVTFrYXZBNlc1elh1TWJxNmRXWXRMWldTWGVVQ0NLLUFlTmRHY0FxSWswV2pmRVBJYm93SENjTTZqMGZBU1F0NllfQkNXd0xhVERHZEtRLUlQZUxGNkcza1NtSDU3UW50d19IM0l6d1VBd1h2QkVtT0hEbktHdlNlR3ZUVTBvTHBid2hSaUgyTG5FWld0RmJPb0ZNeUpNc3dmLUxwREhzSEZrYzFhYVFTb0dsTHc?oc=5) if elected, according to UOL.

In the Banco Master case, the defence of Daniel Vorcaro, [detained since November 17, 2025, asked for his pretrial detention to be revoked](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/18/defesa-de-vorcaro-pede-revogacao-de-prisao-e-cita-indefinicao-sobre-caso-master-e-crise-no-stf.ghtml) and cited the unresolved state of the case at the Supreme Court as an argument, according to O Globo. The defence [stated it will appeal to the full bench](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/defesa-vorcaro-recorrera-plenario-stf-se-fachin-mantiver-prisao/) if Edson Fachin keeps the detention, according to Gazeta do Povo. Gilmar Mendes [acknowledged having had a meeting with Vorcaro](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/politica/gilmar-admite-encontro-com-vorcaro-e-destaca-voto-contrario-ao-master-em-causa-de-ate-r-145-bilhoes/) and said he voted against Banco Master in a case worth up to R$ 145 billion.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Poll x poll:** the two national polls of September 17 disagree on the sign of the runoff, not only on the magnitude. [Datafolha](https://g1.globo.com/politica/eleicoes/2026/pesquisa-eleitoral/noticia/2026/09/17/datafolha-presidente-17-setembro.ghtml), in person, gives Lula 46% to 44%, inside the margins. [Futura/100% Cidades](https://exame.com/brasil/pesquisa-futura-flavio-bolsonaro-cresce-e-empata-tecnicamente-com-lula-no-1o-turno/), by phone, gives Flávio 48.1% to 43.7%, outside two margins. That is 6.4 points on the same pair. In the first round the two agree, and the panel does not choose between them.

> **Market x poll:** in the [third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) Renan Santos opened 8.00pp over Augusto Cury, and both national polls put Cury ahead, 6% to 3% and 6.5% to 3.4%. In the winner contract the same Renan fell to 0.95%. Finishing third and winning are different questions, and the book answers them in opposite directions.

> **Procedure x price:** the increase drew three judicial moves in two days and the [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) fell 0.45pp, in a book of USD 92 thousand. The order between the rulings and the price is not measured, and the panel does not attribute one to the other.

## In summary

**The [spread](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) widened with both sides moving.** Flávio rose 1.80pp, to 56.50%, above the highest value ever recorded in his series, and Lula fell 2.00pp, to 42.50%. The distance reached 14.00pp.

**The [runner-up book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) moved almost four times more.** Flávio fell 7.00pp there and Lula rose 6.70pp, the mirror of the main move, measured in a contract whose price has more room to travel.

**The two national polls from the previous day invert each other.** Datafolha and Futura/100% Cidades give the runoff to opposite sides, and the [judicial dispute over the increase](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/justica/agu-pede-que-stf-reconheca-constitucionalidade-do-reajuste-do-bolsa-familia/) reached the Supreme Court at the government's own hand.
