## 1. Prediction market

In the [Polymarket winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), confirmed reading of September 28, 13:49 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro rises 0.80pp, to 58.05% (USD 12.14M), and Lula falls 1.00pp, to 41.50% (USD 12.52M), against the reading of September 27, 12:47 BRT. The gap goes from 14.75pp to 16.55pp, the third widening in a row between confirmed readings. The pair sum fell from 99.75 to 99.55, and the normalized move is 0.92pp to each side. Total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 156.21M across 19 contracts.

Neither of them is at an extreme of the series recorded since April 14: Flávio Bolsonaro stands 3.65pp from the top, of September 21, and Lula 7.00pp from the floor, of April 25. The Renan Santos contract falls to 0.35% (USD 14.61M) and remains the one with the largest accumulated volume in the presidential book.

In the [third place book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Augusto Cury rises 0.70pp, to 45.10% (USD 0.29M), and Renan Santos rises 1.50pp, to 43.00% (USD 0.45M), so the distance between the two narrows from 2.90pp to 2.10pp. Ronaldo Caiado falls 1.00pp, to 13.50% (USD 0.18M). In the [second place one](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Lula falls 0.90pp, to 24.55% (USD 1.07M), and Flávio Bolsonaro stays at 74.50% (USD 1.25M).

The [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stays at 2.95% (USD 0.09M), practically unchanged. In the [2026 inflation one](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the 5.00% to 5.49% band leads with 48.85% (USD 0.02M), in the 14:12 BRT reading.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x poll:** Quaest and BTG/Nexus, released before the confirmed reading, measure Lula better than in the same firm's September 21 round, with shifts within the 2-point margin. Between the confirmed readings of September 27 and 28, the gap moved 1.80pp in favour of Flávio Bolsonaro.

> **First round x second round:** in Quaest, Lula opens 5 points in the first round and ties in the second round. The difference lies in the transfer measured by the poll itself, in which 81% of Zema's voters and 48% of Renan Santos's would go to Flávio Bolsonaro, against 9% and 23% to Lula.

> **Third place:** the book puts Ronaldo Caiado at 13.50%, well behind Augusto Cury and Renan Santos, and the two national polls of the day tie Caiado with Cury in third, with Renan Santos 1 point behind.

## In summary

**The price and the polls of the day moved in opposite directions.** Both national polls measured Lula better, and the winner contract widened the gap for the third reading in a row, to 16.55pp.

**The last week before the first round begins with 15 national polls registered.** Seven are scheduled for September 29, and Datafolha registered October 1, the day of the TV Globo debate.

**The dispute over the posts about Our Lady reached the full Supreme Court.** The full court review requested by Dino decides whether the overturning of Mendonça's order stands.
