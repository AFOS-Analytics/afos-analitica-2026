## 1. Prediction market

The presidential market on [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) had a day of stability at the top and movement in the tail. Lula maintained the cycle peak at 51.50% (USD 6.50M accumulated volume), while Flávio Bolsonaro declined slightly to 25.35% (USD 6.70M, down 0.20pp). The result is the widest gap yet measured between the two: +26.15pp, compared to +25.95pp the previous day. Total accumulated volume in the presidential market totals approximately USD 100.63M, combining all active candidacies.

The day's movement came from Renan Santos, who rose again to 14.05% (USD 7.10M, up 2.40pp) in the [winner's market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), reversing the previous day's decline, with the highest accumulated volume among all presidential names. Among others, Caiado rose to 1.95% (USD 4.10M, up 0.45pp), tied with Haddad (1.95%, USD 5.80M); Camilo Santana stood at 1.90% and Zema at 1.35% (USD 3.70M, up 0.10pp).

Tarcísio de Freitas remained at 0.15% in the presidential market, despite the largest market book by volume (USD 12.70M accumulated), a legacy anomaly from old bets, since the governor focuses on reelection in São Paulo. In position sub-markets, Flávio led the [2nd place in the 1st round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) with 70% (USD 0.10M volume, low) and Renan the [3rd place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) with 47.5%, ahead of Caiado (14.5%) and Zema (14%).

Outside the presidential, the [STF minister impeachment](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) market declined to 3.25%, in a low-volume book, with no institutional risk repricing. The [Senate by number of seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won) market kept [PL](/en/glossary#pl) in the lead with 73% (USD 0.20M volume), and the [2026 annual inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) market remained tight, with the 5.00% to 5.49% range leading around 24%, followed by the 6.00% to 6.49% range (around 21%).

## 3. What the press covered

Coverage of the day revolved around the two polls. [CNN Brasil](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMikgFBVV95cUxQWnFCd1NyM1BUd2R0MFRVUl9sQ1d3UGRNVFdsdVYxd3RGaGxDNGplUF9tVmVEeFE2ZlljUTY0OXNQTUZ4RktyRk1RcjhmV194czBQZ3Q0QkRYMFIwd2FLOHJ5MXVUT2FSVmsxLXI5R3k0SlNwN3czSGdlQnk2SE5QMVlhcDNHVWFoTHpVMzJLSEpIUQ?oc=5) detailed the figures from Futura/Apex and [Money Times](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMifEFVX3lxTFBVQUExSzJDS0paN28yUlRGR0dUaUZpU2VzcXFlUDY5UWluZkc2OU4ySE1hd1FIN1BVdmdkcG0yXzBPTDA5WGVhNW1UMXFEOFVDeEVDMDdUOUl1V0FJaGhvbDVuMVl4d0dRdXBxckNHa2l3MXFwZk5rZG4xTnk?oc=5) framed CNT/MDA as Lula defeating all opponents in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno). The common thread between the two is the reading of Flávio as more distant: CNT/MDA registers the largest [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) disadvantage in the sample, and Futura/Apex shows the hypothesis, still theoretical, that the right could have more momentum without Flávio in the race.

On the institutional front, the [PGR rejected Daniel Vorcaro's second plea bargain proposal](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqwFBVV95cUxNSkVlME9sODM3WFFXN2ltQlpkNHhaRUNwUWRBZVNxR2pVN3ZHOU16VkFMZnFJV3R2NnpNaXNUZXcwUFI1cmw2cEt5dVl6QlY0cktpMWJ6d3hQaDhWTkZfQWd3V3ZzSXFsZWw1NUt1OFA5OWxISHdpa3lOVHZVdGlNUVBNbDU4Tk11YldMNmZLclo5LXFzSGVpZTJjOXVGRjlxRGRjU2tWZGQ5UE0?oc=5), in a decision communicated to the [STF](/en/glossary#stf) on June 15, following the position that the Federal Police had already adopted the previous week. It will be up to André Mendonça, rapporteur in the Master case at the Supreme Court since February 2026, to decide Vorcaro's detention status, held since November 2025.

The institutional agenda of the day, therefore, was a development and not a watershed moment: the rejection of the plea bargain closes one more avenue of negotiation in the case, but the market for impeachment of an [STF](/en/glossary#stf) minister retreated, a sign that the reading of institutional risk did not rise.

## 4. Divergencies of the day

> **Market × survey (Renan Santos):** the [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) prices Renan at 14.05% to win the election, versus 2% voting intention in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) measured by [CNT/MDA](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMifEFVX3lxTFBVQUExSzJDS0paN28yUlRGR0dUaUZpU2VzcXFlUDY5UWluZkc2OU4ySE1hd1FIN1BVdmdkcG0yXzBPTDA5WGVhNW1UMXFEOFVDeEVDMDdUOUl1V0FJaGhvbDVuMVl4d0dRdXBxckNHa2l3MXFwZk5rZG4xTnk?oc=5). The gap of roughly 12pp is the widest on the panel, and reopened today after having narrowed the day before—a speculative back-and-forth over 48 hours with no counterpart at the polls.

> **Probability × intention (Lula):** Lula's 51.50% on the [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) is probability of victory, not voting intention. [CNT/MDA](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirgFBVV95cUxORjh2TENoVTRRM0lxRjdvaXNCVndMSzVKbksxNmJxd00ydFEtSm1rRjllckNJbFdYU1hUdE5aRzRfbmo0OUZEQkhxTTRNRDhsU3pDZTNpVDBWNFNWcUhxcHAyUU1Gd09sa2puSzdHZ0VuVUk5M21SV01tOFhsMDcxU0w3QkxIaGdHZzJHZFRUdXM4aVg0YTA0WXIyV1ltRWZqTkhZSmY3V0UyVlFRZ0HSAbMBQVVfeXFMT2lFSlVDX3BTRldYZXAzY3BaZmpfVVNHU21vQmoxMGpSSHdGUTlIQWs5ZUgybmxkQjBFSFlQRmxKamJSOUJyYmd2MlNiUUp0X0JJNGZZa3Q0YnpxYl9TWU1iUHdGbHNnYjN5U2lFVW5QLUhXYUVYb01nMFhFMGFzVW5VTEpsVVZpaHNfX2tOTDMwZkNoMHE0aVQ4RjBmN3VSQm1YUGowdWxxOER1RGNYSWlzWHM?oc=5) measures 41.8% of intention in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) and 49.3% in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno). The market prices a conviction of favoritism consistent with the direction of the surveys, albeit on a different scale.

> **Market gap × survey gap:** the distance between Lula and Flávio on [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) reached +26.15pp, while the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) gap from CNT/MDA is +12.5pp. This is the first time that the market's favoritism premium and the gap from a [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) survey have been so close in order of magnitude, after weeks in which the market opened much wider than the polls.

## In summary

1. Two new national surveys, CNT/MDA and Futura/Apex, confirmed Lula widening the advantage in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) and in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno), and the market kept Lula at the peak (51.50%) with the widest gap yet measured (+26.15pp).
2. Renan Santos rose again (14.05%, up 2.40pp) and reopened the largest divergence on the panel, a speculative movement that unwound and reformed over 48 hours with no backing in the surveys.
3. On the institutional front, the [PGR](/en/glossary#pgr) rejected Daniel Vorcaro's second plea bargain proposal on June 15, and it will fall to the case's rapporteur to decide the ex-banker's detention status; the impeachment market retreated, with no elevated risk priced in.
