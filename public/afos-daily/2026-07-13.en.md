## 1. Prediction market

83 days from the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), the top of [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) has not moved. **Lula** closed the fourth consecutive day at **60.50%** (USD 7.3M accumulated volume) and **Flávio Bolsonaro** rose to **23.85%** (↑0.60pp, USD 7.4M), with the presidential gap narrowing to **+36.65pp** (↓0.60pp). Total accumulated volume in the presidential market stands at **USD 112.5M**. The contrast is the data point: the day brought the most adverse polling of the cycle for Lula, and his winner contract did not move a basis point.

The day's movement happened in the tail, and it is about succession, not the main race. In [Presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), **Michelle Bolsonaro doubled**, from 1.05% to **2.25%** (↑1.20pp, USD 8.9M), the largest relative variation on the panel. In the opposite direction, **Jair Bolsonaro fell** from 1.65% to **1.05%** (↓0.60pp, USD 5.0M). Both movements occurred the same day the [STF](/en/glossary#stf) cut contact between father and son (Section 3). There is no way to establish causality from price, and the Daily does not claim it: we register the temporal coincidence and the fact that the market repriced the internal dispute within the Bolsonaro camp without touching the top.

**Flávio posted the largest single-day jump** in the [Second place in first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) sub-market, to **83.50%** (↑5.0pp, USD 175 thousand): the condition of certain challenger to Lula is more priced in than ever, on a day when he accumulated institutional wear.

In the third way, **Renan Santos** remained practically flat in [Winner](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), at **10.05%** (↓0.20pp, USD 8.1M, the largest individual volume among living contenders), and **expanded** favoritism in [Third place in first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) to **70.50%** (↑4.0pp, USD 138 thousand). **Ronaldo Caiado** retreated in the winner market to **1.45%** (↓0.15pp, USD 4.9M), but rose in the same sub-market to 16.50% (USD 33 thousand). Zema remains at the floor (0.85%, USD 4.3M) and Haddad at 0.75% (USD 6.2M).

In the institutional sub-markets, nothing. [Impeachment of STF minister before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) remains pinned to the floor at **2.75%** (USD 82 thousand), not reacting to a day of single-judge decisions and advances in the investigation into amendments. In [Senate by number of seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) maintains 87.50% (USD 251 thousand), against 12.00% for [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) (USD 6 thousand, volume too low for firm reading). In [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the 5.00% to 5.49% band leads with 33.60% (USD 8 thousand).

## 2. What the institutes recorded

**[Nexus/BTG Pactual](/en/glossary#nexus-btg)** published on July 13 the first of four national polls of the week, and it is the most relevant data point of the cycle so far. Sample of 2,003 voters, fieldwork from July 10 to 12, margin of 2 points, telephone interviews in all regions, protocol [BR-07981/2026](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/). In the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), **Lula 40%** and **Flávio 34%**, with Caiado at 5%, Renan Santos at 4% and Zema at 4%. In the [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno), **Lula 47% × Flávio 44%**: three points difference, **within the margin**, in other words, **[Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico)** ([Institute website](https://www.nexus.fsb.com.br/estudos-divulgados/pesquisa-btg-nexus-de-intencao-de-votos-para-presidente-do-brasil-13-de-julho-de-2026/), [Folha de S.Paulo](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMixwFBVV95cUxOdVVhZm8xOTBYQU1xS2RobTNHZ2RjYl9xcWd2N21BSG1xNEYtOVVWaVRMUlJjOXRhSm83bFNCNmdEN3RoYVZBaUFyczRDRUdxdUllY1k4T2swRF9JRGl4VzQwcDlPeXpfN1JfSmhTblVSajNqU01nNFluTmdtVm9SdnR4WmdLQkkxYTc4aFBJdFZIeVZXaVRuTHJUeERTOTVwYVRkSUFIS1A1X2NZZ21HdmZzUjAtZERfMGpyckJrX0w5VmFGVVdr?oc=5), [Poder360](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMipAFBVV95cUxPZnBXYl8zbExZN0tURExPT3QzN1NLU0JLT3BoZm9Jd3JtdkhFSl90WW91dEQ0aUxsNWZzSGtzdjU5bUxzcVFybWdaSUZpQmw5QUtCWkJXa3JweklWRktFeldMRm9yU2pfbkRoMk14N2JFTTI5OTBGbkR4dnJxbWpMYXlSUmtfNUFIQXJDYURwSWFzMUhOdzR3U2Y2bWpQeDZWZDZBeg?oc=5)).

Two numbers from [BTG/Nexus](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) deserve attention beyond the topline. Lula **fell 2pp** in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) against his own round from June 29, when he was at 42%, while Flávio remained stable at 34%. And **decided voting fell from 74% to 70%**: there is more electorate available to switch sides than there was three weeks ago.

The weight of this poll is not in the number, it is in the source. On July 12, when incorporating [Gerp/AESP from 08/Jul](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) ([Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), Flávio winning the [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno)), the mandatory caveat was that it was a smaller institute and known outlier. [Nexus/BTG Pactual](/en/glossary#nexus-btg) is first-tier and points in the same direction. **Gerp is no longer isolated.** The range of the [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) today goes from 3pp loss (Gerp) to 6.5pp victory ([AtlasIntel from 01/Jul](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/)), passing through [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) ([Nexus/BTG Pactual](/en/glossary#nexus-btg)) and 5pp victory (Meio/Ideia of Jul/08). It is the largest dispersion among institutes recorded in this cycle, and it is information, not noise.

## 4. Day's divergences

> **Market × survey:** the [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) gives Lula **60.50%** implicit probability. The most recent survey, published the same day by a top-tier institute, places him in [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) in the [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) (47% × 44%, within the 2pp margin) and still sees him drop 2pp in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno). Probability of victory and vote margin measure different things, and the comparison is directional and one of conviction, not point-by-point. But the level gap is the sharpest of the cycle, and it did not narrow with the new data: Lula's contract did not move.

> **Survey × survey:** the [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) today admits four readings from four institutes [registered with TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/): Lula's defeat by 3pp (Gerp 08/Jul), [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) ([Nexus/BTG Pactual](/en/glossary#nexus-btg) 13/Jul), victory by 5pp (Meio/Ideia 08/Jul) and victory by 6.5pp ([AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) 01/Jul). An average would produce a number that no institute measured. AFOS Analytics reports all four, with sample, field, and protocol, and leaves the contradiction visible.

> **News × market:** the day brought a single-judge decision from the [STF](/en/glossary#stf) cutting contact between the candidate and his main campaign operative, an order to investigate early campaign advertising, and the [PF](/en/glossary#pf) pointing to the Chamber presidency's approval in an amendments scheme. The impeachment contract for a [STF](/en/glossary#stf) minister did not move (**2.75%**), and Flávio's contract **rose** (↑0.60pp). Real money prices institutional friction as cost, not rupture.

## In summary

1. **The [Gerp](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) is no longer alone.** The tightening in the [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) that a small outlier institute signaled on 08/Jul appeared on 13/Jul at a top-tier institute. [Nexus/BTG Pactual](/en/glossary#nexus-btg) gives [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) (47% × 44%) and removes 2pp from Lula in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno).

2. **The market ignored the survey and priced the family.** Lula stayed flat at 60.50% on the day of the most adverse data of the cycle, while Michelle doubled (2.25%) and Jair fell (1.05%), on the same day Moraes cut contact between father and son.

3. **The [Quaest from 15/Jul](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) is the test.** Of the four national surveys of the week, it has the highest methodological reliability. If it confirms the tightening, the +36.65pp divergence becomes the fragile number on the panel, not the robust one.-
