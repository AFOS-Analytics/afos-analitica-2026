## 1. Prediction market

The total accumulated volume in the presidential market adds up to **USD 143.79M**, and this synthesis uses the confirmed reading of September 5, 13:29 BRT. In this round the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) did not confirm across the house's two readings and gets no new price: the values of September 4 stand, with **Lula at 55.50% (USD 9.76M)** and **Flávio Bolsonaro at 40.15% (USD 9.69M)**, a distance of 15.35pp. **Renan Santos** is at **1.85% (USD 12.68M)** and **Augusto Cury** at **1.95% (USD 4.08M)**.

What moved was the position market. In the [first-round runner-up contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), of USD 6.50M, **Flávio rose 2.00pp to 89.50% (USD 597 thousand)** and **Lula gave up 2.25pp to 5.35% (USD 470 thousand)**. Only one point in the series has ever been above 89.50%, the 90.50% of August 21.

In the [first-round third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), of USD 1.01M, **Cury gave up 1.60pp to 51.95% (USD 124 thousand)**, adding 7.55pp since September 3, and **Renan rose 1.00pp to 24.50% (USD 278 thousand)**. **Caiado rose 0.50pp to 9.50% (USD 114 thousand)** and **Marçal gave up 0.50pp to 2.20% (USD 47 thousand)**.

The contract on a [Supreme Court justice being impeached before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) returned to the double reading and stands at **8.65% (USD 88 thousand)**, against 15.85% the day before, a fall of 7.20pp. On September 4 it went out on a single reading with the day's range declared beside it, and now it did not need that.

In the [Senate majority contract](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), of USD 300 thousand, the **PL gave up 0.50pp to 82.00% (USD 260 thousand)** and the **MDB gave up 1.50pp to 10.75%**. In [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the 5.00% to 5.49% band leads with 41.95% (USD 16 thousand).

## 3. What the press covered

The crisis involving the Supreme Court remained the dominant subject, with no new act on September 5. The chronology, verified against two independent sources, puts the acts on September 3 and 4: on September 4 the Prosecutor-General's Office [ordered an inquiry into whether there was interference](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/pgr-manda-investigar-se-houve-interferencia-em-relatorio-da-pf-sobre-moraes-e-vorcaro/) in the production of the Federal Police report on the messages between Daniel Vorcaro and Alexandre de Moraes, and asked for authorisation to investigate André Mendonça, according to Gazeta do Povo. On the same day, the [Federal Prosecution Council opened a case](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/conselho-do-mpf-abre-processo-sobre-gonet-por-conversas-com-daniel-vorcaro/) on the prosecutor-general's conduct, according to Estadão.

On September 5 there was signalling, not decision: Edson Fachin [signalled an intention to take both cases to the full bench](https://g1.globo.com/politica/blog/valdo-cruz/post/2026/09/05/fachin-indica-intencao-de-levar-ao-plenario-crise-envolvendo-moraes-e-mendonca.ghtml), according to G1, and the decision has not been taken. Folha recorded that Lula [narrowed his campaign's inner circle](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiywFBVV95cUxNRHktcDY5bXlYZ3Yzc3JJVDVjUXdJZkw2MHB0a1VkcjVKTTFnamN4eXE0dVlJZThueWE5NGZ6YjVUdVp0N2k5eDNhOURqajMxYWM3b1kwQ2RUbVVRcUY0U2NidnhxSnBWOHFEYlRYNy1ETUxCRFpTdWRhcUw5YTlIOUxHOTlwUlFTaG5SRUVRSk5WRjFhaXhkeHVTdmNtcFRtQlBUVWJOT3h0SFBERHNWQ0VmREltRmFya2w2UTY4d29ILUpCaTFCZDJwMA?oc=5). ## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** three position books moved while the declared ballot has been silent for two days. The most recent vote intention to enter the [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) is from September 3, and the price moved without it.

> **Market × market:** at the same instant, the winner contract did not confirm and the other four did, among them the [first-round runner-up](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place). It is the same election measured in different contracts, and the panel does not add them up.

> **Register × coverage:** three houses appeared in "new poll" headlines on September 5, among them [BTG/Nexus](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMizgFBVV95cUxQckZDblY3LXVsZVNrZW9tMGppQXNOVkdHR2UtOXNHMFFkazIwdmtFaExaUVdoMWFyLWVXWFU1ZUNJS3Fld005OW5hUXc2RHF3c1h5Q0FkdWtmeFFia2tWaG1lREFiZlZZby1wRkVvMzktOENFRVFsd25PQTFiOFhPSmFTd19aOGFIOWpuLWQ2MmZ2MUFUQVJEXzcxMHo4SS1ZSVY4NUlRVjBHNXdJVEtzOHNvNmUyeGlRT3RYUHQ3ZFcwVHJoY1E2ZzZlZXdrQQ?oc=5) according to VEJA, and the TSE schedules their releases for September 8, 9 and 10.

## In short

1. **The winner contract received no new price, and that absence is the method showing on the screen.** Two readings taken eight minutes apart disagreed by 0.30pp on one of the names, and the house rule is to keep the last confirmed price and declare it. The cost is showing the previous day's value; the gain is that no unconfirmed number enters as a measurement.
2. **The [impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) fell 7.20pp and returned to the normal rule, but its size is part of the information.** It holds USD 88 thousand accumulated, the panel's smallest book, and in it the price travelled much of its own range in two days: a high of 19.30% on September 4, 8.65% now and a low of 2.10% on June 8. There was no new act on September 5, and the panel records the move without attributing a cause.
3. **The TSE calendar concentrates ten national polls between September 6 and 10, and it is the densest week of the cycle so far.** Two carry a sample of 5,000, PALVER and AtlasIntel, and 46 state-level registrations from the same period were left out of the table by scope. After two days with no new vote intention entering the market, the week ahead returns ten readings in five days, and the register is a reminder that registered is not published.
