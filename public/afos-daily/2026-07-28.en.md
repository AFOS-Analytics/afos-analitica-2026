## 1. Prediction market

With 68 days to the first round, total accumulated volume in the presidential market is around **USD 116.26M** ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) via the AFOS proxy, capture of Jul 28 at 6:13pm BRT, cleared by the double-reading lock, and re-checked at 7:13pm with no relevant change in the presidential book). **Lula** rose 1.00pp to **63.50% (USD 7.69M)**. **Flávio Bolsonaro** held at **23.85% (USD 7.69M)** for a second session, and the gap between the two went to **+39.65pp**.

Lula's number needs one exact sentence, and it is not "new record". In the [full series of the contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), 63.50% **matches** the ceiling of the AFOS series, marked on July 26 at 22:30 UTC, and does not pass it. The widest gap in the series is still **+39.80pp**, from those same early hours, above today's +39.65pp. This synthesis draws that distinction because it got exactly this wrong in the [July 26](/en/daily/2026-07-26) edition and corrected it on [July 27](/en/daily/2026-07-27).

In the [chasing pack](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), the day belonged to **Renan Santos**, and downwards. He fell 0.80pp in the winner contract, to **8.75% (USD 8.58M)**, losing the 9% handle. **Ronaldo Caiado** fell 0.05pp, to **1.90% (USD 5.19M)**, **Jair Bolsonaro** held at **1.05% (USD 5.26M)**, **Romeu Zema** at **0.55% (USD 4.55M)**, **Michelle Bolsonaro** rose 0.05pp to **0.45% (USD 9.32M)**, **Fernando Haddad** held at **0.25% (USD 6.38M)** and **Tarcísio de Freitas** at **0.15% (USD 13.65M)**, still with the highest accumulated volume of any contract in the book, a legacy anomaly that measures traded history and not current conviction.

The strong move is in the sub-markets, and it separates two things that look like one. In [second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio rose 1.50pp, to **78.00% (USD 4.33M in the book)**, and Renan fell 0.50pp, to **12.15%**. In [third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan gave up 1.50pp, to **60.50%**, and **Caiado went from a range of 19.00% to 19.50% up to 26.50%**, in a book of **USD 483 thousand**, which adds 11.00pp over two sessions counting the 15.50% of July 26. In other words, the market is repricing **who finishes third**, not who wins the election: in the presidential contract, Caiado fell. Renan, by contrast, fell in **all three** contracts where he appears, which is different from the previous day's mixed signals.

In the other markets, [impeachment of an STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) **fell 0.60pp, to 2.80% (USD 83 thousand)**, breaking four essentially flat sessions, and the direction is down, meaning less risk priced in. In the [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the **PL** held at **71.00% (USD 292 thousand in the book)** and the MDB fell 1.00pp, to 17.25%. In the [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the modal band **flipped**: 4.50% to 4.99% went to **39.25%**, against 26.30% the day before, and moved ahead of the 5.00% to 5.49% band, which sat at 38.15%, in a book of **USD 79 thousand**. Two caveats before any reading: the book is small, and the inflation market is **not covered** by the double-reading lock, which watches only the five electoral contracts.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Price × absence of polling:** the [favourite's price](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) rose 1.00pp on a day when **no national poll was published**. There is no polling anchor to explain the move, and the same session carried four candidates for a cause: the extension of the US emergency, the WTO filing, three state polls and the echo of a statement on re-election probability made on July 24. Picking one would invent precision the data does not have. This synthesis records the absence of an anchor, which is the honest information available.

> **National average × state map:** the national reading gives Lula a comfortable cushion, and the day's three state polls show why that cushion is an average and not a uniform lead. In Pernambuco he has 55% against 21%; in Minas he is in a [statistical tie](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiogFBVV95cUxPZHRfWDVUaTZwclZueFhPRFNRcnVuek9MNEVYRWkyU0NqT0pBc0dBU0cxUGZ4a3JSOG9pZnJHazJNQlRkOFB1MTVtZ2phSUlieVNLeGYxQnZuT2VtOHF4dW5udFM2NURpZS1HUk84Qy1uTUd1VF8yRE0yd3J4d3FOUXlRVDJJdzJGR3dmTkZEbHhMWXM4aloyLVZOVXl2Q1h2VHc?oc=5) and behind Zema in the runoff; in Rio he is behind Flávio. None of them enters this panel's national set, and all three matter for understanding what the average hides.

> **Third-place contract × presidential contract:** Caiado rose from the range of 19.00% to 19.50% up to **26.50%** in third place in the first round and, in the same session, **fell** 0.05pp in the presidential contract. Those are opposite moves by the same name on the same day, and the correct reading is that the market is repricing the race for third place, not the race for the presidency. Add to that the counterweight from [Poder360](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirwFBVV95cUxQaGJaNmJHZ3RoY25FRGJtdG1Vem5Zc25JREY3N282ZFFKNTRDRm1TdkpCaFFoTFVuYmdwVExBLS1HRmJZYWFNd09YWnk5ZEhtb2lMZzFkQzJmTGlXWVIzaFdNSUlkRlJJUXFVTFpEV21Fb2l2eFlQZjdLZ3JWMVNnVnR5Um9zMHEtaVlTdkxUZG14SmFrVENsZ2stQXI3SWpZVVBSQm96VHlBWFJUckM4?oc=5): his competitiveness is concentrated in Goiás, worth 3.2% of the electorate.

> **Institutional pile-up × impeachment price:** Tuesday had [the PGR brought in by Moraes](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/07/28/moraes-manda-pgr-se-manifestar-sobre-depoimento-cancelado-de-flavio-em-inquerito-que-apura-calunia-contra-lula.ghtml), judiciary associations responding to a foreign head of state's attack, and the Master congressional inquiry stalled for four months behind a writ that goes unjudged. Even so, the [market on the impeachment of an STF justice](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) **fell** 0.60pp, to 2.80%. Recording the direction is mandatory; building a narrative on it is not, because the whole book adds up to USD 83 thousand and moves on very little money.

> **Correction on the record:** earlier today, in the panel, this synthesis published that the 60% re-election probability attributed to the Quaest director had been stated at the Lide luncheon of July 27. Source verification pointed the other way: the remark is from **Expert XP on July 24**, the figure is **"close to 60%"** and not exactly 60%, and therefore the subtraction against the market's 63.50% does not hold ([InfoMoney](https://www.infomoney.com.br/politica/lula-chega-mais-forte-ao-2o-semestre-mas-disputa-tem-interrogacao-dizem-analistas/)). The panel was corrected before this publication, and the cross-reading itself remains on the record: they are two independent estimates of the **same quantity**, probability of victory, which this panel had never had before, and they now appear side by side, without subtraction.

## In summary

1. **The price rose with no polling.** [Lula](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) went to 63.50%, matching the ceiling of the series without passing it, on a day with no national poll at all. The absence of an anchor is the record, not the cause.
2. **The third-place realignment entered its second day.** In the [third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Caiado added 11.00pp over two sessions and Renan fell in all three where he appears, while Caiado's presidential contract moved the other way.
3. **Two absences count as much as the presences:** the AtlasIntel poll with n=5,000, [registered with the TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), did not come out, and the Master congressional inquiry remains dammed for four months behind a ruling that is not delivered.
