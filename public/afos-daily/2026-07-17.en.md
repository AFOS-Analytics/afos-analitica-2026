## 1. Prediction market

[presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) moved on a day when no national poll was published, and the movement came entirely from one side. **Flávio Bolsonaro rose to 27.05%** (↑1.60pp, USD 7.44M), the largest movement on the panel, and narrowed the gap to Lula single-handedly, who remained at **+33.45pp**. **Lula stayed flat at 60.50%** (0.00pp, USD 7.41M). The gap shrank for the second consecutive session, but through opposite mechanism than the previous day: on July 16 it decreased because Lula moved toward PoderData, and now it decreased because Flávio rose, with Lula unchanged. Without new polling and without clear event behind it, Flávio's rise is market momentum, price adjusting conviction, not reaction to a fact. Total accumulated volume in the presidential market totals **USD 113.6M**.

The reading matters because it contradicts weeks of behavior. During much of June and July, Flávio's price remained stubbornly flat even amid poor polling. Today the market repriced it upward on its own, on a day without a trigger. Even so, the bet sustaining this price gave back a little at the margin: in [sub-market for second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio fell to **82.00%** (↓1.50pp, USD 180 thousand), the probability of being Lula's opponent in the runoff.

In the third way, **Renan Santos fell to 7.85%** (↓0.55pp, USD 8.15M), accumulating a drop from 10.15% to 7.85% in three sessions, and fell in [third place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) to **68.00%** (↓0.50pp, USD 140 thousand). This is the cleanest convergence on the running panel: the price continues descending toward the 6% that the latest poll gave him. **Ronaldo Caiado** remained stable at **1.25%** (USD 4.95M) and rose to **14.50%** in 3rd place (↑0.50pp). In the lower tier, the market reshuffled names without ballot backing: **Michelle Bolsonaro rose to 2.15%** (↑0.75pp, USD 9.06M) and **Fernando Haddad fell to 0.45%** (↓0.80pp, USD 6.24M), the largest relative drop on the panel. **Romeu Zema** fell to **0.55%** (↓0.10pp, USD 4.40M).

In institutional sub-markets, the day was stable. [PL continues at 86.00%](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won) in the Senate by seats (USD 250 thousand), against 11.80% for [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) (USD 10 thousand). [impeachment of STF minister](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stayed practically flat at **3.40%** (↑0.05pp, USD 84 thousand), giving back the previous day's rise and returning to the noise range. Scale caveat applies: this book sums tens of thousands of dollars, against USD 7.4M for the presidential, so a variation there requires far less money than the same in the main market.

In [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the band of **5.00% to 5.49% stayed at 37.95%** (USD 10 thousand) and the 4.50% to 4.99% at 26.85%, totaling around 64.8% between the two. The Treasury's official projection, of 5.1%, remains within the modal range of real money.

## 2. What the institutes recorded

The day had no new national poll. The two most recent readings remain the **Genial/[Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest) of July 15** ([BR-07181/2026](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/)), which gave first round Lula 40% × Flávio 28% and runoff 45% × 37%, and the **PoderData/Aya of July 16** ([BR-00059/2026](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/)), which returned the runoff to [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico), 45% × 43%, with first round 40% × 34%. The background of voting, therefore, has not changed: Lula is identical in both readings, 40% in the first round and 45% in the runoff, and the entire disagreement, six points, is in Flávio, 28% against 34%.

This leaves the panel in a data pause until next Tuesday. **Real Time Big Data and Instituto Indexa publish nationals on July 21**, both [registered with TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), with fields that only close on July 19 and 20, after confirmation of the 25% United States tariff. They will be the first to measure the electorate with the tariff already in effect. A third national, from **Gerp**, is scheduled for July 22.
