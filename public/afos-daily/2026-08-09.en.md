## 1. Prediction market

This edition publishes the reading of **Aug 9, 15:03 BRT**, confirmed by two readings. The [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) has **Lula at 63.50% (USD 8.18M)** against **Flávio at 26.95% (USD 8.09M)**, a gap of **+36.55pp**. Total accumulated volume is **USD 121.32M**.

**The gap narrowed again, and from one side only.** Lula lost 1.00pp and broke below the five-day plateau that had held since Aug 4. Flávio stood still for a third day. On the previous days the narrowing came from both ends, with the runner-up gaining what the leader lost. **Today only the leader moved.** In the [AFOS series](https://www.afos-analytics.com/en/dashboard/br), since Aug 1 the gap has fallen on seven of the eight days, with a single flat day, coming down from +41.80pp.

**The strongest move is in the [third place book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), and it contradicts the Aug 8 reading.** Renan Santos jumped **6.00pp, to 64.50% (USD 176 thousand)**, and Caiado fell **5.50pp, to 25.50% (USD 44 thousand)**, in an almost exact mirror. On Aug 8 Caiado fell 2.50pp and Renan did not rise alongside, and the panel recorded that the probability was leaving the book instead of migrating inside it. Today it migrated.

In [second place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio **stopped at 81.50% (USD 242 thousand)** after losing 6.00pp in two days, and **Renan opened up over Lula, with 8.25% (USD 1.10M) against 6.75% (USD 160 thousand)**, a 1.50pp distance when on Aug 8 it was 0.10pp.

Behind them, **Renan Santos at 7.80% (USD 9.27M)**, still with more money traded than Lula, **Caiado at 1.25% (USD 5.60M)** and **Zema at 0.45% (USD 5.02M)**, the latter back below the 0.5% cut. [STF impeachment](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) rose to **3.60% (USD 83 thousand)** and the **PL is at 74.50% (USD 259 thousand)** in the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Book × book:** in the [third place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan Santos rose **6.00pp** and Caiado fell **5.50pp**, in an almost exact mirror. On Aug 8 Caiado fell alone and the probability left the book. The direction was the same on both days and the mechanism was not, and that is why the panel describes mechanism rather than trend.

> **Price × price:** the [gap](https://www.afos-analytics.com/en/dashboard/br) narrowed 1.00pp without the runner-up gaining anything. When both ends move there is a transfer; when only the leader gives way, the probability may have gone to any other outcome in the book, including none.

> **Market × poll:** the price moved on four of the last five days and the polling was not measured once in the period. The six national polls between Aug 10 and Aug 13 are the first chance to compare.
