## 1. Prediction market

With 69 days to the first round, total accumulated volume in the presidential market is around **USD 116.13M** ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) via the AFOS proxy, capture of Jul 27 at 4:23pm BRT and re-verification at 5:09pm with no change in the presidential book). **Lula** stayed at **62.50% (USD 7.68M)** for a second straight session, with no variation at all. **Flávio Bolsonaro** rose 0.20pp, to **23.85% (USD 7.68M)**, and the gap between the two stood at **+38.65pp**.

This edition records here a correction it owes to anyone who read the [July 26](/en/daily/2026-07-26) edition. That edition stated that 62.50% was the ceiling of the AFOS series and that +38.85pp came down from a peak of +39.65pp. Sweeping the [full contract series](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), the ceiling of Lula's price is **63.50%** and the widest gap on record is **+39.80pp**, both marked in the early hours of July 26, five hours after the capture the panel published. The record happened after the edition went out, and the correction comes with the same prominence the original claim had.

The mover was the [chasing pack](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), and one name in it. **Ronaldo Caiado** rose 0.20pp, to **1.95% (USD 5.15M)**, and **Romeu Zema** fell 0.10pp, to **0.55% (USD 4.55M)**, on the very day he was formally nominated. **Renan Santos** held at **9.55% (USD 8.56M)** after two sessions of losses. Across the rest of the group, **Jair Bolsonaro** stayed at **1.05% (USD 5.25M)**, **Michelle Bolsonaro** fell 0.35pp to **0.40% (USD 9.29M)**, **Fernando Haddad** fell 0.10pp to **0.25% (USD 6.38M)** and **Tarcísio de Freitas** stayed at **0.15% (USD 13.64M)**, holding the largest individual volume in the book, a legacy anomaly that measures traded history rather than current conviction.

In the sub-markets the pattern was clearer. In [second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio fell 1.50pp, to **76.50% (USD 4.32M in the book)**, and Renan rose 0.80pp, to **12.65%**. In [third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan gave up 2.50pp, to **62.00%**, and Caiado went from 15.50% to a range of **19.00% to 19.50%**, in a book worth **USD 481 thousand**. Flávio's three contracts moved in different directions in the same session, the opposite of the previous day's clean reading, and that does not support a single narrative. In the [Senate seats market](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the **PL** stood at **71.00% (USD 292 thousand in the book)** and the MDB at 18.25%. The [impeachment of an STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) ranged between **3.35% and 3.40% (USD 83 thousand)** across the day's captures, a fourth session essentially flat. In the [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the modal band remains 5.00% to 5.49%, at **39.20%**, followed by the 4.50% to 4.99% band at 26.35%, in a book worth **USD 77 thousand**, a distribution centre that remains above the target ceiling.

A methodological caveat about the capture, because hiding it would be worse than recording it: the AFOS double-reading lock **blocked both rounds** on July 27. In the first, the second-place and third-place contracts diverged; in the second, only Caiado's third place, between 19.00% and 19.50%. The presidential book returned identical numbers across all four readings, over 17 minutes, which is why the winner prices enter this edition as firm, while Caiado's placement figure enters as a range rather than a settled price.

## 2. What the pollsters recorded

The **BTG/Nexus** poll released on July 27 is the first national survey whose fieldwork sits entirely after the convention that formalized Flávio Bolsonaro's candidacy on July 25, which makes it the first real measurement of that event's effect. In the first round: **Lula 42% and Flávio 33%**, with Ronaldo Caiado at 6%, Renan Santos at 5% and Romeu Zema at 3%. In the head-to-head runoff, **Lula 47% x Flávio 43%** ([Folha de S.Paulo](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiugFBVV95cUxOeGtyYnI1ZVYwcUVkZmNrel9PRWY1T1prdlNVZHdmT1ZjY19rVWJ6TDZYbkxkUm9odFRQRDdFU0IwSnZYVkJUYmxacWczTnc4N1d5X0c4NTIzWHBPNlA3SHdxVUEyWFBxR1hzZGxNR0VkMFdnSFhfbE1CVk1fdjRfRG9SSDA1S2Y0dS1waUVnVWFlMk8wcGtUSWZLZ0ExWTA0Q0pzVlRyRFhDZ095SmN3blBYOFJaSWdhbFE?oc=5), [CNN Brasil](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMijAFBVV95cUxPQzh4QjJfbTU1UzNiQ1VHTjN6Z0NlcGUxUXBfaWh4eGpIcl9iaDlycWphdi1aSGJ3OVlBMDFtcHFSUDJydDJmVmh6blZQMzJxTGs4RnJUV0R0TXctZ0JMUGc1eUNDc3BnZHlQZXkwQVZSeGRJRGRBeGxDUXFDaU1hQ05iUlFpODljbFI2TA?oc=5), [the institute's own release](https://www.nexus.fsb.com.br/estudos-divulgados/pesquisa-btg-nexus-de-intencao-de-votos-para-presidente-do-brasil-27-de-julho-de-2026/)). Fieldwork July 24 to 26, n=2,004, telephone across all regions, 2pp margin, 95% confidence, TSE filing BR-01489/2026, commissioned by Banco BTG Pactual.

Against the institute's own July 13 round, Lula goes from 40% to 42% and Flávio from 34% to 33%, which widens the first-round difference from 6pp to 9pp ([JOTA](https://www.jota.info/eleicoes/eleicoes-2026/btg-nexus-lula-mantem-lideranca-flavio-bolsonaro-reduz-diferenca-no-primeiro-turno), [Money Times](https://www.moneytimes.com.br/btg-pactual-nexus-pesquisa-27-de-julho-gaep/)). Both moves fit inside the margin, so the widening is nominal and the correct reading is stability with a favourable tilt, not advance. The PL convention, measured for the first time, **produced no launch effect**: Flávio came out one point lower. His rejection stood at **50%**, the highest in the survey, against Lula's 48% ([Poder360](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMisgFBVV95cUxQWGd5OWlDb2xRaGtOTHBQSlFBZnhrb0t5TkhYODJ0SE9WMklJV3RjaVp0elJjdjR4V2M3RGUwZVhZTUlrMlIzRVM4eU15VFJYRWxiaE9TSHA5cnBBZzV5RzdoQXBpWWNYRDYxSVF5VlFQakw4QVE3emR1ekluMUI2SVFGbzhPc01CY0h6TEdqZmRrRUZqOFVxd0pRLUZjT0RDSXo5QTAySG1xYUtIUkc3WHJn?oc=5)).

The poll's finding, however, is not about the top two. **Caiado ties Lula statistically at 45% to 43%**, the narrowest of the four runoff scenarios tested, ahead of Zema (46% x 42%), Flávio (47% x 43%) and Renan Santos (47% x 39%) ([Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/brasil/btg-nexus-lula-empata-com-flavio-zema-e-caiado-no-2o-turno), [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/eleicoes/caiado-cresce-e-empata-tecnicamente-com-lula-no-2o-turno-indica-nexus-btg/)). On approval, the institute shows 47% against 49% disapproval, versus 47% x 47% on July 13, and government ratings at 36% excellent or good against 43% poor or terrible ([Estado de Minas](https://www.em.com.br/politica/2026/07/7468485-btg-nexus-desaprovacao-a-lula-e-maior-que-aprovacao.html)). The Datafolha poll of July 24 showed the reverse on approval, 49% x 48%: both readings carry a 2pp margin and both are statistical ties, which describes a narrow band around 48% rather than a swing.

Also on July 27, a cut of the Datafolha round of July 22 and 23 circulated, with n=2,004, in which **52% believe Trump's tariff push is meant to favour Flávio Bolsonaro's candidacy**, against 37% who disagree and 11% who do not know, running at 73% among Lula voters and 29% among his own ([Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/brasil/datafolha-52-veem-tarifaco-ao-brasil-como-ajuda-de-trump-a-flavio)). It is an opinion cut on an external topic, not a vote-intention measurement.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Institute × institute:** the day's sharpest divergence is not between market and polling, it is between two top-tier houses. [BTG/Nexus](https://www.nexus.fsb.com.br/estudos-divulgados/pesquisa-btg-nexus-de-intencao-de-votos-para-presidente-do-brasil-27-de-julho-de-2026/) puts Caiado two points from Lula in the runoff (45% to 43%) and measures him at 6% in the first round; the Datafolha poll of [July 24](/en/daily/2026-07-25) gave a seven-point cushion (47% to 40%) and 4% in the first round. Fieldwork two days apart, opposite conclusions about which challenger is the most competitive, and no third measurement to break the tie. AFOS records the divergence and does not arbitrate which one is right.

> **Polling × price, agreeing this time:** in the same session in which the poll put Caiado in a statistical tie, the [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) raised his price in both contracts where he appears, 0.20pp in the winner contract and a rise from 15.50% to a range of 19.00% to 19.50% in third place. Convergence between the two sources is more informative than price moving alone, and in his case it had not happened for two weeks. It is worth recording with the same prominence a divergence would get.

> **Launch × market, third occurrence:** on July 25 the PL formalized Flávio and his price fell 0.10pp; on July 26 the PSD formalized Caiado and it fell 0.15pp; on July 27 [Novo formalized Zema](https://www.em.com.br/politica/2026/07/7468607-novo-oficializa-zema-como-candidato-a-presidencia-da-republica.html) and it fell 0.10pp. Three consecutive occurrences are no longer an isolated coincidence, but they still come with no cause asserted: these are small books, single-instant captures, and nothing guarantees the next convention repeats it. It stands as an observation to track until August 5.

> **Institutes × Renan Santos:** the new poll measures Renan at 5% in the first round and **breaks** the convergence at 3% that Datafolha, Gerp and Indexa had formed last week. The panel is back to three levels among recent readings: 3% at three institutes, 5% at Nexus and 9% at Real Time on July 21. The distortion against his market price still exists and now has a less clear numerator, because the polling itself has started to disagree with itself.

## In summary

1. The first [poll with fieldwork after the PL convention](https://www.nexus.fsb.com.br/estudos-divulgados/pesquisa-btg-nexus-de-intencao-de-votos-para-presidente-do-brasil-27-de-julho-de-2026/) confirmed the top, with Lula at 42% and Flávio at 33%, and recorded no launch effect for the candidate formalized two days earlier.

3. Three candidates were formally nominated in three days and all three ended the session lower in the book. The week still brings [AtlasIntel on July 28](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), at n=5,000, to say which of the two readings on Caiado holds up.
