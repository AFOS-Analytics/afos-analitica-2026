## 1. Prediction market

Total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to **USD 137.64M**, and the prices are from the confirmed reading of Aug 27, 10:49 PM BRT (1:49 AM UTC on Aug 28), with all five contracts cleared by the double reading.

Lula closed at **57.50% (USD 9.30M)**, down 5.00pp on the day, and the decline was continuous, across five successive readings. It is his lowest level since Jul 1, checked in the [database backup](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) against the record since Apr 14. Flávio Bolsonaro rose 1.30pp and closed at **36.85% (USD 9.11M)**, the largest gain of the day and his highest level since May 13. The gap between the two fell from 26.95pp to **20.65pp**, narrowing from both sides.

The structural change is **Augusto Cury (USD 2.12M)**, who had no contract until Aug 26 and closed at **4.05%**, the third largest price in the book, with **23.75% (USD 33,000)** in the third-place contract and **3.70% (USD 57,000)** in the second-place one. His series has five points, all from Aug 27, between 0.80% and 5.00%: in a new contract, a swing like that does not tell price from noise.

Renan Santos closed at **2.35% (USD 11.65M)** after touching the floor of his series, at 1.70% across four readings in the record and with none of the 350 points since Apr 14 below that. In the third-place contract he gave up **9.50pp, to 36.00% (USD 207,000)**, the largest single move on the panel, and he is still the most expensive name there. Ronaldo Caiado gave up 5.50pp in that contract, to **33.50% (USD 68,000)**, and closed at **0.15% (USD 6.80M)** in the outright-winner one. Flávio gave up 3.00pp in the second-place contract, to **84.50% (USD 388,000)**, on the day Cury came to be worth 3.70% there.

In the sub-markets, impeachment of a Supreme Court justice before 2027 stays at **3.40% (USD 84,000)**, a fourth day flat, and the PL holds **77.00% (USD 259,000)** in the Senate.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market vs polling, and it is the clearest case of Aug 27:** Ronaldo Caiado ties technically with Lula in PoderData's runoff, 43% to 44%, and is worth 0.15% in the outright-winner contract. Romeu Zema is in the same position. These are different questions, because one measures the hypothetical runoff and the other the probability of getting there, and the panel publishes both without choosing.

> **Market vs market:** Renan Santos is the most expensive name in the third-place contract, at 36.00%, and on the same day touched the floor of his series in the outright-winner one. Finishing third and winning are distinct bets, and they moved in opposite directions for the same name.

> **Poll vs poll:** PoderData measures the first round at 3 points, Gerp of Aug 26 had the runner-up ahead by 1 and Indexa of the same day had the leader ahead by 5. Three houses, one week, six points of range.

## In summary

1. **The move of the day came from both sides.** The leader gave up 5.00pp and the runner-up rose 1.30pp, on the same day PoderData published a technical tie in both rounds. The panel records the coincidence and assigns no cause.

2. **A new name started carrying a price and is already third in the book.** Augusto Cury closed at 4.05%, while the polling measured him between 1% and 3%.
