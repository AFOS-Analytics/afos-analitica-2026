## 1. Prediction market

In the [outright winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) of the presidential election, Flávio Bolsonaro stands at 58.65% (vol USD 11.15M accumulated) and Lula at 41.50% (vol USD 11.45M accumulated). Total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 152.02M. Both rose in 24 hours, Flávio by 1.90pp and Lula by 1.00pp, and the gap widened from 16.25pp to 17.15pp.

That gap is the widest of the 155 days in which the [recorded series](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) has both sides, since April 14, 2026, measured at daily closes, which are a floor because they hide the peak inside the day. The previous one was 16.30pp, on September 19. The climb starts from 2.10pp on September 12, passing through 14.00pp on the 18th. On the 19th what opened the distance was Lula's fall; on the 20th it was the opposite.

In the [third place contract of the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Renan Santos rises 1.50pp and closes at 52.50% (vol USD 355 thousand accumulated) and Augusto Cury falls 3.80pp, to 32.55% (vol USD 237 thousand accumulated), the largest fall of the day. Ronaldo Caiado stays at 11.50% and Romeu Zema rises 0.90pp, to 1.25%. In the winner book, the largest accumulated volume does not belong to whoever leads the price: it belongs to Tarcísio de Freitas, USD 14.08M, parked at 0.05%.

Two contracts did not receive a new price, and for those the panel publishes the range of the recorded readings. In [second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), on September 19 Flávio's contract ranged from 69.00% to 73.50% and Lula's from 25.80% to 29.20%; in the Senate majority contract, the Partido Liberal one ranged from 82.50% to 85.00%.

The [contract paying out on a Supreme Court justice leaving](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) by impeachment before 2027 stands at 4.40% (vol USD 92 thousand accumulated), 0.15pp below the 4.55% of September 19. It is a [thin book](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027), and the move fits inside its own noise.

## 2. What the pollsters registered

No new national poll with numbers in a primary source has come out since September 17. The two most recent remain [Datafolha](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) (n=2,002, in person, margin of 2.0pp) and Futura/100% Cidades (n=2,000, by phone, margin of 2.2pp). In both, Lula leads the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), 39% to 36% and 38.3% to 37.7%, and in Datafolha he leads the simulated [runoff](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) by 46% to 44%. The three distances fit inside two margins.

Instituto Veritá released on September 20 a survey presented as national, with 40,500 interviews. The document shows that this number is the consolidation of 27 state polls, each with its own [registration at the TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), and the sum of the 27 samples comes to exactly 40,500. The margin published with it, 2.0 to 3.5 points, is the range of the state margins, and for that reason the number does not enter the AFOS panel.

The [TSE registry](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) grew by 41 rows in the September 20 ingestion, above the 31 of the 19th, and two independent counts agree that no poll left the archive in the interval.

## 3. What the press covered

The subject of the day was a video published on September 20 by Lauro Jardim's column in O Globo, showing Justice Alexandre de Moraes and his wife, the lawyer Viviane Barci, [leaving an Embraer Legacy 650](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/video-registra-alexandre-de-moraes-saindo-de-aviao-de-vorcaro.shtml) belonging to a company that had Daniel Vorcaro as a partner. The flight is from August 22, 2025. The Barci de Moraes firm denied, in a statement, any personal tie to aircraft owners. ## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x poll:** the [price](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) puts Flávio 17.15pp ahead, and the two most recent national polls, those of September 17, put Lula ahead in the first round and in Datafolha's simulated runoff. What grew was not disagreement between two new numbers: it was the price moving with the poll standing still.

> **Market x market:** in the [third place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Renan Santos stands at 52.50% and Augusto Cury at 32.55%, while the two national polls of September 17 put Cury third and Renan fourth. This divergence does not depend on margin of error: they are different positions, not close numbers.

> **Narrative x price:** the institutional subject dominated coverage on September 20, and the [impeachment contract at the Supreme Court](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) gave back 0.15pp, from 4.55% to 4.40%. AFOS does not attribute the move to the news cycle: it records that the price did not react in the direction the coverage would suggest.
