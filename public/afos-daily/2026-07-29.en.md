## 1. Prediction market

With 67 days to the first round, total accumulated volume in the presidential market is around **USD 116.49M** ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) via the AFOS proxy, capture of July 29 at 7:50pm BRT). **Lula** fell 1.00pp to **62.50% (USD 7.73M)**. **Flávio Bolsonaro** rose 0.10pp to **23.95% (USD 7.70M)**, and the gap between the two eased to **+38.55pp**.

Lula's number needs two sentences, and the order matters. This is not a drop in level: 62.50% is the same value as July 25 and July 27, and the top of the AFOS series marked 63.50% on July 26 and July 28. What happened was the return of the point taken the day before. By the daily close of the series, today's gap is the narrowest since July 24, and the widest gap in the series remains **+39.80pp**, from July 26.

In the [pack](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), the day's move belongs to **Ronaldo Caiado**, who rose 0.45pp to **2.35% (USD 5.20M)**, a value above every daily close of the series since June 20. **Renan Santos** fell 0.05pp to **8.70% (USD 8.59M)**, **Jair Bolsonaro** stayed at **1.05% (USD 5.26M)**, **Romeu Zema** rose 0.20pp to **0.75% (USD 4.57M)**, **Geraldo Alckmin** rose 0.10pp to **0.35% (USD 4.94M)**, **Fernando Haddad** stayed at **0.25% (USD 6.38M)** and **Tarcísio de Freitas** at **0.15% (USD 13.66M)**, still the highest accumulated volume in the book, a legacy anomaly that measures traded history and not current conviction. **Michelle Bolsonaro** enters today as a **range of 0.35% to 0.55% (USD 9.34M)**, and the reason is in the capture note below.

In the sub-markets, the signal inverted against the previous day. In [first-round second place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio rose 0.50pp to **78.50% (USD 4.34M in the book)**, Renan fell 0.45pp to **11.70%**, and Haddad rose 0.30pp to **1.15%**. In [third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), in a book of **USD 484,000**, Caiado **fell** 1.00pp to **25.50%**, and Renan **recovered** 1.00pp to **61.50%**. Yesterday Caiado was rising in the placement book and falling in the winner book; today he did the opposite. The correct reading is that the market moved on the race for the presidency, not on the race for third place.

Among the other markets, [impeachment of an STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) **rose 0.55pp to 3.35% (USD 83,000)**, after falling 0.60pp the day before, and it was the only contract that followed the direction of the day's institutional pile-up. The caveat comes before the reading: 0.55pp in a book that size is very little money, so what is on the record is the direction and not the size. In the [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the **PL** stayed at **71.00% (USD 292,000 in the book)** and the MDB rose 0.35pp to 17.60%. In the [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the modal band went **back** to 5.00% to 5.49%, at **38.30%**, while the 4.50% to 4.99% band fell to 34.90%, in a book of **USD 81,000**. In other words, the shift this brief recorded on July 28 undid itself in a single session, and the inflation market remains **outside** the double-reading guard.

A capture note, and it is about process. The guard ran **three** times on July 29. It rejected the first round over a 1.00pp divergence in Renan Santos's third place, approved the second, and on the third, run at 7:50pm to check the market before the editorial close, it rejected again, this time **only** on Michelle Bolsonaro's book, which swung between 0.35% and 0.55%. Every other price repeated and enters firm; Michelle's enters as a range, with the divergence declared.

## 3. What the press covered

The day's institutional fact was born inside the PL's own convention, and its structure is what matters. On July 28, **Alexandre de Moraes gave 48 hours** for Jair Bolsonaro's defence to explain the video generated by artificial intelligence carrying the former president's image and voice, and he laid out **two mutually exclusive scenarios**: if there was authorisation, the release may amount to a fresh breach of the precautionary measures that sustain his house arrest, with a risk of return to closed custody; if there was not, the piece may amount to a deep fake barred by electoral law, with liability for Flávio Bolsonaro and the party ([Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/colunas/manoela-alcantara/moraes-da-48h-para-defesa-de-bolsonaro-esclarecer-se-autorizou-ia-na-convencao-de-flavio), [Terra](https://www.terra.com.br/noticias/brasil/politica/moraes-da-48h-para-defesa-de-bolsonaro-dizer-se-ele-autorizou-video-de-ia-da-convencao-de-flavio,87165938eca469de92ab769f21076583jllu37qo.html)).

On July 29 the defence replied that he **did not authorise** the use of his own image and voice, and added that he **does not object** to relatives producing this kind of content, a practice the filing describes as notorious and continuous, arguing further that he could not have authorised anything because his visits are suspended for thirty days and Flávio is barred from visiting him for ninety ([CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/blogs/gustavo-uribe/politica/defesa-diz-a-moraes-que-bolsonaro-nao-autorizou-uso-de-video-por-ia/), [Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/colunas/manoela-alcantara/defesa-de-bolsonaro-nega-autorizacao-para-uso-de-imagem-em-video-de-ia), [Folha de S.Paulo](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/07/bolsonaro-diz-a-moraes-que-nao-autorizou-uso-de-imagem-de-ia-em-convencao-do-pl.shtml)). ## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Price × polling, and the inverse of the previous day:** on July 28 there was no national poll and the [favourite's price](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) rose 1.00pp. On July 29 the window's largest-sample poll came out and the price **fell** 1.00pp. The market's direction matches the polling's, which also narrowed the gap, but the sizes differ: 0.6pp in the poll against 1.10pp in the market. And the same session carried the AI video case at the STF and the shift in reading on the US emergency. This brief records the coincidence of direction and does **not** claim the poll moved the price.

> **Convergence that narrowed on the right side:** with 7.8% in the polling and 8.70% in price, **Renan Santos's** distance fell to 0.90pp. What changes is not the level, it is the mechanics: in previous weeks that distance shortened because the **price** gave way, while the polling stayed at 3%; in this round it shortened because the **polling** rose, and rose in the largest-sample poll of the window. They remain different quantities, which do not subtract from one another, and convergence is proof of no one being right. The dispersion between institutes, incidentally, did not resolve: 9% at Real Time on July 21, 7.8% at AtlasIntel, 6% at PoderData/Aya on July 16, 5% at BTG/Nexus on July 27 and 3% at Datafolha, Gerp and Indexa all still stand.

> **Price rising, polling worsening:** **Caiado** rose 0.45pp in the winner contract on the same day AtlasIntel cut him in half, from 6% at BTG/Nexus on July 27 to **3.1%**, and had him losing the runoff 48.2% to 38.9%. The divergence this brief recorded on July 27 between Nexus (45% to 43%) and Datafolha (47% to 40%) gained the third measurement it was missing, and it landed on Datafolha's side. Price and polling moved in opposite directions, and both go on the record with no side chosen.

> **Institutional pile-up × price, with the signal inverted against yesterday:** on July 28 the institutional axis was dense and the [impeachment market](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) **fell** 0.60pp. On July 29 the axis was denser still, with the defence's answer to the STF, TSE justices seeing abuse, the STF preparing to act and the President of the Republic criticising corporatism in the judiciary, and the market **rose** 0.55pp, to 3.35%. Recording the direction is mandatory; building a narrative on it is not, because the whole book adds up to **USD 83,000** and moves on very little money.

## In summary

1. The window's largest-sample poll confirmed the lead and did **not** confirm an advance: against the institute's own previous round, both front-runners fall and the gap narrows 0.6pp, all inside the 1pp margin. What moves outside the margin is the government rating.
2. The day's method finding is the **mechanics** of convergence in Renan Santos, not the level. The distance fell to 0.90pp because the polling rose, and that is different from every previous week, when it fell because the price gave way.
3. The institutional axis inverted the previous day's signal: more fact, and the price rose 0.55pp instead of falling. In a book of USD 83,000, that is a recordable direction and nothing beyond it.
