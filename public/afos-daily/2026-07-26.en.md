## 1. Prediction market

At 70 days from the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), total accumulated volume in the presidential market totals approximately **USD 115.95M** ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) via AFOS proxy, capture from July 26, 2:12pm BRT approved by a double reading 8 minutes apart, plus a third check reading, and re-checked at 4:42pm with no change in the presidential race). **Lula** did not move: he stayed at **62.50% (USD 7.65M)**, the same value he closed at on July 25 and the top of the entire AFOS series. **Flávio Bolsonaro** rose 0.80pp, to **23.65% (USD 7.67M)**, and **Renan Santos** fell exactly 0.80pp, to **9.55% (USD 8.53M)**.

The detail is in the symmetry. Two names in the same camp traded the same amount of price between them while the [front-runner did not move a hundredth](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election). That describes a dispute **within** a camp, not a transfer between camps, and it is different from saying the opposition advanced. The gap between Lula and Flávio pulled back to **+38.85pp**, off the peak of +39.65pp recorded on [July 25](/en/daily/2026-07-25). The top of the series lasted one session, and this synthesis records that as clearly as it recorded the peak.

In the chasing pack, the day was one of across-the-board declines, with no exception ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election)). **Ronaldo Caiado** fell 0.15pp, to **1.75% (USD 5.14M)**, on the very day he was formalized as a candidate. **Jair Bolsonaro** fell to **1.05% (USD 5.24M)** and **Michelle Bolsonaro**, who is not a declared pre-candidate, fell 0.40pp to **0.75% (USD 9.26M)**, dropping out of the 1% range she had crossed the day before. **Romeu Zema** eased to **0.65% (USD 4.53M)** and **Fernando Haddad** gave back in full the rise of July 25, falling 0.30pp to **0.35% (USD 6.36M)**. **Tarcísio de Freitas** stayed at **0.15% (USD 13.63M)**, holding the largest individual volume in the book, a legacy anomaly that measures traded history and not current conviction.

In the sub-markets, the movement reinforced the consolidation reading. In [first-round second place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio rose 1.50pp, to **78.00% (USD 207 thousand)**, with Renan at **11.85% (USD 1.07M)**. In [third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan gave up 1.50pp, to **64.50% (USD 157 thousand)**, and Caiado gave up 0.50pp, to **15.50% (USD 36 thousand)**, in a book of **USD 476 thousand**. In the [Senate seats market](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the **PL** swung between 70.00% and **69.00% (USD 258 thousand)** within the capture window itself, a move of 1.00pp in thirteen minutes in a book adding up to **USD 292 thousand**; that instability is, in itself, the argument for not building a reading on this market. The market on the [impeachment of a STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) fell 0.10pp, to **3.40% (USD 83 thousand)**, the third session effectively flat. In the [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the modal band remains 5.00% to 5.49%, at **39.20% (USD 9 thousand)**, followed by the 4.50% to 4.99% band at 26.20%, in a book of **USD 77 thousand**, a centre of distribution that remains above the target ceiling.

## 2. What the pollsters registered

**No national poll was published on July 26.** The [Datafolha released on the evening of July 24](/en/daily/2026-07-25) remains the heaviest read in the set: 40% for Lula and 32% for Flávio Bolsonaro in the first round, 48% to 43% in the head-to-head [runoff](/en/glossary#segundo-turno), n=2,004, fieldwork July 22 to 24, a 2pp margin and filing BR-01166/2026 with the [TSE](/en/glossary#tse). A reference to a "new PoderData poll" circulated on July 26; checking the filing and the institute's own aggregator shows it is the round released on **July 16**, with n=2,400 and fieldwork July 12 to 15, already incorporated into this panel. It is not a new measurement.

The absence of new polling matters because the weekend had two campaign events and a diplomatic incident, and none of them was measured. According to the [public TSE register](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), the first poll with fieldwork entirely after the PL convention only arrives the following week, and that is precisely when the market's current level goes into testing.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Calendar × price:** the chasing pack had its fullest day of scheduled events and its worst price performance. A candidate was formally launched and **every** name in the group retreated in the same session, including [Caiado](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) himself, who also lost half a point in the third place market. The money that left the pack and Renan Santos showed up concentrated in a single name.

> **Launch × market, second occurrence:** on July 25 the PL formalized Flávio and his price fell 0.10pp; on July 26 the PSD formalized Caiado and his price fell 0.15pp. Two occurrences do not make a pattern, the books are small and the capture is from a single instant, so this synthesis records the repetition and does **not** assert a cause. Worth tracking across the conventions running through August 5.

> **Movement × direction:** the largest rise of the day was Flávio's, but it has an exact counterpart in Renan Santos' fall and it happens with [Lula motionless](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election). There was no advance on the front-runner, there was a choice of name within the same camp.

> **Institutional × price:** within 48 hours a foreign head of state attacked a STF justice by name from a Brazilian platform, the president of the Court replied in public and an ambassador was summoned for consultations. The [market on the impeachment of a justice](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stood at 3.40%, effectively flat for the third session, in a book of USD 83 thousand that supports no narrative.

## In summary

1. The [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) did not change its mind about who wins and spent July 26 deciding who loses better: Lula flat at **62.50%**, with all the movement between the second and third placed names.
2. The [PSD formalized Caiado with Kassab](https://g1.globo.com/politica/eleicoes/2026/noticia/2026/07/26/psd-lanca-candidatura-de-ronaldo-caiado-a-presidencia-da-republica.ghtml), the party's first own presidential candidacy, and his price fell in the same session. It is the second time in a row that a launch ends this way.
3. No [new polling](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) tested any of that. Four national surveys have publication declared between July 27 and July 31, three of them with fieldwork open on July 26.
