## 1. Prediction market

**The winner contract became a pair.** In the confirmed reading of October 5 at 17:31 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 83.10% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 16.98M) and 16.50% for Lula (USD 17.91M). Against the confirmed reading of October 4 at 21:43 BRT, published on the night of the count, Flávio Bolsonaro falls 1.15pp and Lula rises 1.00pp, with 1.03pp to each side in the normalized move, and the gap goes from 68.75pp to 66.60pp. Total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 173.39M in the 18:17 BRT reading. After the first round, Polymarket closed the contracts of the other names, resolved at zero, and counting the closed ones the largest volume in the book is now Lula's, ahead of Renan Santos's, which closed with USD 17.12M.

**Three books resolved.** The [first round second place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) book resolved to Lula, the [third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) one to Augusto Cury and the [party with the most Senate seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won) one to the PL, with 19 of the 54 seats. On the eve, at 23:00 UTC on October 3, the second place book paid 57.50% for Flávio Bolsonaro finishing second, and the third place one paid 47.50% for Renan Santos and 23.70% for Augusto Cury.

**Supreme Court and inflation.** The [Supreme Court justice impeachment by 2027 contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 2.15% (USD 0.09M), 1.25pp below the reading of the night of the count. In the [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) one, the 5.00% to 5.49% band leads with 49.80% (USD 24 thousand), in the 18:18 BRT reading.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × ballot box:** the winner contract pays 83.10% for Flávio Bolsonaro, and the first round count left him 1.87 points ahead. Price is not vote intention: the first measures the probability of winning on October 25, the second is the share of votes of October 4.

> **Poll × ballot box:** five of the nine national polls of the eve put Lula ahead in the first round, and the count gave Flávio Bolsonaro the lead. The range of the nine contained the result; most of them did not.

> **Side books × ballot box:** on the eve, the second place book paid more for Flávio Bolsonaro in second and the third place one more for Renan Santos. The count gave Lula in second and Augusto Cury in third.

## In summary

1. **The winner contract became a pair**, with Flávio Bolsonaro at 83.10% and Lula at 16.50%, and the contracts of the eliminated candidates closed at zero.
2. **On the first day**, Renan Santos backs no one, the PSD stays neutral and Ronaldo Caiado promises to announce support for Flávio Bolsonaro on October 6.
3. **The first national polls of the second round** come out from October 8 to 10, and the dashboard now measures the Lula × Flávio Bolsonaro pair.
