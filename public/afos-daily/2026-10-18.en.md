## 1. Prediction market

**The gap widens by 5.30 points.** In the confirmed reading of October 18 at 17:44 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 79.25% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 22.57M) and 20.50% for Lula (USD 26.35M). Against the confirmed reading of October 17 at 16:59 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro rises 2.30pp and Lula falls 3.00pp; since the pair's sum went from 100.45 to 99.75, the normalized move is 2.84pp for each side. The gap goes from 53.45pp to 58.75pp, below the 72.95pp of October 13. Total cumulative volume in the presidential market comes to USD 187.29M, and the largest volume in the book is still Lula's.

**Supreme Court and inflation.** The contract on the [impeachment of an STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 1.50% (USD 0.10M), 1.20pp below the October 17 reading. In the [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) contract, in the 17:36 BRT reading against the October 17 reading at 17:25 BRT, the 5.00% to 5.49% band goes from 60.15% to 70.05% (USD 28 thousand), the 5.50% to 5.99% band from 16.35% to 14.90% (USD 21 thousand) and the 4.50% to 4.99% band from 16.70% to 13.25% (USD 32 thousand).
