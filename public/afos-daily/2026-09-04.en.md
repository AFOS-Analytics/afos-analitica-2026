## 1. Prediction market

Total accumulated volume in the presidential book is USD 143.62M, in a [confirmed reading](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) of 4 September, 19:53 BRT. Lula rose 1.00pp to **55.50% (USD 9.72M)** and Flávio Bolsonaro gave up 2.70pp to **40.15% (USD 9.68M)**, the largest move of any name in the book in this reading.

The gap between the two went from 11.65pp to **15.35pp**, an opening of 3.70pp that undoes four consecutive readings of narrowing since 1 September. In the same [book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Augusto Cury rose 0.70pp to **1.95% (USD 4.07M)**, with around USD 380 thousand of new volume in one day, and Renan Santos rose 0.10pp to **1.85% (USD 12.64M)**, still the largest accumulated volume among the four names above 1%.

In the side markets the picture is different, and it contradicts the main one. In the [first-round runner-up contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio rose 4.00pp to **87.50% (USD 586 thousand)**, over the same interval in which he was giving ground in the winner contract, and Lula gave up 2.90pp to **7.60% (USD 469 thousand)**. In the [third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Cury gave up 5.95pp to **53.55% (USD 123 thousand)**, breaking three straight days of gains, Renan rose 1.50pp to **23.50% (USD 277 thousand)** and Ronaldo Caiado gave up 1.00pp to **9.00% (USD 114 thousand)**.

The [Supreme Court justice impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at **15.85% (USD 87 thousand)**, against 7.40% in the reading of 3 September at 17:28. It is the only contract on the panel going out without the double reading applied to the others, and the reason is in the recorded series itself: across the recorded readings of 4 September it ranged from 10.60% to 19.30%, an amplitude of 8.70pp, with USD 28 of new money over the whole day. The figure is one reading inside that range, not its centre. In the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the Liberal Party remains at **82.50% (USD 261 thousand)** and the MDB at **12.25% (USD 8.9 thousand)**.

## 3. What the press covered

The day's act comes from the Supreme Court, and the scope is narrow. Edson Fachin [removed from the fake-news inquiry](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/fachin-tira-pedido-de-investigacao-contra-mendonca-do-inquerito-das-fake-news.shtml) the request by Alexandre de Moraes to investigate André Mendonça and turned it into a separate petition under his own docket, with [five working days for the Prosecutor General's Office](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/fachin-da-5-dias-para-pgr-se-manifestar-sobre-acusacoes-de-moraes-contra-mendonca/) and another five for Mendonça afterwards. The five-day deadline given to Moraes, Mendonça, Paulo Gonet and Andrei Rodrigues is from 3 September, not 4, as the [chronology of the case](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/entenda-a-cronologia-da-crise-no-stf-com-embate-entre-moraes-e-mendonca/) shows.

Hours later, Fachin [argued for ending the fake-news inquiry](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/fachin-diz-que-nao-havera-omissao-sobre-crise-no-stf-e-defende-fim-do-inquerito-das-fake-news/), open for seven years, without closing it. The Superior Council of the Federal Prosecution Service [opened proceedings to examine the references to Gonet](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/04/conselho-superior-do-mpf-abre-procedimento-para-analisar-citacoes-a-gonet-em-mensagens-atribuidas-a-vorcaro.ghtml) in the messages attributed to Daniel Vorcaro, in the council the Prosecutor General himself chairs. Flávio Dino [acknowledged the crisis and argued for continuing to judge](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/dino-reconhece-crise-no-stf-e-defende-seguir-julgando/).

On the campaign trail, Flávio Bolsonaro called Fachin's decision ["light at the end of the tunnel"](https://valor.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/09/04/flavio-diz-que-decisao-de-fachin-em-crise-do-stf-e-luz-no-fim-do-tunel-e-rebate-indireta-de-alcolumbre.ghtml) and said in São Paulo that [Davi Alcolumbre and Moraes are seeking self-protection](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMitwFBVV95cUxNTDBsNC1EQlk5RkplQlBHRjZwUGYyRVh0bjFvaWZScGV4QVV2TWNVOVJQQzlEZG1TOW5oTVVOODBTdEdPMHBaY3pxcEtqWnZEbG1hWTdvelFUMjBiS1JOOFJ4TlFyU19obGRxbHQ1aHF3anFubWVTTTVNUWRyVzNJckR6NndWcUFPanU0a2xLaTJiM0E3U0JGUVU0dzR0SDF4dUN5dXZlczB4NV9Jb1pWbnpzSkYwQXc?oc=5). Lula [said he told Donald Trump that Brazil does not accept interference in its elections](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiwgFBVV95cUxOcVVvYTRHRXRKSTByN1I5Q0ppRktjYlhZeUROLWdOem1ybG83N1p1Tm9pU1ZLTkNrekNfWFcwOUFxcE01WkxFY3NjSzdUQ3NCb2ZfV1VBdzAtOWpnYmdLQ2NOd1VZM0EzMDZHa0lEdGIzUnlHbXVfWHd6RU1DZWFaNURJaWlKUTBRYk9ocW1rdURvX3ZBQkhKajg5a1owYUU5cHBVMmtoUHVUMnZYNXBhTTBPZ3NhUDNlR3Vqb0htX1g4dw?oc=5), and Ronaldo Caiado argued, at a question-and-answer session, for [investigating the alleged Vorcaro bribe](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/caiado-diz-defender-investigacao-para-apurar-suposta-propina-de-vorcaro-articulada-por-kassab.shtml).

In a rejected plea-bargain proposal, from 3 September, Vorcaro claimed that the [donation to Jair Bolsonaro and to Tarcisio de Freitas was a bribe arranged by Gilberto Kassab](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/em-delacao-rejeitada-vorcaro-diz-que-doacao-a-bolsonaro-e-tarcisio-era-propina-articulada-por-kassab.shtml). Tarcísio called the claim ["an inference bordering on the ridiculous"](https://oglobo.globo.com/brasil/noticia/2026/09/04/e-uma-ilacao-que-beira-o-ridiculo-diz-tarcisio-sobre-delacao-de-vorcaro-que-aponta-propina-em-doacoes-de-2022.ghtml). The [electoral effect of the case remains disputed](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi4AFBVV95cUxOMFRlZmRac0VOX1pLbjAwZWc4WGxLaklFNkFlUTI1dm1SMnAtNVQ2Rk1KZDdZaGZOSnAyYkhaVDBnUXRoX2tZcnQ4dnM4SWVkaWJLOUZ5Q21KakVDQXN6bHJWZ3BDNDV0cUhmLU5UUDUtWU1aVWR4TE5xdEFMdDNTZlhxQlo2T1RfdlZ0aFFMc2NHRXU1ZGVGRkhsVlBYTkNCcVlSeERtMmdyWGpSVW9WQ0ZqSUYtdzlFOUlEV2lQWGdKZml0cG05blB0WXhaMWFuZzhNQlZmSkt1eU95TXdfTA?oc=5), with no measurement published on 4 September.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x calendar:** on 3 September [three national polls](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqAFBVV95cUxPeXZpa2lUb1BzdFRRY1BnWnpXVlJSOWlhUnBWNlN4dldyMlhsVjM5dGdrU1VjWGxubGZnaHJCSWJKc3l2ZnAwb0xwUkZaY1RyZmhzZWo1RkI2OVBXSU9TZUlEZXBGc3FKcXhaa0NmOXAzcjlXUHNXbHZoYzNCZVIwY1ZXakZYT2J5XzduUFFMT0xRbUVUX1RwOHZkNWRuVUwwVkJDZjcza0U?oc=5) came out within 48 hours and the leader's price sat still for more than eight hours. On 4 September none came out, and the gap opened 3.70pp. The panel records the coincidence of dates and assigns no cause: there is no measurement published on this day either to support or to contradict the move.

> **Contract x contract:** Flávio Bolsonaro gave up 2.70pp in the winner contract and rose 4.00pp in the [runner-up one](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), over the same interval. Augusto Cury did the reverse, up 0.70pp in the winner contract and down 5.95pp in the [third-place one](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place). They are distinct markets on the same names, and in this reading they do not tell the same story.

> **Price x money:** the [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) ranged 8.70pp across the day's recorded readings on USD 28 of new money. It is the panel's smallest book, and the order of magnitude of the move bears no relation to that of the volume that produced it.

## In summary

1. **The day without a poll was the day the [price moved most](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election).** The gap is back to 15.35pp and undid four consecutive readings of narrowing, with no new voting-intention information entering the market.
2. **Seven national polls are in the field according to the [TSE register](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) and none publishes before 6 September.** The largest sample is PALVER, with 5,000 interviews, scheduled for 9 September.
3. **What changed at the Supreme Court is narrower than the headline suggests:** Fachin [split off the request](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/fachin-tira-pedido-de-investigacao-contra-mendonca-do-inquerito-das-fake-news.shtml) by Moraes against Mendonça and argued for ending the fake-news inquiry, without closing it.
