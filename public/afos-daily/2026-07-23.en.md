## 1. Prediction market

73 days from the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), the presidential market registered its largest shift in weeks, with total accumulated volume reaching approximately **USD 115.3M** ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) via AFOS proxy, fetched July 23, 18:12 BRT). **Lula** remains isolated favorite at **60.50% (USD 7.61M)**, flat for the second consecutive day. **Flávio Bolsonaro** fell to **22.85% (USD 7.62M)**, and the gap between the two widened to **+37.65pp**, the largest since July 11, still below the cycle peak of +39.5pp recorded on July 3. Precision reading is warranted: the gap grew 2.10pp without Lula gaining a single point.

The money that left Flávio went to the third-way candidates, not to the favorite. **Renan Santos** rose to **11.85% (USD 8.34M)**, fifth consecutive day of gains, and **Ronaldo Caiado** to **2.05% (USD 5.10M)**, the second largest gain in the book. **Romeu Zema**, at **0.45% (USD 4.49M)**, and **Fernando Haddad**, at **0.35% (USD 6.33M)**, remained outside the rotation, a sign that the market separates the field rather than treating it as a bloc. **Michelle Bolsonaro**, who is not a declared pre-candidate, marks **0.95% (USD 9.17M)**, and **Tarcísio de Freitas**, **0.15% (USD 13.55M)**, the largest individual volume in the book, a legacy anomaly that measures negotiated history, not current conviction ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election)).

The strongest movement of the day occurred in the sub-markets. Flávio fell from 78.00% to **70.50%** for 2nd place in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), a drop of 7.50pp, with Renan at 17.40%. In 3rd place, Renan rose to **68.00%**, with Caiado stable at 16.50%. The [impeachment of STF minister before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) market remained flat at **2.95% (USD 83 thousand)**, unmoved on the day a Supreme Court justice authorized an inquiry involving the financial circle of the main opposition candidate. With a volume base orders of magnitude below the presidential market, both the variation and the absence of it there should be treated as noise.

In [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) fell from 75.00% to **72.00% (USD 257 thousand)**, with [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) rising to 20.90%. In [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the modal band remains 5.00% to 5.49%, at 38.65%, followed by the 4.50% to 4.99% band, at 26.35%, a distribution center that remains above the target ceiling.

## 2. What polling institutes recorded

No national voting intention poll was published on July 23. The most recent poll remains [Gerp/AESP of July 22](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/eleicoes/2026/pesquisa-eleitoral-2026/gerp-presidente-julho-2026-2/), which showed a 38% to 38% tie in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) and Flávio numerically ahead in the direct [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) by 46% to 45%, with TSE registration BR-05026/2026. Method note is warranted: many articles circulated about a new [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) measuring the tariff effect, but the poll **was not released**. The new opinion data came from PoderData, published on July 22, which does not require TSE registration as it measures approval, not voting intention. With fieldwork from July 18 to 20, 2,500 interviews by automated telephone system across 147 municipalities and 2-point margin, the institute measured Lula's personal disapproval falling from 50% to **47%**, against 46% approval, and job evaluation as poor or very poor receding from 47% to **37%** ([CNN Brasil](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMikwFBVV95cUxOUGVSdU51WDJWUVUwc3o5MjMtd1hJamQ5UHBEcHV2VDl3ejk5MHl6Q3l5b01qN1JNMjRRVDNGM0RYanVnSDhUWmhCYU1MWi10SXVRcVlWVW81TV9qUmNva1lGWDdRUGhzaklHMEdCUUVScVJIeHV4QXY1N0N2cWtaa1ZxQTdEbDBVWFY0MHo4XzVQdXM?oc=5)). It is the most favorable reading for Lula in weeks, with a caveat that the survey itself imposes: government evaluation remains disapproved by 51% against 43%, so the improvement is in personal approval, not management. On the other side of the race, CartaCapital highlighted a reading about [lack of conviction among Flávio's voters](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiugFBVV95cUxQMVdHWGNqWXpnbjUwNWxqMUhyVlVmeVRLcXFxU0YxTUQ0UFRqV19QQXlSa2RXTHI4bTJjY2dTalEzamVXWmlzaExlTUNiVlMtbmFJUGlqbWRKVWlhdi1ZWDRMZXA1X3NZTlg4eUNXd3NwV3FQaHRQQ3V4U3Z4eVVhazdmaWFycnNFWEhDRmhEU2pYOEdOZ0ZNRGtwNXBJTkVoUEdOVEw1b2RTTWQtbEZkZnM2MURaYmtyckE?oc=5).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) pays 11.85% for Renan Santos, a level above even the 9% from Real Time on July 21, which is the highest poll reading he has received in the cycle. As Gerp and Indexa measure him at 3%, until July 23 there is not a single poll that supports the price. The distortion widened for the third consecutive time without any new poll having been published.

> **Price × series:** the same rise requires the caveat that history imposes. The 11.85% is not a record: Renan was worth 17.9% on June 9 and 12.8% on July 1, and fell to a floor of 7.8% on July 17. It is recovery from a recent floor, not market discovery. Treating the movement as unprecedented would confuse recent variation with historical maximum.

> **News × price:** Flávio retreated 2.10pp in the winner position and 7.50pp in second place on the same date that the authorization of the inquiry was reported. AFOS Analytics registers the simultaneity and does not assert causality: a market does not declare a reason, and a single trading day does not distinguish reaction to news from accumulated momentum. In prior rounds, developments in the Master case did not move price; this time there was a large movement and a new procedural act on the same date. That is registered correlation, not demonstrated cause.

## In summary

1. The day had no national poll of voting intention and yet produced the largest price displacement in weeks. When the polls do not speak, the movement is internal market repositioning, and this recommends caution with causal readings.
2. Lula's gap over Flávio reached +37.65pp, the largest since July 11, entirely due to the opponent's decline. Three weeks of narrowing were reversed in six days without the frontrunner gaining a single point.
3. The [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) of July 24 and the [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) of July 28 are the next polling tests, and the Renan case arrives at them with the price above any poll reading already recorded in the cycle.-
