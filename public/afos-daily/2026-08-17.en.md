## 1. Prediction market

The [presidential contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) closed the confirmed 6:48 PM BRT reading with the leader at **64.50% (USD 8.52M)**, a 2.00pp drop that gave back what he had gained the day before, and the runner-up at **31.45% (USD 8.46M)**, a 2.00pp rise on his fourth straight day. The distance shrank from 37.05pp to **33.05pp**, 4.00pp in about 26 hours, and the accumulated total volume in the presidential book comes to **USD 126.85M**.

Neither is a record: the leader's high is 66.50%, from Aug 1, and the runner-up's 33.20%, from Jun 2.

In the [pack](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Renan Santos fell to **4.05% (USD 9.95M)** and Caiado to **0.25% (USD 6.07M)**, both below the floor of the 90-day series. Zema rose 0.10pp and Pablo Marçal enters the comparison for the first time, at **0.90% (USD 1.21M)**.

In the [third place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Caiado rose to **38.50%** and Renan gave ground to **52.50%**. The [Supreme Court impeachment market](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stayed flat at **3.40% (USD 84 thousand)** and the PL marks **77.00%** in the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), against 16.85% for the MDB.

**After the capture the market moved again, and the guard blocked on the two following runs.** That is why the numbers above are the 6:48 PM ones, and not the most recent.

## 2. What the pollsters recorded

The first national poll since Aug 14 came out. **[BTG/Nexus](https://www.nexus.fsb.com.br/estudos-divulgados/pesquisa-btg-nexus-de-intencao-de-votos-para-presidente-do-brasil-17-de-agosto-de-2026/)** surveyed 2,003 voters by telephone between Aug 14 and 16, with a 2-point margin, TSE registration BR-03317/2026, checked on the pollster's own site: 41% x 36% in the first round, with Caiado at 5%, Renan Santos at 4% and Zema at 4%, and 47% x 44% in the runoff.

Against the house's own previous round, **the distance stayed at the same 5 points** and the runoff repeated 47 to 44. On the government assessment, 42% rate it bad or terrible against 34% excellent or good, according to [Diário do Grande ABC](https://www.dgabc.com.br/Noticia/4341526/btg-nexus-para-42-governo-lula-e-ruim-ou-pessimo-34-consideram-otimo-ou-bom-e-23-regular).

The panel **does not publish** the approval figure from this round: outlets diverged between 47 x 48 and 46 x 49, and the pollster did not carry the data.

## 3. What the press covered

The Federal Police seized in Asunción an aircraft worth R$ 120 million linked to former banker Daniel Vorcaro, within Operação Compliance Zero, and the aircraft is already impounded in Brasília, according to [Jornal de Brasília](https://jornaldebrasilia.com.br/noticias/politica-e-poder/pf-apreende-no-paraguai-aviao-de-vorcaro/) and [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/blogs/elijonasmaia/politica/pf-localiza-e-aprende-aviao-de-vorcaro-no-paraguai/). Seizing an asset is a precautionary measure, not a ruling on the merits.

Pablo Marçal entered the electoral news, and the panel records the declared status: the PRTB filed his registration on Aug 15, the final deadline; on Aug 5 the São Paulo regional electoral court denied his defence's appeal and he **remains ineligible until 2032**, with an appeal available to the higher electoral court; and the law allows a candidacy to stand **sub judice**, with campaign acts until a definitive ruling, according to [BBC News Brasil](https://www.bbc.com/portuguese) and [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/).

There was friction on the right: the runner-up accused Caiado of helping the leader after Gilberto Kassab, the running mate on Caiado's ticket, said he has zero chance, and Kassab replied that the runner-up's campaign is worried about Caiado, according to [Estadão](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/). And the United States is weighing new sanctions on Justice Alexandre de Moraes, according to the Financial Times as republished by [Valor Econômico](https://valor.globo.com/politica/).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** the poll shortened nothing and the price shortened 4.00pp. The leader went from 40% to 41% and the runner-up from 35% to 36%, so the measured distance stayed at the same 5 points. The panel does **not** claim the price reacted to the poll, and the reason is arithmetic: a measurement that does not shorten cannot explain a shortening.

> **Poll × market, on who is third:** [BTG/Nexus](https://www.nexus.fsb.com.br/estudos-divulgados/pesquisa-btg-nexus-de-intencao-de-votos-para-presidente-do-brasil-17-de-agosto-de-2026/) puts Caiado at 5% and Renan Santos at 4%. The market does the opposite, with Renan at 4.05% against Caiado's 0.25%, and 52.50% against 38.50% in the third place contract. The disagreement has been narrowing: that book was 15.50pp apart on Aug 16 and is 14.00pp apart now.

> **Price × price:** Caiado fell in the [contract on winning](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) and rose in the one on finishing third, on the same day. Adding the two together would produce a number with no meaning.

## Em síntese

1. **The poll measured stability and the price measured shortening, and the difference between the two readings is 4.00pp.** BTG/Nexus kept the distance at 5 points and repeated 47 to 44 in the runoff; on the same day the price went from 37.05pp to 33.05pp. Both 1-point moves in the poll fit inside the house's own 2-point margin, so they are not even distinguishable from stability.
2. **The two universes now disagree about who the third name is, and the disagreement is shrinking.** The ballot box puts Caiado at 5% against Renan Santos's 4%. The market does the opposite, with 4.05% against 0.25% on winning and 52.50% against 38.50% on placing, and that last distance fell from 15.50pp to 14.00pp in a single day.
3. **The guard blocked twice and the panel preferred the old number to the new one.** Between 9:30 PM and 10:18 PM the runner-up was read between 31.65% and 32.95% without two samples agreeing. A price that does not clear two runs becomes recorded instability, not a published quote.
