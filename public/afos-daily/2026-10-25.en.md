## 1. Prediction market

**The contract flips sides.** At the confirmed reading of October 25 at 21:22 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 99.85% for Lula (USD 42.96M) and 0.15% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 56.18M). Against the confirmed reading of October 24 at 20:06 BRT, the one in the previous daily, Lula rises 83.35pp and Flávio Bolsonaro falls 83.60pp; since the pair's sum went from 100.25 to 100.00, the normalized move is 83.39pp for each side. The gap goes from 67.25pp in Flávio Bolsonaro's favor to 99.70pp in Lula's favor.

**The volume.** The total volume accumulated adds up to USD 241.98M at the 21:31 BRT reading, across the 32 contracts of the [presidential book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election). The contract with the highest volume is now Flávio Bolsonaro's, with USD 56.18M, ahead of Lula's, with USD 42.96M, which led the ranking on the eve. The second-place, third-place and Senate books have been resolved since the first round.

**Supreme Court and inflation.** The [STF justice impeachment by 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) contract remains at 2.80% (USD 0.10M), at the confirmed reading of October 25 at 13:39 BRT; this update publishes no new price for it. In the [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) book, at the 21:31 BRT reading, the 5.00% to 5.49% band stands at 49.45% (USD 29.9 thousand), 13.45pp below the October 24 reading, the 5.50% to 5.99% band at 33.85% (USD 23.7 thousand) and the 4.50% to 4.99% band at 12.25% (USD 34.6 thousand).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × ballot box:** on the eve, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) paid 83.75% for Flávio Bolsonaro, and the ballot box gave 51.76% of the valid votes to Lula. Lula stood at 16.50%, which the market read as roughly a one-in-six chance.

> **Polls × ballot box:** fifty of the fifty-five national polls [registered with the TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) conducted after the first round had Flávio Bolsonaro ahead, and in the nine of October 24 in which he led the margin ranged from 0.3 to 3 points in total votes; in valid votes, the [ballot box](https://resultados.tse.jus.br/oficial/ele2026/6258/dados/br/br-c0001-e006258-u.json) gave Lula 51.76%, against 48.44% in Veritá, 49.9% in Futura/100% Cidades and 49% in PoderData/Aya.

> **Poll × poll:** [Colectta](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) of October 24, which had Lula ahead by 46.5% to 46% in total votes and 50.3% of the valid votes, was one of the four national polls with Lula in front, alongside the three Vox Brasil polls.

## In summary

1. **Lula is re-elected with 51.76% of the valid votes**, in the [official TSE partial count](https://resultados.tse.jus.br/oficial/ele2026/6258/dados/br/br-c0001-e006258-u.json) of 21:25 BRT, with 99.86% of sections, after finishing the first round behind.
2. **The [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) takes Lula from 16.50% to 99.85%**, and the highest volume in the book is now Flávio Bolsonaro's, USD 56.18M.
3. **On the eve, fifty of fifty-five national polls and the [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) had Flávio Bolsonaro ahead.**
