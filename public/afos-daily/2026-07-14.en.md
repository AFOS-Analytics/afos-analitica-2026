## 1. Prediction market

The [presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) closed the fifth consecutive day with **Lula at 60.50%** (USD 7.34M), exactly the same figure as July 9. He did not move with the Gerp from July 8, did not move with the BTG/Nexus from July 13, and did not move with the Futura/Apex from July 14. Three consecutive national polls pointing to tightening in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno), and zero cents of price reaction.

**Flávio Bolsonaro** was the one who moved: he rose to **24.85%** (↑1.00pp, USD 7.38M), the only relevant variation in the presidential race on the day. With this the gap over him fell to **+35.65pp** (↓1.00pp), the tightest in two weeks. The distinction matters: **the gap narrowed because Flávio rose, not because Lula gave ground.** He also expanded his favoritism in the [sub-market for second place of first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), to **84.50%** (↑1.00pp, USD 180 thousand).

In the third way, **Renan Santos** rose slightly to **10.15%** (↑0.10pp, USD 8.09M, the largest individual volume among living contenders) and expanded the [third place of first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) to **71.00%** (↑0.50pp, USD 140 thousand). **Ronaldo Caiado** fell to **1.35%** (↓0.10pp, USD 4.86M) and **Romeu Zema** stayed at **0.85%** (USD 4.35M).

The most curious movement was in family succession, and it went backward. In the [same market as winner](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), **Michelle Bolsonaro** gave back the jump from July 13 and fell to **1.85%** (↓0.40pp, USD 8.96M), while **Jair Bolsonaro** fell **by half**, from 1.05% to **0.55%**. The market repriced the hypothesis on July 13, after Moraes' decision, and unwound the bet in 24 hours. It was a reaction, not a trend. The **total accumulated volume in the presidential race totals USD 112.8M**.

In the institutional races, nothing. The [impeachment contract for STF minister](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) remains pinned to the floor at **2.75%** (USD 82 thousand), for the second day, even with Flávio intensifying attacks on the Court. In the [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) remains leader with **86.50%** (USD 251 thousand). In the [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the bands of 4.50% to 5.49% sum **62.40%** (5.00-5.49% at 35.75%; 4.50-4.99% at 26.65%), with the tail of 7% or more at the floor, at 3.60%.

## 2. What the institutes recorded

**Futura/Apex** (Futura Inteligência) published on July 14 the second national of the week, and it is the hardest data of the cycle so far. Sample of 2,000 voters, field from July 7 to 11, margin of 2.2 points, telephone interviews, universe of the Brazilian electorate aged 16 or older, protocol [BR-07294/2026](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/). In the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno), **Lula 46.3% × Flávio 46.1%**: **two tenths of a point**, the tightest [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) ever measured in this election ([Exame](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMimgFBVV95cUxQYS1lakhwR3l3RGRwRVg3UlRlVFZZSi1OYmxWVGQ0UHhfdHdIa2FTSTIzUVlrQm8zcUV2anVQLTNON19hd083SU80dmNWdkZuRlJtVGZNeUhPMC0wWG9YX1lrQmNDZUlONW00eDk5V2ViM21QUm9MSnlITjhBR2dMWHNvbWJTSVp5ME43Nl9SdWlkQTY1R25DanhR?oc=5), [Gazeta do Povo](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMisgFBVV95cUxNeW5QVDZqdU8tVjllaFpWMjZwV3EtM0tYUW5oUEItaC1tQzNoX2haRGRmNkxQY0xqSkJRSmtZblh2QUQ5M0VaMmJrZFVTamNXWDZQa1o2ZHBhSmhxbGpLTWpjdVVxSEpyS05ZYkhpNm1nenFad0d2MXBndElHa1NLYjNQdVVrd09IaVpiXy1xSEtsaHBMMS1BTzdlcHFMdDd3NkVvX3lPUEJQQWcycnZqcjVR?oc=5), [Jovem Pan](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi7gFBVV95cUxNOFJwVnJydjJmX0NUM1U0S3BDUXhOSHFJM2gyUFV5MUZqSFBsM3ZyOG5CeDdzeHp3ZjlXclkxSE1DWGRGZ3pKNi1uTVpWRkJvWEN3S21kSnR0dFl6R1VFeHVnWnhGUjEtck02VnlzWHNkaUVnQUo5TUNaQkdjRUN0bXkycjY0QXZnaFNYYmp4YmRPek5Fc1AxbFBtdlpLRW1TX2FyVTM2SmozVGJrTnVKbmc5QWVKcUhyaGZIR2c2N01HRmJhMF82cmkxMS1jdngtN3BnOFZpN2xWY1d3dzBFYUVrenBLcXNyZTh4cWxn?oc=5)). In the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) stimulated, **Lula 40.1% × Flávio 36.8%**, with confidence intervals overlapping. In the third way, **Caiado 5.0%**, **Zema 3.7%** and **Renan Santos 2.6%**.

The weight of this poll lies in the sequence, not in the isolated number. On July 12, in incorporating the [Gerp of July 08](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), the mandatory caveat was to treat it as an institute of smaller size and known outlier. On July 13 the [BTG/Nexus](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) pointed in the same direction, coming from the first rank. Now Futura/Apex brings the runoff to near perfect tie. **The caveat is over:** three institutes, three methodologies, one direction. The range of the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) today goes from a 3pp loss (Gerp) to a 6.5pp win ([AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) from July 1), passing through two ties.

A less cited number deserves attention. Futura/Apex inverts the order of the third way in relation to the market: **Zema (3.7%) appears ahead of Renan Santos (2.6%)**. In the [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Zema is worth 0.85% and Renan is worth 10.15%.

## 3. Press Coverage

The [TSE](/en/glossary#tse) became the main focus, and the subject is the instrument itself. On July 14, Justice **Kássio Nunes Marques** presented, in a meeting with directors from 19 institutes, a proposal for an **accuracy seal** for research companies: the distinction would be awarded after the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) and would evaluate only surveys from the seven days prior to the election and exit polls actually released, excluding companies sanctioned for serious irregularities. The meeting was called after he suspended, in June, an [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) survey on suspicion of voter manipulation. The institutes have until July 17 to submit contributions, and the sector reacted: experts noted that surveys measure voting intent at a given moment and **do not function as ballot box forecasts** ([JOTA](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMitAFBVV95cUxPejhsd3VsVi04R1gzZ0lTZC0wUGFtb216amw4UzZHYjhuMDV4WHVEZ0FOejYtMDJVdDJfVk1TcW1NaTBpZ1JqSWZVM0FVdUczTVAyc3RzSUpXSHRXdTB0RkFUM1UwZHl6RUc5VHlydkRxNlQzMUgzZlFOWXg0Q29qTzhfSk44OWpldklfeDYxX09aenhySjdKS0tiTnRBV2lBTXZNRHF3cDVUYlNyVkh6QTZBVGY?oc=5)).

In the opposition camp, the fallout from the July 13 decision dominated. After the 90-day suspension of his visits to Jair Bolsonaro, **Flávio changed strategy and intensified attacks on the [STF](/en/glossary#stf)**, seeking to reinforce the persecution framing three months before the election ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/07/14/flavio-muda-estrategia-apos-veto-de-visitas-a-bolsonaro-e-deve-ampliar-ataques-ao-stf-para-reforcar-tom-de-perseguicao-a-tres-meses-da-eleicao.ghtml)). **Romeu Zema** classified the decision as political persecution ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/07/14/zema-classifica-como-perseguicao-politica-decisao-de-moraes-que-suspendeu-visitas-de-flavio-a-bolsonaro.ghtml)). In an interview with [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/07/14/entrevista-campanha-de-flavio-e-muito-turbulenta-e-precisa-prestar-contas-sobre-dark-horse-diz-mourao.ghtml), Vice-President **Hamilton Mourão** said that Flávio's campaign is turbulent and that he needs to account for the Dark Horse, while also criticizing the wing linked to Eduardo Bolsonaro and Paulo Figueiredo. The statement is from a single source and is reproduced here as such.

**Ronaldo Caiado** raised the tone to establish himself as an alternative to Flávio ([CNN Brasil](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMisgFBVV95cUxOYkg4cndvQ0w0ZV9oZE1MVnJWWS1iemtqclpUdkVGNlBicHo0QkhxMXRpOFFqZ0Zadmc3SFZMSWp2OWNoVzZZVDFfOW1UNy12a3hVbnFpSWh1OWZZbFNsWVJKOHpHNTVWbE4teUtXQUNIY3hadHVCSE4yX05SVm0wT1pFRHV6Zy04QXg3eU1kREV2TVlmY3Q2Q0ViV250eEphU3lxZmNTT0l5cUVYNkxGcDhB?oc=5)), and the timing is consistent with what Futura/Apex measures: he is the best of the third way at the ballot box in the second consecutive survey. On the government side, **Lula secured endorsements in 25 states and the Federal District**, with local sacrifices, while Flávio faces resistance in the same movement ([JOTA](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi2gFBVV95cUxQMVhvUzJMYkNNR0RaX2plcEdUWEdoVmd6LWlIVnhvYl8td1REdUZDLWQyeTAxeVVjU0wxWlM2YmRpTVAxcEVJX2xXWlpHemZwYi1HeG9ka1Z1MUhLRDRzN1g2c3BuZ2dXOFk5UC1LSU51MGpoNjc0RmJvTWZESHB2emNGaTVTU0lOaGhUZVlDWkFKWlJxSFo1cnhqS0JyXzJxWXduY2tlZ2JiMEU5MkViOXNsbUlYbVJxU2xSaHFmWjJfdVhTYTdvd3FJY1ZHQ3BYXzJKaG5qY2w4UQ?oc=5)).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × research:** the [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) gives Lula a **60.50%** implicit probability. The most recent survey, published the same day, puts the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) at **46.3% × 46.1%**, a difference of **two tenths of a point**. Win probability and vote margin measure different things, and a candidate can have a 60% chance of winning a tight election. But the magnitude gap is the sharpest of the cycle and, what matters more, **it has not narrowed with three consecutive new data points that contradict it**. The price did not move with Gerp, nor with [Nexus/BTG Pactual](/en/glossary#nexus-btg), nor with Futura/Apex.

> **Research × research, and the inversion no one prices:** the Futura/Apex ([BR-07294/2026](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/)) puts **Zema (3.7%) ahead of Renan Santos (2.6%)** in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno). On the [winner market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), **Renan is worth 10.15% and Zema is worth 0.85%**: twelve times the price, with less declared voting intention. It is not a case of market against research in general. These are **two candidates whose polling inversion reversed, and whose prices did not move**.

> **Institution × method:** the [TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) proposed rewarding polling institutes for **accuracy**, that is, for proximity to the ballot result. The institutes themselves [responded](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMitAFBVV95cUxPejhsd3VsVi04R1gzZ0lTZC0wUGFtb216amw4UzZHYjhuMDV4WHVEZ0FOejYtMDJVdDJfVk1TcW1NaTBpZ1JqSWZVM0FVdUczTVAyc3RzSUpXSHRXdTB0RkFUM1UwZHl6RUc5VHlydkRxNlQzMUgzZlFOWXg0Q29qTzhfSk44OWpldklfeDYxX09aenhySjdKS0tiTnRBV2lBTXZNRHF3cDVUYlNyVkh6QTZBVGY?oc=5) argue that research **measures intention at a moment, does not predict the election**. It is the same category confusion that this Daily separates every day: research and market are not two competing guesses about the same number. They measure different things, and that is why disagreement between them carries information.

## In summary

1. **The caveat is over.** The tightening of the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) went from a small and outlier institute ([Gerp](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), July 8) to a first-tier one ([BTG/Nexus](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), July 13) and now to near-perfect parity ([Futura/Apex](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/), 46.3% × 46.1%). Three institutes, three methodologies, one direction.
2. **The market did not react to any of the three.** On the [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Lula remains at 60.50%, the same number from July 9. The gap fell to +35.65pp, but due to Flávio's gains, not Lula's loss. Sustaining that conviction against three consecutive data points is, in itself, a position.
3. **The next test is immediate.** The [Quaest](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) (n=2,004) comes out on July 15 and the [PoderData](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) (n=2,400) on July 16. If they confirm, the market will be pricing 60.50% against five institutes.-
