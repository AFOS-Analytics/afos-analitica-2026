## 1. Prediction market

The [presidential winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) widened the spread for the second straight day. In the confirmed reading of September 14, at 19:44 Brasília time, Flávio Bolsonaro stands at 51.95% (USD 10.68M) and Lula at 45.50% (USD 11.01M), against 49.65% and 47.50% in the confirmed reading of September 13. The distance went from 2.15pp to 6.45pp, with the total presidential volume at USD 149.82M.

Neither is an extreme: Flávio sits 3.15pp below his series high, of 55.10% on September 10, and Lula 22.00pp below his, of 67.50% on August 16.

In the [first-round runner-up contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), a book of USD 7.12M, Lula rose 1.75pp, to 23.75%, and Flávio fell 1.50pp, to 75.50%. In the [third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Augusto Cury fell 10.20pp, to 45.40% (USD 196 thousand), Ronaldo Caiado rose 7.00pp, to 15.50% (USD 129 thousand), and Renan Santos rose 3.00pp, to 37.50% (USD 307 thousand). None is an extreme: Cury reached 43.90% on September 9, and Caiado 45.50% on August 30.

The [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) rose 0.65pp, to 5.25% (USD 91 thousand), 20.85pp below the 26.10% high of September 11. The [Senate contract](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won) publishes a price again, with the Liberal Party at 84.00% (USD 261 thousand).

## 3. What the press covered

André Mendonça, rapporteur of the Master case, opened access on the morning of September 14 to Petition 15.645, which holds a report on Daniel Vorcaro's payment network. The decision granted a request by Edson Fachin, after a request by Alexandre de Moraes backed by the Prosecutor General's Office, [according to Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/mendonca-derruba-sigilo-de-processo-sobre-pagamentos-de-vorcaro/) and [G1](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/14/pgr-se-manifesta-a-favor-de-retirada-de-sigilo-sobre-rede-de-pagamentos-de-vorcaro-1.ghtml). On the same day, Deputy Prosecutor General Hindenburgo Chateaubriand asked Mendonça's office to clarify what steps were taken in the inquiry, because opening the petition does not show what the rapporteur did, [according to Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/pgr-pede-esclarecimentos-sobre-sigilo-dos-pagamentos-de-vorcaro/).

On September 14 the Federal Police delivered to the offices of Cristiano Zanin and Gilmar Mendes a copy of the data extracted from Vorcaro's phone, at the direct request of both, after Mendonça refused to pass it on, [according to Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/pf-entrega-integra-do-celular-de-vorcaro-a-zanin-e-gilmar/) and [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/politica/pf-libera-dados-de-celular-de-vorcaro-para-ministros-do-stf/).

The September 15 session on Moraes starts at 10:00, with Fachin reading the report, defining what will be judged and voting first, [according to Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/stf-define-regras-para-sessao-que-decidira-sobre-investigacao-contra-moraes/) and [Folha de S.Paulo](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/stf-define-como-sera-a-sessao-inedita-que-vai-julgar-futuro-de-moraes.shtml). The Presidency barred journalists from the plenary, citing limited capacity, and Flávio Dino said he had not been consulted and would have favoured their presence, [according to Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/brasil/stf-proibe-jornalistas-de-entrarem-em-julgamento-de-moraes-dino-se-opoe) and [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/14/stf-acesso-jornalistas-plenario-julgamento-sobre-mensagens-de-vorcaro-a-moraes.ghtml).

On the same day, the Federal Police carried out Operation Transparência 3, authorized by Dino, against congressman Cezinha de Madureira, on suspected influence peddling before the higher courts and with no arrest, [according to Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/pf-deputado-cezinha-de-madureira-suspeita-trafico-influencia/) and [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/14/pf-tribunais.ghtml).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × polls:** the two national polls of September 14 tie the runoff with opposite signs, and the price moved 4.30pp in a single direction, most of it after 13:02, hours after both releases. The order of the facts is measured, the link between them is not.

> **Market × market:** the winner contract and the runner-up contract are independent books, and both moved the same names in the same direction on the same day. A single book moving alone would be easier to read as an isolated flow.

> **Poll × poll:** BTG/Nexus, by phone, and Genial/Quaest, in person, measure the same 5 points in the first round and come out with opposite signs in the pair. The difference fits inside the margin, so neither contradicts the other.

## In summary

**The spread widened 4.30pp with both sides moving in opposite directions, and most of it after 13:02.** Flávio Bolsonaro rose 2.30pp, to 51.95%, and Lula fell 2.00pp, to 45.50%, in a presidential book of USD 149.82M. At 13:02 the distance was 3.25pp, with Lula flat, and at 19:44 it stood at 6.45pp. It is not an extreme: in the recorded readings it has already reached 14.30pp in Flávio's favour, on September 11.

**Two independent books moved in the same direction, and that is what separates repricing from noise.** In the first-round runner-up contract, Lula rose 1.75pp, to 23.75%, and Flávio fell 1.50pp, to 75.50%. Whoever gains the chance of winning loses the chance of finishing second, and the USD 7.12M book confirmed the winner one. The move of the most points on the day is outside it: Augusto Cury fell 10.20pp in the third-place contract, a book of USD 1.15M.

**The two national polls of the day agree on the first round and disagree on the sign of the runoff, and the week still has ten to come.** BTG/Nexus and Genial/Quaest give Lula the same 5 points in the first round, and in the pair the first gives 47% to 46% for Lula and the second 42% to 40% for Flávio, both inside the margin. From September 15 to 18, ten national polls registered with the TSE come out, five of them on the 17th and Veritá, with 40,500 interviews, on the 18th.
