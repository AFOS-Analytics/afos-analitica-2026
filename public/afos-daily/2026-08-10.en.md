## 1. Prediction market

At the confirmed reading of Aug 10, 21:32 BRT, **Lula stood still at 63.50% (USD 8.20M)** and **Flávio Bolsonaro rose 0.30pp, to 27.25% (USD 8.11M)**. In the [presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), the **total cumulative volume adds up to USD 121.85M**.

The gap narrowed to **+36.25pp**, against +36.55pp on Aug 9, and the mechanism is the opposite: yesterday the distance shrank because the leader gave way 1.00pp with the runner-up flat; today, because the runner-up rose with the leader flat. Flávio was the only one in the [chasing pack](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) to rise. Since Aug 3 the gap has fallen on six of the seven sessions, from +38.90pp.

**Renan Santos fell 0.15pp, to 7.65% (USD 9.32M)**, the largest volume among the names above 1%. **Caiado fell 0.10pp, to 1.15% (USD 5.62M)** and **Zema stayed at 0.45% (USD 5.05M)**, below the 0.5% cut that separates price from noise in the [book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election). In the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the PL has **75.50% (USD 259 thousand)**.

⚠️ **Three contracts go without a published number today: the [STF impeachment](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) one and the first-round second and third place ones.** They are the thinnest in the panel, from USD 83 thousand to USD 244 thousand, and today's readings did not hold up against each other. AFOS only publishes a price that two independent readings confirm, eight minutes apart. For the STF, the last confirmed value is the one from Aug 9, 3.60%.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll, and the caveat is the institute's:** Renan Santos comes in at 4% at Genial/Quaest in person on Aug 5, 4.7% at Meio/Ideia by telephone, 4% at BTG/Nexus by telephone today, and [**10% at Palver over the internet**](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMitwFBVV95cUxNczZtaTJHd2RoN2dCR01IRzRCa0JCWjN5U0JmSnhrQ0xkTlZYRzNkdEY5UlVvLWdwQ085alVsSXR0S2Z6aWRwRUN1R1gtb3dDa095ZmhlVVlxUkZWdUtHdy15dHpYUmk4OEJ5aUptUzFhNklWX1dXajFJWWx5ZmpDU1l3MzBDTG1Ba2ZXdnRha3NDZDMtN2QxWldHeEhYMnZyc3VFUnB3UGVUVjBmcDhRZVB2Mm9NR1k?oc=5). Palver itself assessed that the digital format may have boosted his performance, since he keeps an active base in that environment, and said it is testing approaches to reduce the effect. The price sits **between the two methods**, at 7.65%.

> **Price × price:** the same candidate is at his ceiling in the polling and near his floor in the [book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) in the same week. In the 88-day series, 84 of them had a value equal to or above the current 7.65%, with a maximum of 17.90% on Jun 9.

> **Poll × poll:** adding the [four national polls](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMijAFBVV95cUxQeFBWZWctVFJsTUVQVUhzVTRheDN6Unc2VktiQ3JpVjVTMXN4T2tLUkFyc25VRGZfbWNvV3g3T095TC0wRDJ6SzFtWHNvZ2RlZG1HYURud0plYldNLUZKMFZiRC1kdjNhOVByZmFYUHEtT0UwVWZHSG55ZWhqdGtEd2h3c2VNU0pVUXVJMg?oc=5) since Aug 5, Lula runs from 39% to 44% and the runner-up from **30% to 40%**, a 10pp band, double the leader's. In the runoff the distance between them runs from 0pp to 5.5pp depending on the house. Choosing one poll is choosing the conclusion, and that is why this panel publishes the set.
