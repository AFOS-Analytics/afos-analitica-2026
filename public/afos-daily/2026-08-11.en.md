## 1. Prediction market

Prices from the [confirmed reading](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) of August 11, 18:22 BRT. **Lula closed at 63.50%** (vol USD 8.21M cumulative), flat for the fourth session running. **Flávio Bolsonaro closed at 27.25%** (vol USD 8.12M), where he already stood on August 10, after marking 26.95% at 16:27. The gap ended at **+36.25pp**, the same as August 10. The total volume of the presidential book adds up to **USD 122.17M**.

**Renan Santos was the only name to move, and the move came back.** At 16:27 he marked 8.40%; at the close he stood at **7.75%** (vol USD 9.44M), 0.10pp above August 10. In the 90-day series, 78 had a value equal to or above it. No cause is attributed to the swing: nothing was measured linking it to an event of the day.

The chasing pack gave up 0.10pp each: Caiado 1.05% (vol USD 5.63M), Zema 0.35% (vol USD 5.05M) and Haddad 0.05% (vol USD 7.01M). Tarcísio de Freitas remains at 0.05% with **USD 13.90M**, the largest volume in the book: conviction already priced in, not movement.

In the sub-markets, Flávio stands at 80.50% in the first-round runner-up contract (vol USD 245 thousand), against 82.00% on August 10, and Renan at 64.00% in the third-place one (vol USD 178 thousand). Impeachment at the STF marks **3.80%** (vol USD 83 thousand), the thinnest on the panel. In the Senate, the PL stands at 76.50% (vol USD 259 thousand).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** three institutes published readings eleven points apart in the runoff and the price did not move. Lula flat for the fourth session, Flávio back at his August 10 value, gap identical. The two instruments measured the same week under opposite uncertainty regimes, and this panel records the difference without saying which one is right.

> **Poll × poll:** Flávio's spread in the window reached 11.3pp, almost double Lula's 6pp. The measurement uncertainty is concentrated in one of the two names, and choosing one of the three rounds of August 11 is choosing the conclusion.

> **Price × price:** the same Renan Santos contract was read at 8.40% and at 7.75% within two hours, with both readings confirmed. Publishing only one of them would make a day with a 1.4pp swing look like a flat day.

## In summary

1. **The market closed where it started**, with the gap at +36.25pp, on a day with three polls.
2. **The polls do not agree with one another**: eleven points in the runoff, 9.3 in the first round.
3. **The Master case is stuck on a document**, and the deadlock is circular.
