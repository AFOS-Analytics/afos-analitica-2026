## 1. Prediction market

The [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) turned around in this snapshot on Monday, June 22, a day without a new national voting survey. **Lula** jumped to 55.50% (USD 6.67M, ↑4.00pp), a new cycle peak, while **Flávio Bolsonaro** retreated to 23.75% (USD 6.90M, ↓1.15pp), so the gap between the two opened to +31.75pp, a record for the period (it was +26.60pp). Total accumulated volume in the presidential market totals around USD 103M.

The methodological reading is one of repricing, not a single trigger: with no ballot event on the day, the market appears to have digested the [Datafolha](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqgFBVV95cUxNXzFIcDBDb0lLajJpVHlDOVpaTmlUTWVNUXRfaXJLR1Vfc0prMENrQXNjakZSdG1sSHJ1MjNnVFFNMXpKQ25SYnFLdDFQSHEtRXFSbGNXdkNNZFYwZHBOSzVzZ3l1am4tcHIwTDlYT0s5YXA2T19PWnlXU20xQXd6UFgydlhqbW12NENBR3oyMkplUENVV216QjhYcngtcFBGcGNSRm5uVGRoZw?oc=5) from Saturday, which measured a competitive [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) (47% × 43%), in light of a Master case that the press began to read as greater damage to the opposition. Correlation does not authorize causation, but the day's net result was the market opening distance in favor of the president.

**Renan Santos** retreated to 13.70% (USD 7.33M, ↓0.60pp), still the largest accumulated volume in the presidential market, and remains locked in as the favorite for 3rd place in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), at 60.5% in the [placement sub-market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place). The third way retreated as a bloc: **Caiado** fell to 1.55% (USD 4.32M, ↓0.45pp), **Haddad** to 1.75% (USD 5.87M, ↓0.35pp) and **Zema** to 1.15% (USD 3.81M, ↓0.25pp), with **Camilo Santana** at 1.65%. In the [2nd place in the 1º turno sub-market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), **Flávio** remains isolated at the front, at 68.5%.

In other markets, the [STF impeach](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) rose slightly to 3.75% (USD 82k), at a low level. In [Senate by number of seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) retreated to 76.5% (USD 243k), maintaining the lead. In [2026 annual inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the 5.00-5.49% band remains leader at 33.3%, followed by the 5.50-5.99% band at 21.8% and the 6.00-6.49% band at 21.6%, a distribution that indicates uncertainty.
