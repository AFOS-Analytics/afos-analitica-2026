## 1. Prediction market

**The stalled price.** In the confirmed reading of October 12 at 15:00 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 87.05% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 19.63M) and 12.50% for Lula (USD 22.27M). Against the confirmed reading of October 11 at 17:33 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro rises 0.40pp and Lula stays unchanged; since the sum of the pair went from 99.15 to 99.55, the normalized movement is 0.05pp to each side. The gap goes from 74.15pp to 74.55pp. The total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 180.36M in the 16:02 BRT reading, and the largest volume in the book is still Lula's.
