## 1. Prediction market

The [presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), whose total accumulated volume sums to **USD 113.7M**, spent the day reversing yesterday's movement. Flávio Bolsonaro fell 0.55pp to **26.50% (USD 7.5M)**, undoing the 1.60pp spike from yesterday, while Lula remained flat at **60.50% (USD 7.4M)**. With this, the gap between the two widened again, from +33.45pp to **+34.00pp**, due to the opponent's decline, not Lula's rise. Without new national polling and without ballot events, the back-and-forth in Flávio's price is [market momentum](https://www.afos-analytics.com/pt-BR/how-it-works), AFOS adjusting conviction, not reacting to fact.

In the [third way](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan Santos halted the correction he had been making and rose 0.45pp to **8.30% (USD 8.2M)**, after three sessions declining from 10.15% toward the 6% that PoderData measured. Michelle Bolsonaro fell back to **1.80% (USD 9.1M)** and Ronaldo Caiado rose to **1.40% (USD 5.0M)**, adjustments for residual names without ballot backing.

In the sub-markets, the 2nd place in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) tracked the presidential: Flávio ceded to **81.50%**, followed by Renan (10.7%). In 3rd place, Renan leads with **68.50%** and Caiado advanced to **16.50%**. In the [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) remains comfortable at **85.50% (USD 250 thousand)**, ahead of [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) (13.8%).

The [impeachment of STF minister](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) market rose slightly, to **3.60% (USD 84 thousand)**, and remains near the floor, in the noise range. A methodological note is warranted: the volume of this book is orders of magnitude below the USD 7.4M of the presidential, so the signal is weak. On 2026 inflation, the modal range of the market is 5.00% to 5.49%, at 39.60%, in line with the official Finance projection (5.1%), above the Central Bank target.

## 3. Press coverage

The political fact of the day was institutional. Alexandre de Moraes, presiding over the [STF](/en/glossary#stf) during recess, [denied the request for Javier Milei to visit Jair Bolsonaro](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/politica/2026/07/7463769-moraes-nega-pedido-para-que-milei-visite-bolsonaro-em-domiciliar.html) in house arrest. The visit was scheduled for July 25, when the Argentine president would come to Brazil for the [PL](/en/glossary#pl) convention and the launch of Flávio's candidacy. In a sole decision, Moraes blocked visits with political-electoral objectives until the end of the elections and suspended general visits for 30 days, allowing only lawyers, doctors, and physiotherapists. The decision came after Flávio released a handwritten letter from Bolsonaro to the Brazilian people, and the [Bolsonarism classified the act as silencing](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/moraes-nega-pedido-para-visita-de-milei-a-bolsonaro/).

In the opposition camp, Flávio Bolsonaro had [day of scheduled testimony and new response to the tariff dispute with the US](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi0wFBVV95cUxQMjEtd1BsS2tZdnVwaDd4ZlFLdDhnWENZYzFQSzJHU2xvNGxSdGJlSURqRF9JRkhybFR2aVpxVVRzS05scEp5U1VsaGpGTTlBZy1XSjNfalJXbjVET2RMaHJQQUx2OHVpN3NlZXRacFJjRVFGaUlIcVhpclZWSjBtNThvdDNlMTFvUlZqR0RaZzBzQzlTcDBIdjNNNjBrREhna1R3WTdkX1dBQlFYdzdjaVFLVFJkbHlHcDlmTGVwdUJVNVphTmRXdTRZR28tcUNLX0Zj?oc=5), while the [PT intensified attacks on him on social media](https://veja.abril.com.br/politica/pt-intensifica-bombardeio-a-flavio-bolsonaro-nas-redes-sociais/). On the government side, Folha de S.Paulo pointed to [Lula as the week's winner among the three branches of government](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/colunas/painel/2026/07/tres-poderes-lula-e-o-vencedor-da-semana-nunes-marques-o-perdedor.shtml), but his agenda remains stalled: the Senate left for recess without voting on priorities, [projects remained in limbo](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMisgFBVV95cUxNQ2Vvc0E5NEU3Y3RuWFRmNkRfd0FDSzhPaHlQQ3FkcHY1c3owSlZZdTAwYmprNF9qZlliNlZTUUdQbjZSY1BZZW1zQjF5WXU0anRxUjJzVVdyekJjVUF4ODZ4aDJick9XaUFsVmhOWlVfSUQycHNaSWJEMTMwSFYtNU1mb1dDM2pnR01ZeVkzWnR0aWRDa0F4NFR5bHAzNWhsYnphOF8zZU9SOHhpNVJGRUlB?oc=5) and [four provisional measures lapsed in July](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqwFBVV95cUxOQ282NHU2ZGlLTTQ5WVRobXlBYV9ycFozYzNhYlFDS284UlZGUGNnZ1h0RGQ3UWd4d0lSdms5RWRnaWxuWXV6bmd2VXpvMlZmR1FSS04yaUZKLWtXQkpkQTBMajh5ZEc1eUlzX0dMUndpenltdHFPOFM0NzVJODVKRUZkT2NLNDR5cnhQOXFnYWgzdFhiang5RGt6WllSb1FUUU5yVzNnTjZCVkU?oc=5) due to lack of voting.

> **Market × polling (Renan):** Renan Santos is worth **8.30% in the winner's market**, against **6% in the best poll** he has received (PoderData July 16) and **3% in [Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest)** (July 15, the most reliable one). It is the largest relative distortion on the panel, and the two polling firms still disagree on who leads the third way. Without new polling, the price adjusted itself today, rising.

> **Probability × vote (Flávio):** the market gives **26.50% probability** of Flávio winning the election, a number that measures chance of winning, not voting intention. In the polls he appears between 28% and 34% in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno). These are different measures, and the gap between them is the signal that AFOS Analytics tracks, not a polling error.

> **Market × narrative ([STF](/en/glossary#stf)):** despite Moraes' decision denying Milei's visit dominating the news, the [STF](/en/glossary#stf) minister impeachment market remained at **3.60%**, near the floor. Real money prices institutional friction, not rupture.
