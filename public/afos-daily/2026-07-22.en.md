## 1. Prediction market

74 days from the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), the presidential market closed the day practically where it started, with accumulated total volume summing about **USD 114.6M** ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) via proxy AFOS, fetched July 22 14:42 BRT). **Lula** remains the isolated favorite at **60.50% (USD 7.5M)**, unchanged. **Flávio Bolsonaro** retreated to **24.95% (USD 7.5M)**, and the gap between the two widened to **+35.55pp**, still below the cycle peak of +39.5pp registered on July 3. The movement came from the opponent's side, not from a step by the favorite.

The highlight of the day is in the third way. **Renan Santos** rose to **10.70% (USD 8.3M)**, the second highest price in the presidential book, consolidating double digits. **Ronaldo Caiado** retreated to **1.50% (USD 5.0M)** and **Romeu Zema** to **0.50% (USD 4.5M)**, returning part of the previous day's gain. **Michelle Bolsonaro**, who is not a declared pre-candidate, marks **1.25% (USD 9.1M)**, and **Fernando Haddad**, focused on São Paulo's government, **0.40% (USD 6.3M)** ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election)).

In sub-markets, Flávio maintains a comfortable lead for second place in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) at **78.00%**, and Renan leads third place at **63.50%**, with Caiado at 16.50%. The [impeachment of STF minister before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) market continues near the floor at **2.95% (USD 83 thousand)**, a volume base orders of magnitude below the presidential, which recommends treating any variation there as noise, not signal.

In [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) leads with **75.00%**, ahead of [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) (12.45%). In [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the modal price range is 5.00% to 5.49%, with 37.6%, followed by the range of 4.50% to 4.99%, with 26.55%, a distribution center that continues above the target ceiling.

It is the reading of a market that treats Gerp as the outlier it is, not as a turnaround.

> **Signal × confirmation:** the two highlights from the previous day, Renan's 9% and Caiado's tie with Lula in the [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno), both from Real Time, were not confirmed in Gerp, which returns Renan to 3% and shows Lula ahead of Caiado 44 to 41. For now, they remain as a reading from a single house.
