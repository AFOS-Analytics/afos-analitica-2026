## 1. Prediction market

In the presidential market, the day was marked by near immobility despite two national polls released. [Lula](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) remained stable at 60.50% (USD 7.5M), and Flávio Bolsonaro declined 0.10pp, to 26.15% (USD 7.5M). The gap between the two widened to +34.35pp, a rounding movement stemming from the opponent's decline rather than Lula's advance, and remains well below the cycle's peak of +39.5pp on July 3. Total accumulated volume in the presidential market totals approximately USD 114.2M, the signature of real money that sets AFOS Analytics apart from a polling aggregator.

In the third way, [Renan Santos](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) remained virtually flat in the winner, at 8.85% (USD 8.2M), maintaining itself as the third largest price in the book, behind only Lula and Flávio, on the day a poll gave him [surprising performance](https://veja.abril.com.br/politica/o-surpreendente-desempenho-de-renan-santos-na-pesquisa-real-time-big-data/). Caiado rose 0.50pp, to 1.85% (USD 5.0M), and Zema remained at 0.85% (USD 4.4M). The contrast that defines the field persists: the three together measure, in declared voting intention, much more than the market attributes to them in the winner contract.

In the placement sub-markets, Flávio leads the [second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) with 78.00%, yielding 1.00pp on the day, and Renan leads the [third place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) with 63.50%, with Caiado second at 16.50%. These are the two contracts where betting on Renan and Caiado has backing-placement rather than victory-and where their price makes more sense than in the winner market.

In institutional and economic markets, the [impeachment of STF minister before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) remained stable at 2.95% (USD 0.08M), near the floor, in a book with volume orders of magnitude below the presidential, where no variation sustains narrative. In the [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the [PL](/en/glossary#pl) rose to 86.00% (USD 0.25M), the most solid asset in the right field across the entire panel. And the [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) market maintained the modal range of 5.00 to 5.49% priced at 37.50%, with the center of distribution above the ceiling of the target.

## 2. What the institutes recorded

The national polling drought ended with two rounds released on the same day, and they agreed on essentials and diverged on the runoff. The [Indexa](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/politica/como-esta-a-disputa-pela-presidencia-segundo-a-nova-pesquisa-indexa/) (n=2,000, field July 16–19, registration BR-02904/2026) gave [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) Lula 41% × Flávio 30%, with Caiado 6%, Zema and Renan Santos 3%; in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno), Lula 46% × Flávio 39%, and Lula also winning against Zema, Caiado, and Renan. The [Real Time Big Data](https://exame.com/brasil/pesquisa-real-time-big-data-lula-tem-40-e-flavio-bolsonaro-33-no-1o-turno/) (n=2,000, field July 18–20, registration BR-09247/2026) gave [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) Lula 40% × Flávio 33%, with Renan Santos at 9% and Caiado at 7%; in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno), Lula 45% × Flávio 42% ([technical tie](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/eleicoes/real-time-presidente-2o-turno-julho-2026/)), Lula 43% × Caiado 44% and Lula 44% × Renan 35%. ## 3. What the press covered

In government affairs, the calendar became the stage. In a speech at the PDT convention, Lula adopted [campaign tone for reelection](https://g1.globo.com/politica/eleicoes/2026/noticia/2026/07/21/democracia-privatizacoes-recado-aos-eua-discurso-de-lula-em-convencao-do-pdt-indica-tom-de-campanha-a-reeleicao.ghtml), with emphasis on democracy, criticism of privatizations, and a message regarding the tariff dispute with the United States. The party conventions, opened on July 20 and running until August 5, are the first structural test of the cycle, when a pre-candidacy becomes a registered slate.

In the opposition and third-way fields, the day's highlight was the early scheduling of the Missão convention, which [officially nominated Renan Santos as a presidential candidate](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/07/citando-ameacas-missao-antecipa-convencao-e-oficializa-renan-santos-como-candidato-a-presidencia.shtml) on July 20, with Aroldo Medina as vice. The event, originally scheduled for August 1, was moved up on security recommendations after threats attributed to criminal factions, so the candidate would have Federal Police support at the launch. In the other parties, the calendar opened with [uncertainties about the running mates of Flávio Bolsonaro and Romeu Zema](https://g1.globo.com/politica/eleicoes/2026/noticia/2026/07/20/calendario-de-convencoes-comeca-com-indefinicao-dos-vices-de-flavio-bolsonaro-e-romeu-zema.ghtml) and [incomplete tickets in Rio, Minas Gerais, and Pernambuco](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/07/20/convencoes-presidenciais-e-estaduais-iniciam-com-indefinicoes-sobre-vices-de-flavio-e-zema-chapas-incompletas-no-rj-mg-e-pe-e-centrao-indeciso.ghtml). In the internal friction of Bolsonarism, Eduardo Bolsonaro stated that [Michelle could never have made the video against Flávio](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/eduardo-bolsonaro-diz-que-michelle-jamais-poderia-ter-feito-video-contra-flavio-foi-imaturidade/), calling the episode immaturity.

On the institutional agenda, Jair Bolsonaro's defense, under house arrest since March, [asked Alexandre de Moraes to restore Flávio's visits to his father](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/07/20/defesa-de-bolsonaro-visitas-flavio.ghtml), currently suspended by a ruling in effect until mid-October. The Master case continued as a background development, with no new verified fact that justifies treating it as a turning point on this date.

A methodological note: none of today's price movements had an identifiable event trigger. The formalization of Renan and his jump to 9% in Real Time are facts of the day, but the market did not price them as a turning point; his price rose 0.05pp. We describe the coincidence as context, not as cause.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × polling, on the third way:** for weeks Renan Santos was the most glaring example of a price disconnected from polling, trading close to 9% in the market against a ceiling of 6% in surveys. On July 21 that was tested and did not converge, it broke: Real Time gave him 9%, matching the price, and Indexa, on the same day, measured him at 3%. There is now a polling result on the market's side, but without a second source, and real convergence requires two readings in the same direction.

> **Market × polling, on the runoff:** the market prices Flávio at 78.00% in second place in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), the bet that he is the right opponent for Lula in the runoff. Real Time challenged that premise by giving Caiado numerically ahead of Lula in a direct runoff (44% x 43%), with the most competitive opponent to Lula not being Flávio. It is a reading from a single house, to be confirmed, but it directly attacks the central asset of Flávio's candidacy.

> **Market × polling, on the level:** two national polls published and Lula's price did not move, staying at 60.50% against a runoff that the hardest reading of the day (Real Time) shows as [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico). The gap of +34.35pp remains well above the margin that polling measures, and the level distance between real money and declared vote continues to be the underlying tension of the panel.

## In summary

1. Two national polls broke the polling drought and agreed on the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) (Lula 40-41%, Flávio 30-33%), but diverged on the runoff: Indexa gave Lula +7pp, Real Time returned it to [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico).
2. Renan Santos had the densest day of the cycle, officially nominated as a candidate by Missão and hitting 9% in Real Time, but Indexa kept him at 3% and the market barely moved, which keeps the distortion open rather than resolving it.
3. Real money ignored the two polls: Lula at 60.50%, gap at +34.35pp and accumulated volume of the presidential at USD 114.2M, with the only new polling signal, Caiado ahead of Lula in a runoff, still pending confirmation.-
