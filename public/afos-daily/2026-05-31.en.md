## 1. Prediction market

The presidential market closed May 31 with the top flat: Lula at [40.50% (USD 6.01M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), no change in 24 hours. Flávio Bolsonaro falls to [28.35% (USD 6.14M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) (↓0.30pp), and since the decline came only from his side, the gap between the two WIDENS to +12.15pp (versus +11.85pp the previous day). On a Sunday with low activity, the movement is noise, not repricing.

Renan Santos gives back part of the jump that took him to his peak on May 30: falls to [16.85% (USD 6.25M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) (↓0.35pp), in a technical correction after five days of gains, but comfortably maintains second place in the market. Haddad rises slightly to [5.30% (USD 5.29M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) (↑0.10pp), Zema breaks the losing streak and advances to [2.75% (USD 3.24M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) (↑0.20pp), and Caiado moves to 1.45% (USD 3.50M). In position markets, Flávio leads second place with [62.50% in 2nd place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) and Renan is the favorite for third.

In the Senate, [PL](/en/glossary#pl) maintains [68.00% in the race for more seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won); below it, [União Brasil](/en/glossary#uniao-brasil) (14.90%), [PSB](/en/glossary#psb) (14.30%, in a sharp book jump), [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) (15.20%) and [PSD](/en/glossary#psd) (6.55%) move in sub-markets with low liquidity, which require cautious reading. The [STF](/en/glossary#stf) impeach remains at [5.45% (USD 80k)](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027), stable.

The underlying signal of the day came from inflation. The market shifted the 2026 curve upward: the [6.00–6.49% band jumped to 23.70% (↑6.75pp)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) and the 6.50–6.99% rose to 14.90%, with the modal 5.00–5.49% band still at 40.95%. This is repricing for slightly higher inflation — the type of macro movement that electoral surveys do not capture.
