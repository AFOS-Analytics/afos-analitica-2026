## 1. Prediction market

The [presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) retreated on the survey day, and it is the first time in weeks that this has happened. **Lula fell to 60.50%** (↓1.00pp, USD 7.39M), returning exactly the previous day's gain, just hours after the national poll of the day tightened the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno). The gap over Flávio stood at **+35.05pp**. Total accumulated volume in the presidential market amounts to **USD 113.3M**.

**Flávio Bolsonaro** remained practically flat at **25.45%** (↑0.15pp, USD 7.42M) and remained the comfortable leader of the [sub-market for second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), at **83.50%** (↑0.50pp, USD 180 thousand). In 24 hours he received the worst and the best reading of the week and the price did not move in either direction.

In the third way, **Renan Santos fell to 8.40%** (↓0.10pp, USD 8.13M), accumulating a decline from 10.15% to 8.40% in two sessions, and retreated in the [third place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) to **68.50%** (↓0.50pp, USD 140 thousand). **Ronaldo Caiado** was at **1.25%** (↓0.15pp, USD 4.93M) and rose to **14.00%** in third place; **Romeu Zema** fell to **0.65%** (↓0.15pp, USD 4.39M). **Michelle Bolsonaro** rose to **1.40%** (↑0.10pp, USD 9.03M) and **Fernando Haddad** to **1.25%** (↑0.15pp, USD 6.23M).

In the sub-markets, the largest movement was the [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won): **[PL](/en/glossary#pl) rose to 86.00%** (↑1.50pp, USD 252 thousand), against 11.75% for [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) (USD 10 thousand). The [impeachment of STF minister](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) rose to **3.35%** (↑0.55pp, USD 84 thousand) and remains near the floor. It is worth noting the scale caveat: these books sum to hundreds of thousands of dollars, against the USD 7.4M of the presidential market, so a 0.55pp variation there requires much less money than the same variation in the main market.

In the [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the range of **5.00% to 5.49% stood at 37.75%** (USD 10 thousand) and 4.50% to 4.99% at 26.90%, summing approximately 64.7% between the two. Treasury raised on July 16 the official projection to **5.1%**, above the Central Bank's target: the government's projection fell within the modal range of real money.

## 2. What the institutes registered

**PoderData/Aya** published on July 16 the national poll that the AFOS panel had been pointing to as the week's test. It showed **[first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) Lula 40% × Flávio 34%** and **[second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) Lula 45% × Flávio 43%**, [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) by a margin of 2pp ([Poder360](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMipAFBVV95cUxOLVBVYWVBMV9fRkE3Wm5aTFpiM3lDVFBzVE8wM2c4S1pBVXczQ19ZQV9QY2FJWmN5TE55QllVRjNEcVgzUmlSWVRDR1FwNUhYcUh6ZlY4T3lSM3paekRKS2IzZmp5NEMwNFNFaVNfQS1wM1RPU05fTXE2S1hLS0tYMzU2UFgzMVQwamFsdHBmN01sYnNiQzZ3OWdWeF9leFNTNWtLbA?oc=5)). There are 2,400 interviews, fieldwork from July 12 to 15, telephone, in 685 municipalities across 27 states, registration BR-00059/2026. **Renan Santos** appeared with **6%**, **Ronaldo Caiado** and **Romeu Zema** with 4% each and **Augusto Cury** with 3% ([CNN Brasil](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirAFBVV95cUxNYm1SS0hrTHVFMGN3Vm41OUJhZ09LcmxwWGVfWHgxVzdiR3U1YnlPbGU5X3dlNXBhTDBjZFB5UzRmZWQzQWt1OHFsWko2VXkycldsUGVYREl6TEo2UXJoWFB5U3lfS0tBbk9QRGQtWGRXU1pnQ2hBV1VCYkVaQVQ3OTlEWWVhRFcxcG94TmQ3ZklTc01WNXZlQmIza3R2NFMxanFlV1FFWm9wR3VI?oc=5)).

The contrast with **Genial/[Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest)** from July 15 (n=2,004, [BR-07181/2026](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/)) is the finding of the day. Quaest showed [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) 40% × 28% and [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) 45% × 37% ([Metrópoles](https://www.metropoles.com/brasil/genial-quaest-lula-lidera-em-todos-os-cenarios-de-1o-e-2o-turnos)). Notice what does not change: **Lula marks 40% in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) and 45% in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) in both polls**, identical number, in houses of opposite methodology, one in-person and the other by telephone, with overlapping fieldwork periods. The entire disagreement is about Flávio: **28% against 34%** in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) and **37% against 43%** in the runoff. Six points on the same candidate, in the same period.

This is a house effect, and the series allow verification rather than speculation. Within PoderData itself, Flávio went from 36% on June 25 to 34% now. Within Quaest itself, he went from 29% in June to 28%. **Each house measures at a different level and each one measures a decline.** The two agree on the direction and disagree on the level. Lula's approval follows the same pattern: **48% × 47% at Quaest** against **42% × 51% at PoderData** ([Poder360](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqgFBVV95cUxPSDVabFB3OHZkakQ2aVBQc250ejZISTFnVzRMN2dON2dSaGQxRU5ja3BGWUYxUnNWbG1vY2VzTXc5YUZWeEE3VjNFYkRGOW4tOHBuOTlNOUphcUQ5eEdQWTdvbU13czlSdFF5eUpzNE9pUmlvR3lxNGtONjl1M0hjNFg4UXM0Tk4za0VIc0tZMlNOTVQ3NWM0cDZoN3dBcElIYmxkYXVoRkVpUQ?oc=5)), a nine-point difference in disapproval. Rejection as well: Quaest gives Flávio at 57% and Lula at 50%; PoderData **ties the two at 48%** ([Poder360](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqwFBVV95cUxNbUswVlFtOWdKLUJuSUZ2YXNzT2x4dHVJbm51SlBJS0hxd2w5cHlQa0tZWW5PWl80azhKUE4zZVFEUlBuQmN4T0RJSTBGZVhncWpJeGg4dGJ2RENtWDdNaUxyWkFzN2YzY3FjeFJKMXFyZFNPNTVNejJpN0VEUzNfU3RVWkZNUGJjdld4S0daV0xuRkV1TmpfWVlWdTRfRV9KcERTejJmWUFyUnc?oc=5)).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Poll × poll:** the two best national polls of the week **agree on Lula and disagree on Flávio**. Lula marks 40% in the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) and 45% in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) in both; Flávio marks 28% in [Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest) and 34% in PoderData. When two polls agree on one candidate and diverge six points on another, what is at stake is **measurement, not voter movement**. Within each series, Flávio declines: 29% → 28% in [Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest), 36% → 34% in PoderData. Comparing [Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest) from July 15 with PoderData from July 16 and calling the difference a turnaround is changing the scale midway through the measurement.

> **Market × poll:** the price **read the poll and moved**. Lula ceded 1.00pp to 60.50% on [Polymarket]([presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election)) a few hours after PoderData returned the runoff to [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico). For weeks the market sustained Lula above 60% while the national polls pointed to a tie. It is small in magnitude and relevant in significance. The level distance remains: **60.50% probability against a [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) in [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico)**. And the tension of the day is that the most reliable poll ([Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest), in-person) supports the market, while the most recent (PoderData, telephone) contradicts it.

> **Bilateral convergence, the first of the cycle:** the widest divergence on the panel narrowed **from both sides on the same day**. The price of Renan Santos came from 10.15% to 8.40% in two sessions on [Polymarket]([third place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place)), and the ballot rose from 3% ([Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest)) to **6%** on [Polymarket]([PoderData/Aya](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMipAFBVV95cUxOLVBVYWVBMV9fRkE3Wm5aTFpiM3lDVFBzVE8wM2c4S1pBVXczQ19ZQV9QY2FJWmN5TE55QllVRjNEcVgzUmlSWVRDR1FwNUhYcUh6ZlY4T3lSM3paekRKS2IzZmp5NEMwNFNFaVNfQS1wM1RPU05fTXE2S1hLS0tYMzU2UFgzMVQwamFsdHBmN01sYnNiQzZ3OWdWeF9leFNTNWtLbA?oc=5)), his highest mark in the cycle and also a rise within the firm itself, which measured him at 4% on June 25. From 8.50% against 3% to 8.40% against 6%. A divergence does not always resolve with one side surrendering to the other.

> **News × market:** neither the July 15 image in the Master case, nor the escalation against the [STF](/en/glossary#stf), nor the bombshell agenda moved Flávio's price, stuck at 25.45%. The [Polymarket]([impeachment of STF minister](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027)) rose to 3.35% (↑0.55pp) and remains near the floor, in a book of USD 84 thousand. Real money continues to price friction, not rupture.

## In summary

1. **The announced test came out backwards.** The AFOS panel registered on July 15 that PoderData would be the test of [Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest)'s recovery in leeway. It came and returned the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) to [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) (45% × 43%). Recording this matters more than having gotten it right.
2. **The disagreement is about Flávio, not Lula.** The two firms agree on Lula's number and diverge six points on Flávio's, and agree that he falls within each series. The direction is robust; the level depends on who measures.
3. **Tuesday, July 21, is the next test.** Real Time Big Data and Instituto Indexa publish national polls on the same day, both [open source]([registered with TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/)), with fields that close only on July 19 and 20, after tariff confirmation. They will be the first to measure the electorate with the tariff already confirmed.-
