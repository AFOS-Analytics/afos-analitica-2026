## 1. Prediction market

The [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) registered on June 18 a partial reversal of the previous day's movement. **Lula** remained stable at 50.50% (USD 6.53M), while **Flávio Bolsonaro** recovered part of the week's decline and rose to 25.95% (↑1.90pp, USD 6.71M). With this, the gap between the two narrowed to +24.55pp, below the record renewed yesterday (+26.45pp). The total accumulated volume in the presidential market totals ~USD 101.6M.

The method data from recent days reversed direction: **Renan Santos** gave back part of the week's gains and fell to 14.55% (↓1.20pp, USD 7.21M), still the highest accumulated volume in the presidential market. Flávio's recovery came with Renan's decline, a sign of communicating vessels in the pricing of the opposition field, with no single triggering event explaining the swap.

On the third way, the picture remains at the floor. **Caiado** rose slightly to 2.20% (↑0.25pp, USD 4.18M), **Haddad** remained stable at 1.85% (USD 5.78M), ahead of Camilo Santana (1.75%), and **Zema** held at 1.45% (USD 3.72M). In the [sub-market for 2nd place in the 1º turno](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio widened the lead to 69.5% (USD 63.5k); in the [3rd place market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan fell to 46.55% (↓7pp, USD 81.1k), followed by Caiado and Zema, both at 13%.

In other markets, [STF](/en/glossary#stf) impeach fell to 2.45% (↓1.15pp, USD 81.8k), even with the new phase of [PF](/en/glossary#pf) in the Master case, indicating that the market did not raise the priced chance of minister removal. On the [Senate by number of seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) widened its lead to 77.5% (↑2.5pp, USD 242.9k). On [annual inflation in 2026](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the 5.00-5.49% band remains leader at 31.75%, now closely followed by the 5.50-5.99% band at 29.90%, with 6.00-6.49% at 22.35%.

## 2. What the institutes registered

No new national survey was published on June 18. The ballot backdrop remains anchored in the three nationals of June 15-16, all with Lula expanding: CNT/MDA ([2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) 49.3% × 36.8%, the largest gap in the sample), Futura/Apex ([2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) 48.1% × 42.9%) and [Nexus/BTG Pactual](/en/glossary#nexus-btg) ([2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) 49% × 43%, with government approval exceeding disapproval for the 1st time in the cycle, 48% × 47%).

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × survey:** even after today's decline, **Renan Santos** maintains the largest divergence on the Dashboard: the market prices him at 14.55%, against the 2% measured by CNT/MDA in the [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) (~12.55pp). The movement away from the peak (↓1.20pp in the winner's market, ↓7pp in the third-place sub-market) narrowed the gap, but did not close it.

> **Market × narrative:** Flávio's recovery (↑1.90pp) coincided, in time, with the symmetry of the Master case, which began to reach the government field. The data does not authorize direct causation — Renan's simultaneous decline suggests internal reallocation in opposition pricing, not an isolated reaction to the operation against Wagner.

> **Event × institutional risk:** the 9th phase of [PF](/en/glossary#pf) in the Master case coexisted with the **decline** of [STF](/en/glossary#stf) impeach in the market (↓1.15pp, to 2.45%). Even with the investigation reaching both fields, the market did not raise the priced probability of removal of an [STF](/en/glossary#stf) minister.
