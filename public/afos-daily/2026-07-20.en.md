## 1. Prediction market

The presidential market repeated on Monday the pattern that dominates the week, with a reversal of sign. All the movement came again from the opponent, but this time upward: **Flávio Bolsonaro rose 0.60pp, to 26.25% (USD 7.5M)**, recovering part of three consecutive sessions of losses through July 19, while **Lula remained flat at 60.50% (USD 7.5M)** for the third trading session. The gap between the two narrowed to +34.25pp, still well below the cycle peak of +39.5pp registered on July 3. There was neither polling nor exit data behind the movement, which characterizes it as momentum oscillation rather than reaction to an event. The [total accumulated volume in presidential market amounts to approximately USD 114.0M](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), the base of real money that sustains the reading.

On the third way, **Renan Santos rose 0.50pp, to 8.65% (USD 8.2M)**, consolidating himself as the third largest price in the book, behind only Lula and Flávio. It is the most didactic distortion on the panel: he is worth 8.65% in the market against a ceiling of 6% in the latest polling. **Ronaldo Caiado remained stable at 1.35% (USD 5.0M)** and **Romeu Zema at 0.65% (USD 4.4M)**, both without polling evidence to justify the price, in a contrast that persists, since both measure 4% of voting intention and are worth a fraction of that in the market.

On the [sub-markets of the presidential panel](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), **Flávio remains comfortably in 2nd place of the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), at 79.00%**, the bet that he is the right opponent to Lula in an eventual runoff. **Michelle Bolsonaro remained stable at 1.45% (USD 9.1M)** in the winner, and **Fernando Haddad at 0.55% (USD 6.3M)**, residual name without a priceable presidential candidacy.

The [impeachment market for STF minister before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) **fell 0.80pp, to 2.95% (USD 83 thousand)**, reversing the recent rise and returning near the floor. The methodological caveat is decisive here: with accumulated volume of about USD 83 thousand, orders of magnitude below the USD 7.5M of the presidential, the variation does not sustain narrative and reads as book noise. In the Senate by seats, the **[PL](/en/glossary#pl) remains comfortably ahead at 85.50%**. In the 2026 annual inflation market, the modal range remains at 5.00% to 5.50%, priced at 36.85%, with the center of the distribution above the target ceiling.

## 2. What the institutes registered

It was the fourth consecutive day without new national voting intention polling. The two latest readings remain [Genial/Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest) from July 15 (reliability 5), which gave [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) 40% to 28% and [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) 45% to 37%, and PoderData/Aya from July 16 (reliability 3), which gave [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) 40% to 34% and [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) 45% to 43%, [empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico). Lula is identical in both readings, with all disagreement concentrated on Flávio.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) gives Lula a 60.50% probability of victory, while the most recent poll, PoderData from July 16, places the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) in [Empate técnico](/en/glossary#empate-tecnico) (45% to 43%). The margin that real money prices is larger than what the freshest ballot measures, and this level distance is the central tension of the panel.

> **Market × ballot, in the third way:** Renan Santos is worth 8.65% in the winner market, the third largest price in the book, against a ceiling of 6% in the best poll it has ever received and 3% in the most reliable one of the week. It is the portrait of a name that the market prices as a tail bet well above what declared voting intention sustains.

> **Expectation × intention:** [Quaest](/en/glossary#quaest) disclosed that 55% believe in Lula's victory and 25% in Flávio's, a perception measure that echoes the market's direction, but which should not be confused with voting intention, where the distance between the two is much smaller.
