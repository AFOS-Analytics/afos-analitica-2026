## 1. Prediction market

**The gap narrows.** In the confirmed reading of October 11 at 17:33 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 86.65% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 19.50M) and 12.50% for Lula (USD 22.21M). Against the confirmed reading of October 10 at 19:19 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro falls 1.50pp and Lula rises 1.00pp; since the sum of the pair went from 99.65 to 99.15, the normalized movement is 1.07pp to each side. The gap goes from 76.65pp to 74.15pp. The total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 180.19M in the 17:54 BRT reading, and the largest volume in the book is still Lula's.

**Supreme Court and inflation.** The contract on the [impeachment of an STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 3.35% (USD 0.10M) in the confirmed reading of 17:33 BRT, 0.05pp below the 3.40% of October 10. In the [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) one, the 5.00% to 5.49% range leads with 44.65% (USD 25 thousand), ahead of the 4.50% to 4.99% range, with 36.20% (USD 32 thousand), in the 17:54 BRT reading.

## 3. What the press covered

That same afternoon, the Electoral Justice inspector-general, Ricardo Villas Bôas Cueva, [suspended the decision and reinstated the channel](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMivAFBVV95cUxQSFBCQ0ptR0JuVHNZZ1BFaE9TdGFEV0g0aUlYWUhWQlhmcTlYa05HNU9yQThzR0xFX2JkRkNobE5YR1M4Q1ViX3JVeTZIMkdLRnJSR3JzZEZVTTZoWXRRX3dtbm1aUW1mb3A5NDlrVUc0N29IQWVCanBVdTJQbWQ2WnNsckdIeHBFNnhYNWVIYmdKQVhIZ1VOeTdpVW5POHJkU3kya0ZXRlJiNFhKVm0tajhBX2FQNU9CM3JNYQ?oc=5), in a preliminary ruling that goes to the TSE full court.
