## 1. Prediction market

Prices are from the confirmed reading of September 1, 8:34 PM BRT, and total accumulated volume comes to **USD 141.39M** in the [presidential book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election). Lula stands at **55.50% (USD 9.56M)** and has not left that number for the fourth day running. Flávio Bolsonaro gave up **0.10pp, to 38.95% (USD 9.43M)**, after ceding 2.05pp on August 31. The [distance between the two](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) widened to **16.55pp**, against 16.45pp on August 31.

The names below moved. Renan Santos rose **0.70pp, to 2.55% (USD 12.30M)**, the largest move in the presidential contract this round, on the same day the ruling that was blocking his campaign was reversed. Augusto Cury rose **0.50pp, to 2.35% (USD 3.50M)**, against the last published price, from August 30. Ronaldo Caiado, at **0.25% (USD 7.03M)**, remains below the 0.5% floor and has no new price this round.

In the placement contracts, [second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) took Flávio Bolsonaro to **86.00% (USD 0.51M)**, up 0.50pp. In [third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Augusto Cury took the lead with **46.65% (USD 0.10M)**, against 30.00% for Renan Santos.

The panel's largest move is not a candidate's. The contract on a [Supreme Court justice leaving through impeachment before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) went from 3.60% to **10.85% (USD 86 thousand)**, up **7.25pp**. The size of the book changes the reading: it is the panel's smallest, and accumulated volume went from USD 84 thousand to USD 86 thousand, that is, some **USD 2 thousand** of new business accompanies the swing. Within the day the price passed through 3.50% and 19.10%. In the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), the PL stands at **78.50% (USD 0.26M)**. ## 3. What the press covered

The fact of September 1 is the lifting of secrecy, not a new investigation. The messages between banker Daniel Vorcaro and Justice Alexandre de Moraes are from November 16, 2025; Vorcaro has been in custody since November 17, 2025, and Banco Master was liquidated on November 18. The Federal Police report was delivered on August 27, and on September 1 Justice André Mendonça lifted the case's secrecy, according to [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/vorcaro-pediu-a-moraes-encontro-com-galipolo-para-resolver-tudo/). The content surfaced on the night of August 31, in a Metrópoles column. The document indicates that Moraes edited a contract worth BRL 131 million between his wife's law firm and the businessman, plus a second contract worth BRL 50 million, according to the [same report](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/vorcaro-pediu-a-moraes-encontro-com-galipolo-para-resolver-tudo/).

The reaction came from both sides. The prosecutor general asked for the report to be annulled, and presidential candidates demanded the justice's departure, among them [Ronaldo Caiado](https://www.metropoles.com/sao-paulo/moraes-x-vorcaro-caiado-pede-afastamento-de-ministro-do-stf-conivencia) and [Romeu Zema](https://valor.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/01/romeu-zema-defende-impeachment-e-prisao-de-alexandre-de-moraes.ghtml). The Senate president [signalled to allies that he will not schedule impeachment](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/09/01/alcolumbre-sinaliza-a-aliados-que-nao-cedera-a-novos-apelos-para-pautar-impeachment-de-moraes-apos-novas-mensagens-de-vorcaro.ghtml), and a senator predicted a [flood of requests](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiuAFBVV95cUxOX1lfbmJZSlh4ci1tV214LTYtUDdBR0pkRC1WaVpfRHhEa3NBTW95X1JxUXV5OTQ1a3FNMlNwU1k4bW5ORWtqeUUyTHVsU2I1NHd4c3lXdUZXb0NlM3QybUdVRmpCeC1YRnRhZVlidG1uUVBDUmRiRFpIOERoaVdHcDdhb1hodVUwaVFBaXA2WWVULU1GM0U2Q0RUaDRDVGFoWU03T0ZGMVNfc2M2ZlZ3Z2k3Q3h6UW9W?oc=5). The [joint committee on the pension agency requested an investigation into the same contract](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMijwFBVV95cUxPZ0xPeXFRcTVoSkFnVG1VNHNLbUc4bDRTdlhnaE1wNEN1d0hoMFB3NWprRF81OEJiV0lQbWpVdmY5Tk8tVjlsRW5McHlIS2M0RzY4aU5LVm9NOFZfXzVSaXk0aXNyTnA4NENxQUhKUnRJTHhTMW85TERmblF2WDVRNTlzUTJLMFFvbGVhb1Ezcw?oc=5), which for the first time ties together the two investigations the panel had been following in parallel.

In the electoral contest, there were rulings on both sides. Dias Toffoli [backed down and released Renan Santos's digital campaign](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/toffoli-recua-e-libera-campanha-eleitoral-de-renan-santos-nas-redes.shtml), the fund transfers and the right to debate, reversing the August 30 ruling. And André Mendonça [suspended Lula's advertising that used the "Rap do Silva"](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/eleicoes/andre-mendonca-suspende-propaganda-de-lula-com-rap-do-silva-por-nao-apresentar-alckmin-como-vice/) for not presenting Alckmin as running mate, a ruling also [reported by CartaCapital](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/politica/mendonca-proibe-lula-de-voltar-a-exibir-rap-do-silva-sem-nome-de-alckmin/).
