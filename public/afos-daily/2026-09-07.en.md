## 1. Prediction market

The [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) closed the confirmed reading of September 7, at 13:02 BRT, with **Lula at 57.50%** (USD 9.92M accumulated) and **Flávio Bolsonaro at 39.45%** (USD 9.80M), a rise of 1.00pp and a fall of 0.25pp against the confirmed reading of September 6. Total accumulated volume in the presidential market comes to **USD 144.60M**. Renan Santos, at 1.55% (USD 12.76M), gave up 0.05pp, and Augusto Cury, at 1.15% (USD 4.19M), did not move.

In the [third-place book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) of the first round, **Augusto Cury rose 2.35pp to 58.55%** (USD 126 thousand) and **Renan Santos gave up 1.50pp to 24.50%** (USD 279 thousand). Ronaldo Caiado gave up 0.50pp to 9.50% (USD 115 thousand), Pablo Marçal 0.40pp to 1.85% (USD 48 thousand), and Romeu Zema stayed at 0.85% (USD 51 thousand).

Three [books](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) received no new price and keep the values of the previous round: the runner-up one, with Flávio Bolsonaro at 89.50% (USD 625 thousand), the Supreme Court impeachment one, at 8.60% (USD 88 thousand), and the Senate majority one, with the PL at 82.50% (USD 261 thousand). AFOS publishes only prices that two independent readings confirm, and records when there is none.

## 3. What the press covered

September 7 split the agenda into two stages. Lula took part in the [parade in Brasília](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/desfile-de-7-de-setembro-em-brasilia-reune-lula-fachin-alcolumbre-e-hugo-motta/) alongside Edson Fachin, of the Supreme Court, Davi Alcolumbre, of the Senate, and Hugo Motta, of the lower house, and made a national address on sovereignty, saying the country ["is not and will not be anyone's colony"](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqgFBVV95cUxORkFVNmpuNkszd05lZlZxRXAzM3Y2cmw0dTJYbk9vbUstTlN5UmgxakdNdlR4VGFuUGpXYkpjaC1wZnNBS09sWGFuLXBRT3RBZGxQT1Y1N2I5Z2J4ZGxnQ3p5aEl2eF9UVk9fVUhWNVpRM1NqRm9Vc0loQi1rbW9UdUxNVEhWOElZOW40SHd1YmcwSDVnZTV3TDRscXg0VXJDdmgwdllSVjFyQQ?oc=5).

Flávio Bolsonaro did not attend the parade and [took part in a church service alongside Tarcísio de Freitas](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/09/07/tarcisio-troca-desfile-de-7-de-setembro-por-culto-ao-lado-de-flavio-bolsonaro.ghtml), who also missed it, according to O Globo and [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-eleicoes-2026/tarcisio-falta-a-desfile-de-7-de-setembro-e-vai-a-culto-com-flavio/). [Michelle Bolsonaro stayed away from the rally](https://veja.abril.com.br/brasil/michelle-fica-fora-do-ato-de-7-de-setembro-ao-lado-de-flavio/), according to VEJA.

The Supreme Court crisis was the theme of the street rallies. Romeu Zema [displayed a panel showing Alexandre de Moraes behind bars](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/eleicoes/2026/zema-exibe-painel-com-moraes-atras-das-grades-em-ato-na-paulista/) on Avenida Paulista and [called for a change in how justices are appointed](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/09/07/na-paulista-zema-critica-ministros-do-stf-e-volta-a-defender-indicacoes-ao-orgao-por-lista-triplice.ghtml) and [judicial reform](https://valor.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/09/07/zema-faz-protesto-contra-stf-na-avenida-paulista-e-defende-reforma-do-judicirio.ghtml). In Brasília, the right-wing rally carried [an effigy of Moraes in prison](https://www.metropoles.com/distrito-federal/ato-da-direita-em-brasilia-tem-boneco-de-moraes-preso-e-cartaz-contra-o-pt) and gathered [candidates from Novo](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-eleicoes-2026/com-candidatos-do-novo-direita-faz-ato-contra-stf-em-brasilia/).

Augusto Cury held a rally in Copacabana, [nodded to justice André Mendonça](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/09/07/em-ato-em-copacabana-augusto-cury-faz-aceno-a-ministro-andre-mendonca-do-stf.ghtml) and [criticised the polarisation](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi0gFBVV95cUxOSlluVWRUSWNtT1BLWGxiNzFTQ1plcjFfNnR2UWE2b0k4bWFhUUhDcHNndXYzLUhGMXlCR3d2ZllOTjFQeENFUWRTeENST3FoYXk5ZDQtZm5ydUNaeUpWZk5pcjBkRzlXbzRJU3ljaS1MZ0kwZXUzSEhzdDd2SUd0Y0lkc1B1TmpBRlJ0SDJlNWZwMEpwRlZRRjhteG44QnFEMU1fTy1ORWdmRmkzMktJWnhNdXVCakl3TXM0SllwS1haMmo4ZGVmRGs2TFQ2Rzh1RVE?oc=5) between the top two. ## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x poll:** the [most recent poll](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiiAFBVV95cUxOQWtjMXYwVHlrdDJWaWdwMjZuR1BweFhOdFlOLU5zMWU0blVpbXRVeDl0UV9PQS1Mc3VFVmVxQVJpWk4tUG51WGpxbHFMNFhpR2xYRW45aVo0dmt6Y3JiQ0VRTmZuSDBTYlVqbTYweUtaa25oZ2xOcnl0dFhEVFlNdy1xTGRiMmhh?oc=5) hands the runoff to Flávio Bolsonaro by 5.4 points, and the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) rose 1.00pp for Lula over the same window. The two quantities do not subtract from one another, because one is declared voting intention in a pairing and the other is the implied probability of winning the election. AFOS records that today they rank the race in opposite ways, without arbitrating.

> **Poll x poll:** three national houses tested the same pairing in five days and two hand the runoff to Lula, by 2 and by 1 point, while the third hands it to his opponent by 5.4. Dispersion between [institutes registered with the TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) is wider than the distance measured inside any one of them.

> **Street x price:** September 7 put the Supreme Court crisis at the centre of the [campaign rallies](https://www.metropoles.com/distrito-federal/ato-da-direita-em-brasilia-tem-boneco-de-moraes-preso-e-cartaz-contra-o-pt), in two capitals, and the [impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) before 2027 received no confirmed new price, holding at 8.60%.

## In summary

1. **The two measurements ranked the race in opposite ways for a second day, and by a wider distance.** [Veritá](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiiAFBVV95cUxOQWtjMXYwVHlrdDJWaWdwMjZuR1BweFhOdFlOLU5zMWU0blVpbXRVeDl0UV9PQS1Mc3VFVmVxQVJpWk4tUG51WGpxbHFMNFhpR2xYRW45aVo0dmt6Y3JiQ0VRTmZuSDBTYlVqbTYweUtaa25oZ2xOcnl0dFhEVFlNdy1xTGRiMmhh?oc=5) published the runoff of the September 1 to 4 fieldwork round: 48.5% for Flávio Bolsonaro against 43.1% for Lula, a difference of 5.4 points that does not fit inside the survey's own margin of 2. Over the same stretch the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) rose 1.00pp for Lula and widened the gap from 16.80pp to 18.05pp. On September 6 the divergence was 0.5 point in the first round, today it is 5.4 in the runoff, and the price moved the same way both times.

2. **The third-place book changed protagonist, and that is where the mass moved.** Augusto Cury rose 2.35pp to 58.55%, a second consecutive gain, while Renan Santos gave up 1.50pp to 24.50%, breaking three gains. The two moved in the [same market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) in opposite directions, and in the contract on winning the election Cury did not move from 1.15%: the market is consolidating him as the third-placed finisher, not as a candidate to win.

3. **Three of the five books were left without a new price, and the calendar ahead is the fullest of the window.** Runner-up, Supreme Court impeachment and Senate majority keep the values of the previous round. On the other side, ten national polls [registered with the TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) are scheduled for release between September 8 and 11, among them a Veritá of 40,500 interviews and a Datafolha, and today's Quaest published no figures. Four days should bring more measurement than the whole week just past.
