## 1. Prediction market

The day's movement came from the third-place tier. Ronaldo Caiado jumped to [2.60% (USD 3.70M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) (↑1.15pp in 24h), the book's largest relative gain, repriced after a national poll showed him tying with Lula in the [second round](/en/glossary#segundo-turno). At the top, Flávio Bolsonaro rose to [29.20% (USD 6.17M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) (↑0.85pp), and as Lula remained stable at [40.50% (USD 6.03M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), the gap between the two NARROWED to +11.3pp (versus +12.15pp the previous day).

Fernando Haddad rose to [6.10% (USD 5.32M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) (↑0.80pp), in fine market noise with no direct presidential event. Renan Santos remained stable at [16.80% (USD 6.44M)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), maintaining second place in the market, and Zema moved sideways at 2.75–2.80%. In position markets, Flávio remains the leader for second place and Renan is the favorite for third.

The institutional highlight was the Senate: [PL](/en/glossary#pl) jumped to [77.50% in the race for most seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won) (↑9.5pp), a large move that, given the relatively thin book of the sub-market, calls for cautious reading. The [STF impeach](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) remained stable at 5.50% (USD 80k), with no repricing.

Today's technical reading: money reacted to the new poll by pricing upward the [third option that appeared competitive (Caiado)](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), while the top matchup adjusted marginally. The presidential gap in the market remains wider than what has been registered in polls.
