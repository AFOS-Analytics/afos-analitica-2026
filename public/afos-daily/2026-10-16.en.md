## 1. Prediction market

**The gap narrows for the fourth day.** In the confirmed reading of October 16 at 17:01 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 76.45% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 21.23M) and 23.50% for Lula (USD 25.11M). Against the confirmed reading of October 15 at 17:37 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro falls 0.80pp and Lula rises 1.00pp; since the sum of the pair went from 99.75 to 99.95, the normalized movement is 0.96pp to each side. The gap goes from 54.75pp to 52.95pp, after going from 72.95pp to 70.50pp on October 14 and from 70.50pp to 54.75pp on October 15. The total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 184.80M, and the largest volume in the book is still Lula's.

**Supreme Court and inflation.** The contract on the [impeachment of an STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 2.70% (USD 0.10M), the same as the October 15 reading. In the [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) one, in the 17:02 BRT reading against the October 15 one at 19:31 BRT, the 5.00% to 5.49% range goes from 51.10% to 60.80% (USD 27 thousand), the 5.50% to 5.99% range from 20.40% to 18.40% (USD 21 thousand) and the 4.50% to 4.99% range from 17.40% to 17.00% (USD 32 thousand).

> **Market × poll:** the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 76.45% for Flávio Bolsonaro, and the day's three national polls measure him between a tie and 1.5 points ahead, within margins of 1.8 to 2.2 points.
