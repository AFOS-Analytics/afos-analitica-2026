## 1. Prediction market

Prices come from the confirmed reading of August 24, 6:25 PM BRT, and the **total accumulated volume adds up to USD 132.34M**. **Lula closed at 62.50% (USD 8.92M) with no change at all** and **Flávio Bolsonaro rose 1.80pp to 35.05% (USD 8.84M)**, the largest gain of the day. The distance fell from 29.25pp to **27.45pp**, and the entire compression came from the second-place gain, not from a loss by the leader.

Both superlatives were checked against the series that begins on April 14, and neither is an extreme. The 35.05% is his highest value **since May 13**, against a peak of 45.50% on May 6, and the 27.45pp is the narrowest distance **since June 21** on the daily close. On May 5 that distance was **negative by 7.80pp**, with him ahead: narrowing, in this pair, is nothing new.

In the third way, **Renan Santos gave up 0.55pp to 2.90% (USD 10.94M)**, with accumulated volume larger than the leader's own, and **Ronaldo Caiado rose 0.15pp to 0.70% (USD 6.55M)**. In the **third-place contract** both gave up 2.00pp, to **54.00% (USD 0.19M) against 39.00% (USD 0.06M)**; in the **runner-up contract, Flávio Bolsonaro pays 85.50% (USD 0.37M)**, down 1.00pp. **Romeu Zema is at 0.30% (USD 5.93M)**, below the 0.5% floor at which the double reading confirms variation. The institutional contracts did not move: **Supreme Court at 3.35% (USD 84 thousand)** and **Senate with the Liberal Party at 77.50% (USD 0.26M)**. In **inflation**, the 5.00% to 5.49% band pays **50.25% (USD 15 thousand)**, with the ten bands summing to 104.95%, inside the 95% to 105% gate.

## 2. What the institutes recorded

**BTG/Nexus** is the first new national poll since August 21: released at 6:36 AM, registration BR-09028/2026, fieldwork August 21 to 23, 2,006 telephone interviews and a 2-point margin, it measures the [runoff at 46% to 45%](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiqgFBVV95cUxPZ2Z5dEhBMkdJbzZwZlNUWTVOZVExdTlxZGJYWTZxNnNUYVFwN1JrQURnNkY1OG1RREpodWZsUlFXaHJDZXRSNVBjYzM2SnJsS3FaVlBXN0VUUVhvN1YyRk1YREFsNnpLTUxXNXNMSERvell5QlVUZjZoaFJaZlZKSDZ0N19ZSGFPR05hR29sTkdaVFN3WnpoV19XY2gzU09CeUd4QnA4SFBPQQ?oc=5) for Lula. In the first round there are two scenarios, and the difference between them is Pablo Marçal: without him, Lula 41% and Flávio Bolsonaro 37%; with him, 40% and 34%, with Marçal at 4%. Caiado has 5%, Renan Santos and Zema 3% each.

The runoff distance fell from 3 points to 1 against August 17, but **that 1 point ties what BTG/Nexus measured on August 3**, and today's 41% to 37% repeats that day. The runoff series reads 4 points on July 27, 1 on August 3, 3 on August 10 and 17, and 1 now. A second caveat: fieldwork closed on August 23 and the debate was at 8 PM that day, so **the poll does not measure the debate**. In the same wave, [48% approve and 49% disapprove](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMitAFBVV95cUxPWURBYnQxTF9JWC1vZHExRFJWMmJkR0VEU3p2Zy1IWnMyeGd2eElQNHQ0Q29oT2dINFJBUGdhOWN2N2FwMEJ2cnU5b2VMN1UwNHMxRjVaSVBCX2J3S0NVQzUxUXNJcVRiSnJiNHhSQ0k5d0psWEpURXJSRzdmVzVKMEg4aFhwbXpJWUVLb0VXQUhCSVlfN2tmUkE1M2o1dEFrdHVHSmgtT0k2aWk4NkpJTlRwYXQ?oc=5) of the government, [43% rate it poor or terrible against 35% excellent or good](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMipgFBVV95cUxQTkhZcFZUei1LSU9fWUVJUmFhbDBFX0p2bVpCdjVEZ3RueGpHeEVSS2ZkX2ZpUEpBckFqRk8yTEFFOGlSdHRYQ2NQcUpzcC1tZW1TaTNUeU12UXlxYWphS2dHRHo1NXFrVkREdjZCSHhfUkVveVZ1NGpkUjBSMUFhQVNPUGt6SGpkZFhKNFVydnpOMGZ3OXBKQ0FjVzFESmxzRWpUN1R3?oc=5), and rejection stands at 49% for Lula against 48% for Flávio Bolsonaro: the leader is also the most rejected. ## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll, this time in convergence:** the two moved in the same direction on the same day, which is unusual here. AFOS records the direction and does not perform the subtraction: 35.05% is a probability of winning and 45% is voting intention, quantities that are not comparable by difference.

> **Poll × itself:** the tightening the coverage described compares today only with August 17. Against the house's series, runoff and first round **repeat August 3**.

> **Market × ballot box in third place:** the inversion continues and widened. At the ballot box, Ronaldo Caiado has 5% against 3% for Renan Santos; in the third-place contract the price does the opposite, with 54.00% for Renan Santos against 39.00% for Caiado.

## In summary

1. **The movement of the day came from one side only.** Lula still at 62.50% and Flávio Bolsonaro up 1.80pp to 35.05%, his highest value since May 13, with the gap at 27.45pp, the narrowest since June 21.

2. **The poll agrees with the price, and its own series imposes the caveat:** the runoff at 46% to 45% and the first round at 41% to 37% tie with August 3.

3. **Two institutional rulings entered with checked dates:** the São Paulo electoral court rejected the appeals of Pablo Marçal and of the electoral prosecution service on August 21, and BRB, on August 24, authorised civil action without holding anyone liable.
