The full series shows that point was the floor of a band that stood at 55.00% at 3:01 PM and returned to 56.00% at 9 PM the same day. The capture guard proves a price is not in transit; it does not prove the price is the level.

> **Market × poll:** the polling ruler has been still for two days and so has the price. Comparing the two on a day like this measures the stability of both instruments, not the race.
