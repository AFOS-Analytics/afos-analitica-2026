## 1. Prediction market

In the [presidential contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), **Lula** closed the confirmed reading of August 19, 2:58 PM BRT, at **63.50% (USD 8.69M)**, unchanged for a second day running, and **Flávio Bolsonaro** at **32.55% (USD 8.63M)**, up 0.50pp and a sixth consecutive day of gains. The distance between them narrowed 0.50pp to **30.95pp**, a third day running of convergence: it was 37.05pp on August 16, 33.05pp on the 17th and 31.45pp on the 18th, that is 6.10pp in three days. Total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to **USD 129.21M**.

In two days Lula lost no price at all: the entire convergence came from the runner-up. No superlative: 32.55% is not a record, because of the 88 days in the series since May 22 only 2 posted a value equal to or above it, and the high is still 33.20%, from June 2.

**Ronaldo Caiado** gave back what he had gained the previous day, from 0.80% to **0.55% (USD 6.35M)**, and in the [third place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) he gave ground from 37.00% to **36.00%**. **Renan Santos** fell to **4.05% (USD 10.14M)** in the presidential book, but stayed at **57.00%** in that same placement contract: the distance between them there widened from 20.00pp to 21.00pp, and the one who widened it was Caiado giving ground.

**Pablo Marçal** marks **0.75% (USD 1.72M)**, with volume rising from USD 1.66M in one day, and **Romeu Zema** stays at 0.15%. In the [second place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) there is no new reading to publish on August 19.

The [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stayed flat at **3.40% (USD 84 thousand)** and the PL marks **78.50%** in the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), against 16.50% for the MDB.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** the price narrowed 6.10pp in three days and the latest national poll measured the distance between the top two unchanged at 5 points. BTG/Nexus of August 17 lifted Lula and Flávio Bolsonaro by 1 point each, which leaves the difference intact and fits inside the house's own 2-point margin. A measurement that does not narrow cannot explain a narrowing.

> **Price × price, on placement:** the distance between Renan Santos and Ronaldo Caiado in the third place contract widened from 20.00pp to 21.00pp without Renan moving at all. He stayed at 57.00% and the one who gave ground was Caiado. A move in distance does not always have two authors.

> **Price × ballot box, on who is third:** BTG/Nexus gives Caiado 5% and Renan Santos 4%. In the third place contract the market does the opposite, and by a wide margin, with Renan at 57.00% against 36.00%.
