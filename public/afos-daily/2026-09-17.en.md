## 1. Prediction market

The [presidential election winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) widened the spread for a second day, this time with only one side moving. In the confirmed reading of September 17, at 13:21 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro stands at 54.70% (USD 10.88M), 0.95pp above the 53.75% of September 16, and Lula remains at 44.50% (USD 11.19M). The distance went from 9.25pp to 10.20pp, with total presidential volume at USD 150.87M accumulated.

Flávio sits 0.40pp below his series high, of 55.10% on September 10. Renan Santos gave up 0.20pp, to 1.15% (USD 13.60M), below the lowest value of the recorded readings in his series, and Augusto Cury 0.10pp, to 0.15% (USD 4.95M). The largest accumulated volume in the book is still Tarcísio de Freitas's contract, USD 14.08M, flat at 0.05%.

The day's biggest move is in the [first-round third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place). Renan Santos rose 7.50pp, to 44.00% (USD 324 thousand), and passed Augusto Cury, who fell 2.60pp, to 42.90% (USD 215 thousand). Ronaldo Caiado gave up 3.00pp, to 12.50% (USD 133 thousand). In the [runner-up contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio rose 1.00pp, to 76.50% (USD 890 thousand), and Lula gave up 1.20pp, to 22.65% (USD 780 thousand).

The [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 5.05% (USD 91 thousand), 0.20pp above the previous day, with USD 199 of new money in the book. In the [Senate contract](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won) the Liberal Party stands at 84.00% (USD 261 thousand), 0.50pp above September 16.

## 2. What the polling houses recorded

[AtlasIntel](https://www.brasildefato.com.br/2026/09/17/atlasbloomberg-lula-mantem-vantagem-sobre-flavio-no-1o-turno/), with Bloomberg, surveyed 5,018 voters online from September 11 to 16, margin of 1pp and register BR-06221/2026. In the first round Lula has 44.1% and Flávio 41.7%, and the distance within the house narrowed from 5.6 to 2.4 points since September 10. In the runoff Flávio has 47.2% and Lula 46.8%, against 46.4% to 46.2% in the previous round. Lula wins the other four pairs, and government approval is 45.4% against 53.6% disapproval, according to [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/eleicoes/2026/pesquisa-eleitoral-2026/atlasintel-presidente-setembro-2026-2/) and [CartaCapital](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/politica/lula-e-flavio-bolsonaro-seguem-empatados-em-2o-turno-mostra-atlasintel/).

[PoderData](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/eleicoes/poderdata-aya-flavio-tem-46-no-2o-turno-lula-44/) conducted 3,000 automated telephone interviews from September 13 to 16, margin of 1.8pp and register BR-00360/2026. The runoff gives Flávio 46% and Lula 44%, the same 2 points as September 10, and the first round Lula 37% and Flávio 36%, with Cury at 8%, according to CNN Brasil and [CartaCapital](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/politica/pesquisa-poderdata-mostra-empate-entre-lula-e-flavio-em-1o-e-2o-turnos/). [Gerp](https://exame.com/brasil/pesquisa-gerp-flavio-bolsonaro-tem-40-e-lula-37-no-1o-turno/) surveyed 2,400 voters by telephone from September 14 to 16, margin of 2pp and register BR-00535/2026. It gives the first round to Flávio by 40% to 37% and the runoff by 50% to 43%, the same 7 points as September 9, according to Exame and [CNN Brasil](https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/eleicoes/gerp-flavio-tem-50-no-2o-turno-lula-43/).

The Superior Electoral Court register also set Datafolha and 100 Cidades for September 17, and this briefing does not carry numbers from either.

## 3. What the press covered

The president of the Supreme Court, Edson Fachin, [removed from the September 23 agenda](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/fachin-adia-julgamento-do-pedido-para-investigar-mendonca/) the judgment of the request to investigate André Mendonça's conduct as rapporteur of the Master case, without setting a new date, according to Poder360 and [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/fachin-desmarca-sessao-stf-analisaria-pedido-moraes-contra-mendonca/). The analysis on investigating Alexandre de Moraes remains stalled on Flávio Dino's review request of September 15, which has a deadline of up to 90 days.

The dispute between justices moved to social media. Gilmar Mendes wrote on X that Mendonça acted as a ["campaign puppet" and "electoral pixuleco"](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/gilmar-defende-gonet-e-chama-mendonca-de-pixuleco-eleitoral/) and defended Prosecutor General Paulo Gonet, according to Poder360. Mendonça [replied in a statement](https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/politica/2026/09/7502545-mendonca-rebate-criticas-e-defende-publicidade-de-decisoes-no-caso-master.html) that lifting the secrecy was a reasoned decision within his authority and defended an ethics code for the Supreme Court, according to Correio Braziliense.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Poll x poll:** in the runoff between Flávio and Lula, PoderData repeats 2 points, Gerp repeats 7 and AtlasIntel goes from 0.2 to 0.4. The distance between the houses, from 0.4 to 7 points, is larger than the movement of any of them against its own previous round. In PoderData and AtlasIntel the pair fits inside the margin; in Gerp, it does not.

> **Market x poll:** the third-place book put Renan Santos ahead of Augusto Cury on the same day that only one of the three national polls agrees with that order. AtlasIntel gives 5.1% to 3.5% for Renan; PoderData gives 8% to 3% and Gerp 7% to 2% for Cury. The order between the releases and the price is not measured.

> **Narrative x price:** the Supreme Court was left without a date for both requests to investigate justices, and the impeachment contract rose 0.20pp with USD 199 of new money. A price that moves with almost no volume is a reading of the book, not of the judgment.

## In summary

**The spread widened with only one side moving.** Flávio rose 0.95pp, to 54.70%, 0.40pp from the series high, and Lula stayed at 44.50%. The distance went to 10.20pp.

**Three national polls, three stable distances.** AtlasIntel, PoderData and Gerp put Flávio ahead in the runoff by 0.4, 2 and 7 points, each close to what it measured in its previous round. The movement in the polls is in the AtlasIntel first round, where Lula's lead fell from 5.6 to 2.4 points.

**The Supreme Court postponed the other side of the crisis.** Fachin took the judgment on Mendonça off the agenda, and Gilmar and Mendonça traded public accusations on the same day.
