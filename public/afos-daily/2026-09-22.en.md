## 1. Prediction market

In the [outright winner contract on Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Flávio Bolsonaro stands at 59.55% (USD 11.50M) and Lula at 40.50% (USD 11.87M), in the reading confirmed on September 22 at 14:40 BRT. The gap is 19.05pp, against 22.40pp in the reading confirmed on September 21 at 17:01 BRT. Accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 153.62M across 19 contracts.

The two moves are not the same size, and the reason is arithmetic. The sum of the pair went from 99.40 to 100.05, so raw Lula rises 2.00pp and Flávio Bolsonaro gives up 1.35pp, and normalized each side moves exactly 1.75pp. The gap closes 3.35pp by the raw count and 3.49pp by the normalized one, and a move that survives normalization describes the race, not the book's spread.

Neither price is an extreme: Flávio Bolsonaro's stands 2.15pp below the series high, 61.70% recorded on September 21. And the two books moved in opposite directions on the same day: in the [first-round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) [second-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio Bolsonaro rises 1.00pp, to 69.50% (USD 1.06M), and Lula rises 0.40pp, to 29.80% (USD 0.89M).

In the [third-place book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan Santos gave up 3.00pp, to 42.50% (USD 0.39M), the largest fall of any contract on the panel this round, and Augusto Cury gave up 0.80pp, to 39.45% (USD 0.27M). The distance between the two shrank for the second round running: 19.95pp, then 5.25pp, now 3.05pp. Ronaldo Caiado got no new price, at 15.50% (USD 0.16M), and keeps third place.

The [contract on a Supreme Court justice leaving by impeachment](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) before 2027 remains at 4.30% (USD 0.09M), with no new price, 21.80pp below the series high. In the Senate book this round publishes no new price.

## 3. What the press covered

At the Supreme Court the day was about case assignment and petitions. Luiz Fux [was drawn as rapporteur](https://www.metropoles.com/colunas/manoela-alcantara/fux-e-sorteado-relator-de-acao-em-caso-que-envolve-mulher-de-moraes) of a writ of mandamus asking the Senate for entry and exit records of Viviane Barci de Moraes and Daniel Vorcaro. The case was reassigned after Alexandre de Moraes, drawn earlier, declared himself disqualified because it involves his own wife.

André Mendonça's brief became public on September 22 and the act is from the 21st: on the last day of the deadline set by Edson Fachin, he filed 76 pages [asking for an investigation into Moraes](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/mendonca-pede-investigacao-de-moraes-por-abuso-de-autoridade-e-denunciacao-caluniosa/) for abuse of authority and false accusation, and for summary dismissal of the inquiry against him, [denying the charges](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/coluna-do-estadao/mendonca-responde-a-moraes-e-diz-que-colega-adotou-metodos-ilegais-para-acusa-lo-veja-ponto-a-ponto/). The request to revoke Vorcaro's arrest has been with Fachin since September 18, with no decision.

Outside the Supreme Court there was an electoral act. The Superior Electoral Court [accepted the Lula campaign's petition](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiswFBVV95cUxNa1FIU0tpLUk1VDF0YXBreHlxU1R1d3MzZldHT3ljTTdTVDNqQ21FdzZPWThnVXp0Vm9Rc0FNdERGdllCb2wtR3N4SDhUVnAxWF9ZNGFmVWloaDIxWkRBeFZWdnl0OUpnUjFmNlcwRkR0MzEyOVQzaGFyVUpURFh4WHpvdDN5eW5XcGxxUm5fZFhFclRXNmVPemVKYU1GOE5mZy1HdjhteVNRbDdnQWN2bjFUbw?oc=5) pointing to a network of Instagram profiles boosting content about Flávio Bolsonaro through co-authored posts. The rapporteur [only ordered the case to proceed](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMivgFBVV95cUxOWllfOUpDN1ZlQ3IxTjF3OHVQMHU5LTgtTzRqUW1Qcl9fVzBDWEJkYVdfZUhFLTZwVk1paHgxVS1mQ3ZLLVNaaTI4QjNsOExVYl9QclNERHB1VkxpNVp1NGtBc1l0eW5VWkhIQjRKLV9waDREVm5kQnNJNVptRm93NDZ1bTFsTFA2YzA2bXFjMmd6LVdjN0FTVFlCcU9ObmRsQWJNeEhyZVZCSGIxT1d2LU1QeWxQZjZ6OXhuTEZ3?oc=5) and did not conclude that there was any irregularity, with five days for the defense to respond.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × poll:** the price gives 59.55% to Flávio Bolsonaro and 40.50% to Lula, a gap of 19.05pp. The three polls with fieldwork closing on September 20 measure distances between 4 points in Lula's favor and 1 point in Flávio Bolsonaro's favor. The two instruments do not measure the same thing, but the order of the names is the same question in both.

> **Poll × poll:** the divergence between firms is wider on the third way than at the top. Renan Santos has 11% at Palver and 3% at Quaest and BTG/Nexus, eight points apart between firms that interviewed people up to the same day.

> **Raw × normalized:** the same day produces two numbers for the same move, a 2.00pp rise for Lula raw and 1.75pp normalized, because the sum of the pair moves on its own. Whoever reads only the raw figure credits the candidate with part of what belonged to the book.

## In summary

**The gap gave back in one day part of what it had opened the day before, and the move is real.** It was 3.35pp raw and 3.49pp normalized, and surviving normalization is the test that separates price from spread.

**The third national poll from the same fieldwork did not resolve the disagreement, it widened the picture of it.** Two firms out of three put Lula ahead in the first round, and the one that disagrees at the top is the same one that disagrees on the third way.

**Nothing was decided at the Supreme Court on September 22.** What happened was a rapporteur drawing and a petition filed the day before, and the impeachment contract got no new price.
