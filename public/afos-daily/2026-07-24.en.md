## 1. Prediction market

At 72 days until the [first round](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno), the total accumulated volume in the presidential market amounts to approximately **USD 115.55M** ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) via AFOS Analytics proxy, fetched July 24, 17:31 BRT). After three trading sessions locked at 60.50%, **Lula** broke through the range and closed at **61.50% (USD 7.63M)**, his highest value since July 15. **Flávio Bolsonaro** remained virtually flat at **22.95% (USD 7.64M)**, up 0.10pp after losing 2.10pp the previous day. The gap between the two rose to **+38.55pp**, the largest since July 9, when it marked 38.60pp, and remains below the cycle peak of +39.5pp recorded on July 3.

The difference relative to [July 23](/en/daily/2026-07-23) is one of nature, not magnitude. On that day the gap widened by 2.10pp because the challenger fell; on this Friday it widened by 0.90pp because the frontrunner rose, with Flávio unchanged. No triggering event is identified at the time of the movement, so the correct reading is market momentum, and this synthesis does not attribute the increase to any specific news ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), series measured in 22 and 90-day windows).

In the field, price shifted from alternatives to the frontrunner, with one exception ([Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election)). **Renan Santos** remained virtually flat at **11.75% (USD 8.45M)**, the highest individual volume among the competitive names, above those of Lula and Flávio. **Ronaldo Caiado** gave back the previous day's gain and fell to **1.75% (USD 5.11M)**. **Jair Bolsonaro** settled at **1.05% (USD 5.23M)** and **Michelle Bolsonaro**, who is not a declared pre-candidate, retreated to **0.85% (USD 9.23M)**. The exception was **Romeu Zema**, whose price more than doubled, from 0.45% to **0.95% (USD 4.52M)**, the largest relative variation in the book, with no new poll behind it. **Fernando Haddad** remained flat at **0.35% (USD 6.33M)** and **Tarcísio de Freitas** at **0.15% (USD 13.57M)**, the highest individual volume in the presidential book, a legacy anomaly that measures negotiated history rather than current conviction.

The most informative movement of the day was in the sub-markets. In [Second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio recovered from 70.50% to **74.00% (USD 201 thousand)**, fully returning the 7.50pp decline from the previous day, and Renan fell from 17.40% to **11.40% (USD 1.07M)**, the largest decline on the panel for the date. In [Third place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan remained steady at **67.00% (USD 153 thousand)**, with Caiado at 16.00%. In plain terms: the market reduced the hypothesis of Renan replacing Flávio in the runoff and maintained him as third-place finisher, without moving his main price.

In [Senate by seats](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), [PL](/en/glossary#pl) declined from 72.00% to **68.50% (USD 258 thousand)** and [MDB](/en/glossary#mdb) fell to 15.45%. The entire book of this market sums to **USD 292 thousand** in accumulated volume, against USD 115.55M in the presidential market, so percentage point variation there does not translate to large money. The [Impeachment of STF minister before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) market rose 0.60pp, to **3.55% (USD 83 thousand)**, one day behind the investigation authorized on July 23, and remains near the floor. In [2026 inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the modal range continues to be 5.00% to 5.49%, at 39.05%, followed by the range 4.50% to 4.99%, at 26.05%, in a book of **USD 77 thousand**, a distribution center that remains above the target ceiling.

## 4. Day's divergences

> **Market × survey:** [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 11.75% for Renan Santos, a level above the 9% from Real Time on July 21, which remains the highest opinion poll reading he has received in the cycle. Gerp and Indexa measure him at 3%, according to the [TSE public records](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/). The test that would arbitrate this distance was [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) this Friday, which was not released, so the distortion carries through the weekend without any new survey.

> **Main price × sub-market:** the same Renan who remained flat in the winner market lost 6.00pp in the [second place in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), while Flávio recovered 3.50pp there. The two movements coexist because they measure different things: who wins the election and who reaches the runoff. The market reduced the second hypothesis without touching the first, and reading only the main price would have hidden the entire day.

> **News × price:** the political agenda on July 24 was mostly favorable to Lula, with the revelation of the [government coordination behind the Progressive Union federation's neutrality](https://g1.globo.com/politica/blog/valdo-cruz/post/2026/07/24/governo-lula-atuou-para-convencer-federacao-pp-uniao-brasil-a-ficar-neutra-e-nao-apoiar-flavio-bolsonaro.ghtml), decided two days earlier. Still, there is no triggering event at the time of the price movement, and the visible effect appeared in the [Senate book](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won), where [PL](/en/glossary#pl) retreated 3.50pp in a market of only USD 292 thousand, and not in the presidential probability of the opponent. Correlation registered, cause not affirmed.

> **Registration × publication:** [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) had a publication date declared to the [TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) for July 24 and did not release it. It is different from the case of [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) on July 23, when articles circulated about a survey whose registered date is July 28, that is, it has not even arrived yet. In both cases the practical conclusion is the same: registration informs intention to publish, not publication, and treating it as a result is the easiest mistake to make in a crowded calendar.

## In summary

1. Lula's gap over Flávio reached +38.55pp in the [presidential market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), the largest since July 9, and this time due to the favorite rising and not the opponent falling. Since there is no triggering event at the time, the honest reading is market momentum, not reaction to news.
2. The day's data on Renan Santos is not in the main price, which remained flat, but in the 6.00pp drop in the [second place sub-market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place). The market stopped treating him as a possible substitute for Flávio in the runoff and kept him as third place.
3. The survey that had a date declared to the [TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) for this Friday was not released, and the panel enters the weekend with the last national reading being from a house that is a recognized outlier. JOTA on July 26, Nexus on July 27, and [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) on July 28 are the next tests.
