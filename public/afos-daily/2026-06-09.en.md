## 1. Prediction market

The day was marked by volatility at the top. On [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Flávio Bolsonaro recovered part of recent losses and rose 2.20pp to 29.35% (USD 6.54M accumulated volume), while Lula remained stable at 40.50% (USD 6.20M). This narrowed the Lula × Flávio gap from +13.35pp to +11.15pp in 24 hours. This is the second short-term rally for Flávio, and no event from the news cycle directly explains it, pointing to a technical recovery reading rather than an isolated news trigger.

In the third way, all candidates declined. Renan Santos fell to 17.15% (USD 6.74M, ↓0.40pp), but maintained third place in the [winner market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election). Camilo Santana dropped to 3.75% (USD 3.23M, ↓0.10pp) and remained in fourth, ahead of Haddad at 2.85% (USD 5.62M, ↓0.10pp). Zema recovered from the previous day's spike and returned to 1.95% (USD 3.53M, ↓0.25pp), and Caiado stood at 1.65% (USD 3.92M, ↓0.10pp), at the floor.

In position markets, Flávio remains the clear leader for [second place](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) at 62.50% (USD 61k, ↓1.00pp), and Renan is the favorite to finish [third in the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) at 43.50% (USD 76k, ↓2.00pp), ahead of Zema (22%) and Caiado (14.50%) in this specific contract.

In institutional and macro markets, the [impeachment of an STF minister](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) contract before 2027 fell to 3.55% (USD 81k, ↓1.90pp), the lowest recent level. In the Senate, [PL](/en/glossary#pl) rose to 73.50% (USD 243k, ↑1.00pp) in the probability of having the [largest caucus](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won). And for 2026 annual inflation, the 5.50% to 5.99% range leads pricing at 26.75%, followed by the 6.00% to 6.49% range at 22.30%.

## 3. What the press covered

The institutional fact of the day was the escalation of the [AtlasIntel](/en/glossary#atlasintel) poll suspension. The injunction was signed on June 8 by [TSE](/en/glossary#tse) president Kassio Nunes Marques, responding to a request from Flávio Bolsonaro for alleged voter inducement, and suspended a survey from May 19 (BR-06939/2026) that showed a decline for the pre-candidate. On June 9, the [research companies association classified the decision as misguided and a dangerous precedent, according to Valor](https://valor.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/06/09/associacao-de-empresas-de-pesquisa-diz-que-decisao-de-nunes-marques-e-equivocada-e-abre-precedente-perigoso.ghtml). Part of the [TSE](/en/glossary#tse) [criticizes the measure and there is an interpretation that the case could reach the STF, according to Estadão](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/ala-do-tse-critica-decisao-de-nunes-marques-de-sustar-pesquisa-e-caso-pode-parar-no-stf/), while another faction [attempts a middle ground, according to Folha](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/colunas/painel/2026/06/ala-do-tse-tenta-encontrar-meio-termo-para-decisao-de-nunes-marques-que-suspendeu-pesquisa-eleitoral.shtml), and there is discussion about postponing the outcome of the ruling. CNN Brasil summarized the episode as a case that [transforms polling methodology into electoral battle](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirwFBVV95cUxQMzBHTERTckRNRktfcjhxYTVkU3F1cnpxS2RVd3p1dV9ranBKZGVVZURHTkdONWl3Z041U1hDYTdmZUlFMGwxVnBYbllfTTBiQXUwdXFybjlNNktwZEFrWnZwSEtuTXJEaWhEWUVBbnhtX213X2E3c1I0QU5IbmlKNWtBM3NmMXNMczQ1TTBhcU91a21BTm1LeGNyZVhDdExjeWY0cXE2UWs0M1dZenR3?oc=5).

In the Master case, Daniel Vorcaro presented a second plea bargain proposal that [cites Ciro Nogueira and the film "Dark Horse", according to G1](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/06/09/caso-master-daniel-vorcaro-cita-dark-horse-e-ciro-nogueira-em-nova-proposta-de-delacao-premiada.ghtml). According to the coverage, the Federal Police assess rejecting the proposal again for contradicting evidence already obtained, and the decision on its approval falls to minister André Mendonça, rapporteur for the case at the [STF](/en/glossary#stf), who days earlier had revoked precautionary measures (O Globo, June 8). The episode also [postpones the definition of Flávio's running mate, according to AJN1](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMihAFBVV95cUxPejNvNDJael9YNWo0U01RemFWMzVFNHBFVXlSODRRRkhfMXNqc3kyU1hPbndCc3dZd280OFNTN3kzdzVXdHNmU09rUGZicUZ3WTc3czRhalpydkgxbW9tTjFhV3VhcUxsQldPM3hSaWx3ZTJEdXFOYWdUeV9BWDRjV0FNd3I?oc=5).

On the right wing, two movements. Flávio structures an economic team, with [Daniella Marques pointed as his main economic adviser, according to Valor](https://valor.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/06/09/daniella-marques-desponta-como-principal-conselheira-economica-de-flavio-bolsonaro.ghtml), cited for the role of "Posto Ipiranga" (also O Globo, June 9). At the same time, the field shows friction: Caiado publicly demanded that Flávio explain his connection to Vorcaro, while Zema stated that [he would support Flávio even after the involvement with the banker, according to Congresso em Foco](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMitAFBVV95cUxNazR3M0t3dE1iVEphU3g4TGNuQ3JEdk5Wc24xOFZHbEtyQWplT2tIRjRLanJmTUZtUVpmZUhuN2tYUjBNWTM3YUhEa1V6SUdXazZKOHBuSUpWRkUxaHBScmpGTGNRay1VcnFxR1d0a2hwYTJ3LV96bXdrQXIxNG9HcGlmbVMwNjR0OHFZaUYtX0puNDA5TWJmZ0ZLd19rRkhzb2xReGlycnpXQldTeE9TZ2RRREg?oc=5). In government, the underlying driver remains the tariff hike, with Lula on the offensive to remove the threat of new tariffs from Trump's mind (Folha).
