## 1. Prediction market

This edition publishes the reading of **Aug 6, 17:44 BRT**, confirmed by two independent readings. The [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) has **Lula at 64.50% (USD 8.09M)** against **Flávio at 26.85% (USD 8.05M)**, a gap of **+37.65pp**. Cumulative volume in the presidential book totals **USD 120.41M**, measured at 18:06 BRT.

**The gap narrowed for the fifth day running.** In the [AFOS series](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) it came from **+41.80pp on Aug 1**, the maximum of the 88-day series, passing through +40.90pp, +38.90pp, +38.60pp and +38.50pp to get here. Lula gave up 1.00pp since Aug 3 and Flávio rose 1.40pp over the same stretch. **No event in the window explains the move**, and no national poll has been released since Aug 5: this is a band exit by momentum, not a reaction to news.

Flávio's level is not an extreme. In the 88-day series, **34 days matched or beat it**, with a peak of 43.30% on May 8 and a floor of 22.00% on Jul 3. Lula's peak remains 66.50%, from Aug 1.

Behind them, **[Renan Santos](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) at 6.90% (USD 9.10M)**, still with more money traded than Lula, and **Caiado at 1.85% (USD 5.56M)**. Zema sits at 0.45% (USD 4.82M).

Among the side markets, [impeachment of an STF justice](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) fell to **2.75% (USD 83 thousand)**, the thinnest contract on this panel, and the **PL sits at 73.00% (USD 293 thousand)** in the [Senate](https://polymarket.com/event/next-brazil-senate-election-most-seats-won) book. ⚠️ [Brazil's 2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) **leaves the screen today**: its bands total **110.20%**, against 99.65% on Aug 5, failing the coherence gate of 95% to 105%. Bands that add past 100 are not probabilities, so AFOS does not publish them as such.

## 2. What the pollsters registered

**No new national poll** in the [TSE registry](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/). The latest remain Genial/Quaest and Meio/Ideia, both from Aug 5 and fielded over the same window, Jul 31 to Aug 3.

On approval, the picture sharpened with a figure that entered the panel today: **three houses measured in the same window and disagree on the sign of the net.** [Quaest](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) gives **48% x 47%**, a positive net of 1pp. Meio/Ideia gives **48.5% x 49%**, a negative net. BTG/Nexus from Aug 3 gives **47% x 48%**, also negative. All three sit within any margin of one another: what is measured is a tie, and which side shows up ahead depends on the house.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × polls:** the [price](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) tightened for **five days running** and the ballot side did not move once in the period, because no national poll came out after Aug 5. The two are not in disagreement: one of them simply was not measured on those days. Attributing the narrowing to vote movement would be inventing a cause.

> **Book × book:** in the [third-place contract for the first round](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), probability migrated from **Renan Santos, who fell from 57.50% to 56.00% over the afternoon**, to **Caiado, who rose from 25.00% on Aug 3 to 35.50%**. In the polls the two are tied within the margin: Renan at 4% and 4.7% in the last two national surveys, Caiado at 4% and 5.7%. The market is separating what the polls do not.

> **Internal coherence:** the [inflation market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) totalled 99.65% on Aug 5 and totals **110.20%** today. Nothing changed in the economy between one day and the next; what changed was the book's arithmetic. That is why the 95% to 105% gate exists.

1. **The gap closed at +37.65pp, a fifth day of narrowing**, down from the +41.80pp peak of Aug 1, with no new poll in the interval.
