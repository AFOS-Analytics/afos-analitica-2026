## 3. What the press covered

The May 26 agenda was consumed by **Flávio Bolsonaro's visit to the White House**, with coverage in at least six prestige outlets simultaneously. As reported by [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/05/26/flavio-trump.ghtml), Flávio was received by Donald Trump amid a crisis involving [Banco Master](/en/glossary#banco-master) and Daniel Vorcaro. [G1](https://g1.globo.com/mundo/noticia/2026/05/26/comitiva-de-flavio-bolsonaro-divulga-foto-do-senador-com-donald-trump.ghtml) and [Estadão](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/flavio-bolsonaro-posta-foto-com-donald-trump-na-casa-branca/) registered the event as a symbolic moment of Trump's formal entry into Brazil's electoral process. In a video released by G1, Flávio called Trump "President Lula" in a Freudian slip corrected shortly after. The editorial analysis by Octávio Guedes in G1 characterized the photo as closer to a "fan meeting an idol" than to a pre-candidate meeting a president.

On the domestic front, **Tarcísio de Freitas ([PL](/en/glossary#pl)-SP)** publicly distanced himself from Flávio in an interview cited by [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/05/26/tem-muitas-questoes-que-ele-mesmo-precisa-explicar-diz-tarcisio-sobre-relacao-de-flavio-com-vorcaro.ghtml), [G1](https://g1.globo.com/sp/sao-paulo/eleicoes/2026/noticia/2026/05/26/tarcisio-diz-que-flavio-bolsonaro-precisa-explicar-visita-a-vorcaro-e-deseja-sucesso-na-viagem-do-pre-candidato-aos-eua.ghtml) and [Estadão](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/tarcisio-sobre-flavio-bolsonaro-tem-muitas-questoes-que-ele-mesmo-precisa-explicar/), stating that Flávio "has many issues he himself needs to explain" about his relationship with Vorcaro. According to coverage in Estadão and InfoMoney (May 26), allies of the Rio de Janeiro governor already consider his Senate candidacy unviable.

The [Banco Master](/en/glossary#banco-master)/Vorcaro front advanced in parallel. The **Federal Police operation against Cláudio Castro** was the headline in [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/05/26/operacao-da-pf-contra-claudio-castro-provoca-tensao-na-alerj-e-amplia-temor-eleitoral-na-base-governista.ghtml) and Estadão (Fausto Macedo blog, May 26), investigating Rioprevidência deposits in Banco Master. Detailed reports from Malu Gaspar's column in [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/malu-gaspar/post/2026/05/daniel-vorcaro-financiou-viagens-e-eventos-de-claudio-castro-ate-no-exterior-diz-pf.ghtml) state that Vorcaro financed Castro's travel and events, including abroad. In [another report from the same outlet](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/05/26/investimento-da-rioprevidencia-no-master-se-deu-por-alinhamento-politico-entre-castro-e-vorcaro-diz-mendonca.ghtml), the [PF](/en/glossary#pf) points to "political alignment" between Castro and Vorcaro as the driver of the investment. Two of Castro's cell phones were seized, according to [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/rio/noticia/2026/05/26/claudio-castro-alvo-da-pf-dois-celulares-sao-apreendidos-diz-advogado.ghtml). Folha de S.Paulo (May 26, Business) reported that the RJ Court of Justice denied a preliminary injunction for the installation of the Master Commission of Inquiry in the State Assembly, closing one of the institutional fronts that attempted to accelerate the case at the regional legislative level.

In Lula's allied camp, **Soraya Thronicke (ex-PSL/Podemos) declared support for reelection** in a move that marginally reorganizes the non-Bolsonaro center-right space, according to coverage in G1 (May 26). On the legislative front, Lula and Hugo Motta finalized the agreement on the 6x1 schedule, with greater transition for concessionaires allowing the matter to move forward without friction (Folha de S.Paulo, Markets, May 26). Finally, Davi Alcolumbre [avoided answering about the installation of the Master Commission of Inquiry](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/05/26/alcolumbre-diz-que-vai-instalar-cpi-da-adultizacao-o-mais-rapido-possivel-e-desconversa-sobre-cpi-sobre-o-master.ghtml) in the Senate, prioritizing the Adultization Commission of Inquiry.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × research:** [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) prices the Lula × Flávio gap at **+11.65pp**, while [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) May 22 registers a [1º turno](/en/glossary#primeiro-turno) gap of only **+9pp** and [2º turno](/en/glossary#segundo-turno) gap of **+4pp**. The sustained distance of at least 2.65pp between the gap priced by the market and the gap measured by the most recent Tier 1 research is the most persistent signal of the cycle, and grew marginally in today's compression.

> **Market × narrative:** The prestige press covered May 26 with headlines dominated by the Trump-Flávio visit + [PF](/en/glossary#pf) Operation against Cláudio Castro — two events that could suggest a negative reading for the Bolsonaro camp. But Flávio on [Polymarket](/en/glossary#polymarket) **rose ↑1.20pp**, against the tide of the narrative. Correct attribution: technical movement of outsider redistribution, not informational reading. Market and press signal opposite vectors in the short term.

> **Research × narrative:** The [Datafolha](/en/glossary#datafolha) May 22 showed Flávio's rejection at 46% (surpassing Lula at 45% for the first time in the cycle), but today's narrative about the US trip is presented by columns in O Globo, Estadão and G1 as a "symbolic meeting of the world's right-wing identity". There is a disconnect between the quantified metric (rejection) and narrative framing (brand affirmation).
