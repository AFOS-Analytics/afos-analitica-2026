## 1. Prediction market

The [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) for the presidential election changed hands. In the confirmed reading of September 10, at 2:20 PM BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro is at **52.85%** (USD 10.17M) and Lula at **43.50%** (USD 10.46M), against 46.25% and 52.50% in the confirmed reading of September 9. The total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to **USD 147.09M**.

The [previous top was 45.80%](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), on September 9. The 43.50% leaves Lula 24.00pp below his own top, of 67.50% on August 16, and the 9.00pp fall in a single day is his largest daily move in the series.

The [second-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) for the first round confirms the same picture from the opposite side: Flávio fell 7.00pp and stands at **71.50%** (USD 718 thousand), while Lula rose 4.90pp and stands at **24.50%** (USD 578 thousand). In other words, the money now sees it as more likely that Lula finishes the first round in second. In the [third-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Augusto Cury is the favourite at **49.25%** (USD 150 thousand), ahead of Renan Santos at **42.50%** (USD 300 thousand) and Ronaldo Caiado at **5.00%** (USD 120 thousand).

Outside the main block, the [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) gave up 0.50pp and stands at **4.35%** (USD 88 thousand), 14.95pp below the top of 19.30% on September 4. The Senate book receives no new price in this round. Renan Santos remains at **2.15%** with **USD 13.15M** accumulated, the largest individual volume in the panel.

## 3. What the press covered

The heaviest act of the day was the Federal Police operation in the investigation into the financing of the film Dark Horse. According to [Poder360](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/dark-horse-pf-ve-frias-como-agente-central-em-esquema/), there were 49 search and seizure warrants in São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Ceará and the Federal District, authorised by Flávio Dino, and the force points to congressman Mario Frias as the central figure in the scheme. [Valor Econômico](https://valor.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/10/mario-frias-repassou-r-2-milhoes-em-emendas-para-ong-de-produtora-de-dark-horse.ghtml) details R$ 2 million in his parliamentary earmarks to the Instituto Conhecer Brasil and R$ 645.4 thousand to the production company, and [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/pf-apreende-armas-ligadas-a-frias-em-operacao-do-caso-dark-horse/) recorded the seizure of weapons. Dino [barred Frias from leaving the country](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/justica/caso-dark-horse-dino-proibe-mario-frias-de-sair-do-brasil/) and from contacting other people under investigation. Flávio Bolsonaro [called the operation an attempt at political interference](https://redir.folha.com.br/redir/online/poder/rss091/*https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/flavio-bolsonaro-diz-que-operacao-que-mira-filme-dark-horse-e-interferencia-politica-de-flavio-dino.shtml).

The chronology has three distinct dates: the plea agreement of the operator of the transfers came out on September 3, the [approval by André Mendonça](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/dark-horse-pf-ve-frias-como-agente-central-em-esquema/) on September 9, and the operation on September 10.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x poll:** both measurements point the same way today, and the difference in intensity is the finding. The [market](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) moved 15.60pp in the gap within 24 hours; the poll measures two points, inside the margin of 1.8, in both scenarios. A price is the probability of winning the election and a poll is declared voting intention, and AFOS does not subtract one from the other.

> **News x price:** the [Federal Police operation](https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-justica/dark-horse-pf-ve-frias-como-agente-central-em-esquema/) reaches the surroundings of the campaign that took the lead in the winner contract, and its price rose 6.60pp on the same day. Both facts are measured and the link between them is not. The page records the coincidence of dates without claiming a cause.

> **Inside the market itself:** the [second-place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) moved in the opposite direction to the winner contract, with Lula rising 4.90pp in the chance of finishing the first round in second. That is not a contradiction: one book asks who wins the election and the other, who comes second in the first stage.

## In summary

**The lead in the [presidential contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) changed hands for the first time in this cycle.** Flávio Bolsonaro rose 6.60pp and stands at 52.85%, Lula fell 9.00pp and stands at 43.50%, and the 6.25pp gap that favoured Lula on September 9 became 9.35pp the other way. The 52.85% is the highest point in the series, which opens on April 14 with 506 readings.

**The poll of the day runs the same way, at an intensity that does not compare.** [PoderData/Aya](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) measures 38% to 36% in the first round and 47% to 45% in the runoff, and the two points fit inside the margin of 1.8 in both scenarios, which makes each of them a statistical tie. It is the seventh consecutive national reading since September 6 without Lula ahead in that pairing.

**The week holds four national polls, and the largest has 40,500 interviews.** [Datafolha and Veritá](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) are scheduled for September 11, Quaest and NEXUS for September 14, all registered with the TSE and none of them confirmed so far.
