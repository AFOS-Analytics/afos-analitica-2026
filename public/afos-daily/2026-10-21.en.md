## 1. Prediction market

**The gap narrows by 3.40 points.** In the confirmed reading of October 21 at 18:28 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 78.70% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 24.38M) and 21.50% for Lula (USD 28.10M). Against the confirmed reading of October 20 at 19:55 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro falls 1.40pp and Lula rises 2.00pp; since the pair's sum went from 99.60 to 100.20, the normalized move is 1.88pp for each side. The gap goes from 60.60pp to 57.20pp. Cumulative volume in the presidential book totals USD 190.93M, and the largest volume in the book is still Lula's.

**Supreme Court and inflation.** The contract on the [impeachment of an STF justice before 2027](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stands at 3.55% (USD 0.10M), 0.75pp above the reading of October 20. ## 3. What the press covered

**The secrecy challenge.** On October 21, Flávio Bolsonaro said he accepts Lula's challenge to open his bank and phone records, with conditions: among them, the opening of the records of Lula's relatives and of his former chief of staff, Marco Aurélio Santana Ribeiro, known as Marcola, and of data on the Presidency's corporate card, according to [CartaCapital](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiuwFBVV95cUxNZDJuVml0enc4Y2VjdDB6clBDcmYtN3FDbGRvc0h4YWJkSDdMNXdrRFhwelgzNWpQTUFScng5Z082dGhZNlMtSlJjRjJsMEEyZG1PVmlVOFdHbDAyVm1taENDczREcFNmR2xnaDVDSk0wNGp3eTRpcXVqWEhoa2J2M2dQS2E2VnRNWlROX0Z4clh1NXBrQS04dnFfYUJyNFFtN0JMT2VYUHJXazA1NjR1bDFhMkhOQTdXVGJn?oc=5). His campaign formalized the conditions with the TSE, according to [Folha de S.Paulo](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/10/flavio-bolsonaro-diz-que-abre-sigilo-se-lula-abrir-o-de-marcola-e-critica-ministros-do-stf.shtml). Lula had filed with the TSE and the Supreme Court, on October 20, the authorization to open his own records if Flávio Bolsonaro did the same.

**The TSE and WhatsApp.** TSE vice-president André Mendonça drafted a resolution to block WhatsApp channels with more than 200 thousand users in the 48 hours before the vote, according to [Folha de S.Paulo](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/10/kassio-e-mendonca-propoem-proibir-canais-de-whatsapp-politicos-nas-48-horas-antes-do-segundo-turno.shtml). On October 21, the court's president, Kassio Nunes Marques, said the court will not consider the proposal in this election, according to [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/eleicoes-2026/noticia/2026/10/21/tse-discute-proposta-para-restringir-canais-politicos-no-whatsapp-a-48-horas-da-eleicao-nunes-marques-diz-que-texto-e-esboco.ghtml).

**Meta and the debate.** UN experts said Meta's conduct may compromise the Brazilian election and asked the company for explanations, according to [CartaCapital](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/mundo/especialistas-da-onu-questionam-a-conduta-da-meta-na-eleicao-brasileira/) and [Estadão](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/eleicoes/especialistas-da-onu-dizem-que-atuacao-da-meta-pode-comprometer-eleicao-e-pedem-reparacoes/). Globo's debate, the last before the vote, is scheduled for October 23.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market × polls:** the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 78.70% for Flávio Bolsonaro, and the five national polls of the day put him ahead by 1 to 2.1 points in total votes, with margins of 1 to 2.5. Price measures the probability of winning on October 25, and the poll measures voting intention during each one's fieldwork.

> **Poll × poll:** with fieldwork overlapping between October 15 and 20, the distance in Flávio Bolsonaro's favor falls in [Quaest](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMijwFBVV95cUxPaXdVTjhib1JzR0lBeFpCVGRsb3hkUFBDbHVkM3Jka0tNNGRuNmVGUUNnMlJUVTA4ZjNnS1F2OHFHVTgxU2lzR0l6V2ZTT0hRZXhXQkdJS1gyS3lJbGpfX05qcmprZ0VLN2dyY01PRG9lVDJsS3FvcGVXQW5EN1VfRzJWNzZycHRzYzJJR2RtSQ?oc=5) and Veritá and rises in Palver and Futura, always within each pollster's margin.

> **Debate × polls:** [Futura/100% Cidades](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMihgFBVV95cUxOYkotZVZzN1RIb2xpRDg3T2RBZ2FRb1ZXTE1KX1JlZ1VBWDFxLTFfdFBnbjlyb0hyREpPYnZvSU1aRVhfVHVVRC0yTEFsbXQ0Q3hRMEUzTzd1empzX05iT3hWR0tPWGtwcURnVkhTbldMR0ZWT1lzM2ZmY0k2dVI3WWEtMC16QQ?oc=5), the first with its entire fieldwork after the Band debate, measures the distance rising from 0.8 to 2.1 points; Quaest, with two of its four fieldwork days after the debate, measures the distance falling from 2 to 1 point.

## In summary

1. **The five national polls of October 21 put Flávio Bolsonaro ahead by 1 to 2.1 points in total votes**, and thirty of the thirty-three conducted after the vote put him in front.
2. **The [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) takes Flávio Bolsonaro from 80.10% to 78.70%**, and the gap goes from 60.60pp to 57.20pp.
3. **Flávio Bolsonaro answered the secrecy challenge with conditions formalized with the TSE**, and the court's president dropped the draft on WhatsApp channels for this election.
