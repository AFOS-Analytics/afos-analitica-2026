## 1. Prediction market

**The gap opens again.** In the confirmed reading of October 10 at 19:19 BRT, the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) pays 88.15% for Flávio Bolsonaro (USD 19.26M) and 11.50% for Lula (USD 22.17M). Against the confirmed reading of October 9 at 14:06 BRT, Lula falls 2.00pp and Flávio Bolsonaro rises 1.00pp; since the sum of the pair went from 100.65 to 99.65, the normalized movement is 1.87pp to each side. The gap goes from 73.65pp to 76.65pp. The total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 179.88M in the 19:39 BRT reading, and the largest volume in the book is still Lula's.
