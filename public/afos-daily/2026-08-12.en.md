## 1. Prediction market

Prices from the [confirmed reading](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) of August 12, 16:41 BRT. **Lula closed at 63.50%** (vol USD 8.22M cumulative), the fourth day running at the same value since August 9. **Flávio Bolsonaro rose 0.40pp, to 27.65%** (vol USD 8.12M), and the gap narrowed to **35.85pp**. The total volume of the presidential book adds up to **USD 122.49M**.

**The largest move of the day was not in the contract about who wins.** In the one about who finishes second in the first round, Flávio rose **3.50pp, from 80.50% to 84.00%** (vol USD 250 thousand). Winning and reaching the runoff are distinct questions, and the money moved the second one. No cause is attributed, because nothing was measured linking the move to an identifiable event.

**Caiado pointed both ways on the same day.** In the contract about winning he fell 0.10pp, to **0.95%** (vol USD 5.66M), below 1% and 0.05pp from the floor of the series, 0.90% from July 7. In the third-place one he rose **2.00pp, to 31.50%** (vol USD 47 thousand). Renan Santos gave way in both: 0.30pp in the presidential, to **7.45%** (vol USD 9.48M), and 1.50pp in the third-place one, to 62.50% (vol USD 178 thousand).

Zema remains at 0.35% (vol USD 5.07M), below the 0.5% cut that separates price from noise, and Haddad rose 0.10pp, to 0.15% (vol USD 7.06M). 📌 And **Jair Bolsonaro, who is ineligible, is priced at 1.20%** (vol USD 5.52M), above Caiado, a figure the panel records without building a thesis on it.

In the other books, impeachment at the STF rose 0.10pp, to **3.90%** (vol USD 83 thousand), the thinnest on the panel, and in the Senate the PL remains at 76.50% (vol USD 259 thousand).

## 3. What the press covered

**The judicial fact of the day is a decision, and it has a defined reach.** Justices [Moraes, Dino, Gilmar Mendes and Zanin ordered seven courts of justice to return amounts paid as penduricalhos](https://www.estadao.com.br/politica/blog-do-fausto-macedo/moraes-determina-devolucao-de-penduricalhos-em-7-tribunais/), citing exorbitant payments. The measure [reaches Goiás, Maranhão, Paraná, Rio de Janeiro, Rio Grande do Norte, Rondônia and the Federal District](https://www.cartacapital.com.br/justica/moraes-suspende-penduricalhos-pagos-a-magistrados-de-sete-tribunais/), with a limit of 70% of the salary ceiling, R$ 46.3 thousand, and the court presidents [have 72 hours to identify the beneficiaries](https://www.brasil247.com/brasil/stf-da-72-horas-para-agu-comprovar-cumprimento-do-teto-e-exige-devolucao-de-penduricalhos-em-sete-tribunais/).

He fell 0.10pp in the one about winning and rose 2.00pp in the one about finishing third. It is not a contradiction: they are two questions, and adding them would erase the information.
