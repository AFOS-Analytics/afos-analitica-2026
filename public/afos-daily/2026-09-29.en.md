## 1. Prediction market

In the [Polymarket winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), confirmed reading of September 29, 11:58 BRT, Flávio Bolsonaro rises 1.75pp, to 59.80% (USD 12.27M), and Lula falls 2.00pp, to 39.50% (USD 12.59M), against the reading of September 28, 13:49 BRT. The gap goes from 16.55pp to 20.30pp. The pair sum fell from 99.55 to 99.30, and the normalized move is 1.91pp to each side. Total accumulated volume in the presidential book adds up to USD 156.82M across 19 contracts, with USD 0.61M of new money.

Neither of them is at an extreme of the series recorded since April 14: Flávio Bolsonaro stands 1.90pp from the top, 61.70% on September 21, and Lula 5.00pp from the floor, 34.50% on April 25. The Renan Santos contract stays at 0.25% (USD 14.68M) and remains the one with the largest accumulated volume in the presidential book.

In the [third place book](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place), Renan Santos rises 8.50pp, to 51.50% (USD 0.46M), and Augusto Cury falls 9.25pp, to 35.85% (USD 0.30M). Normalized by the book sum, which went from 103.10 to 101.85, the move is 8.86pp and 8.55pp. The distance goes from Cury 2.10pp ahead to Renan Santos 15.65pp ahead, and Ronaldo Caiado stays at 13.50% (USD 0.19M). In the [second place one](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place), Flávio Bolsonaro rises 2.00pp, to 76.50% (USD 1.27M), and Lula falls 0.95pp, to 23.60% (USD 1.09M).

The [Supreme Court impeachment contract](https://polymarket.com/event/any-brazil-stf-justice-removed-by-impeachment-before-2027) stays at 2.90% (USD 0.09M). In the [2026 inflation one](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026), the 5.00% to 5.49% band leads with 49.65% (USD 0.02M), in the 12:07 BRT reading.

## 4. Divergences of the day

> **Market x poll:** of the four national polls of September 29, two put Lula ahead in the first round and all four put Flávio Bolsonaro ahead in the second, three of them within the margin. The winner contract widened the gap to 20.30pp in the confirmed reading of 11:58 BRT.

> **First round x second round:** in AtlasIntel, Lula opens 3.1 points in the first round and trails by 0.1 point in the second. In Vox Brasil, he opens 3.3 points and trails by 0.5.

> **Third place:** the book puts Renan Santos 15.65pp ahead, and in the national polls of the day the third-placed candidate changes from firm to firm: Renan Santos in AtlasIntel and Palver, Caiado in Vox Brasil and Cury in Gerp.

## In summary

**The gap widened for the fourth confirmed reading in a row.** It went from 16.55pp to 20.30pp, with Flávio Bolsonaro 1.90pp from the top of the series.
