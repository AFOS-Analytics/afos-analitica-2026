methodology: "Polymarket prices are the reading certified at 17:05 UTC on November 23, 2026 by two captures eight minutes apart; the win contract passed as a whole book, and for the runoff and ballot contracts only individually certified contracts are printed. Kalshi prices are the 17:10 UTC reading of the same day, last traded price in points. Polymarket activity is in US dollars traded, Kalshi's in contracts, from the November 16 close to the last recorded point. Weekly changes run from the November 16 close, the last point recorded that day. Series highs and lows are checked against the full series, which begins on November 8, 2026 for both books. The two books are never added or averaged; the gap is Polymarket minus Kalshi. Only poll lists whose official Commission des sondages notice matches the pollster are used. Counts cover first-round lists with fieldwork ending in the last 45 days; the average is a simple mean over the lists of the last 30 days that include the candidate, printed with how many lists and pollsters it covers, so it changes when lists enter or leave the window. Two published polls with notices, Ifop-Fiducial for Sud Radio (10392) and Toluna Harris Interactive (10393), are not yet structured in our base and are not counted. Under French law (loi 2010-476, article 57) this issue links to no trading page, and prices are shown as measurements. We never subtract a market price from a poll figure."
