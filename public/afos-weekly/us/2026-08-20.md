methodology: "AFOS reads three signals and does not merge them: prediction market prices, published polls, and press coverage. The house average of the generic ballot is a simple mean, unweighted, and it is declared as such on the dashboard. Market prices come from a certified capture, two readings eight minutes apart that must agree inside 0.20pp before anything is published. Band markets are published only when the bands sum between 95% and 105%; the popular-vote-margin market is currently near 151% and is collected daily for the series without being published as a reading. This brief never subtracts a market probability from a polling lead, because they are different quantities. Open source, Apache 2.0."
