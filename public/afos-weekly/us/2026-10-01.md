methodology: "Market prices in this issue are October 1 closes, the last point recorded that day in our daily database backup, compared with the September 24 closes. The House series opens on July 29, 2026 and the Senate on April 14, 2026, so every superlative here means since those dates. Series highs and lows are measured against the full recorded series in that backup, not against the public history route, which caps its window at 90 days and would hide the start of the Senate series. A certified double reading was also taken at 22:08 UTC on October 1 and is reported in the market footnote. The polling average is the simple mean of every national round fielded in the last thirty days, one round per institute per wave, choosing likely voters over registered voters over adults when a house publishes more than one screen. A wave that a house publishes with a different instrument, such as a ballot naming the actual candidates in the respondent's district rather than the generic question, is excluded and listed with its proof. Press coverage comes from a fixed list of 23 outlets, at most two items per outlet, with no political labelling of any outlet."
