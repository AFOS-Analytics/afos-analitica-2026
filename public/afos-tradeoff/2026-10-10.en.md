sinalDaSemana: "The first week of the second round widened the gap in the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) from **68.20pp** at Monday's close to **78.50pp** at Friday's, with **Flávio Bolsonaro at 89.00%** and **Lula at 10.50%**. That is **+10.30pp** in five trading sessions, the third largest weekly move in absolute value among the 26 weeks with at least four paired trading sessions since 14 April, and the second largest among those that opened in favour of Flávio Bolsonaro. The high of **78.50pp**, touched at 19:00 UTC on Friday, is the highest point in the entire paired series, 5.90pp above the previous record, of 72.60pp on the night of the first-round count.\n\nThe week did not move in a straight line. On Monday the gap opened at **61.40pp** at 07:30 UTC, with Lula at 19.50%, and closed the day at 68.20pp, below Sunday's 72.60pp. Tuesday and Wednesday stayed between 68.80pp and 70.00pp. The widening came on Thursday and Friday, +3.40pp and +6.30pp, the same days on which the first four national polls conducted after the first-round vote were released. AFOS measures both with each one's timestamp and does not attribute the price move to the polls.\n\nThe four measured different things. Datafolha (49% to 45%), PoderData/Aya (49% to 44%) and AtlasIntel (51.1% to 45.7%) put Flávio Bolsonaro ahead by 4 to 5.4 points in total votes. Vox Brasil put Lula ahead, by 44.2% to 42.7%, within the margin. The voters of Augusto Cury, who declared his support for Flávio Bolsonaro on 8 October, went to him by 56% to 27% in PoderData/Aya and to Lula by 50.4% to 38.9% in AtlasIntel.\n\nThe contract that took in the most new money was the one that lost the most price: **USD 3.70M** on Lula, against USD 2.09M on Flávio Bolsonaro, in a book that took in **USD 5.78M** over the week and reached **USD 179.00M** accumulated. On Saturday, 10 October, after the window, Ipespe measured 49% to 44% for Flávio Bolsonaro, and the week ahead brings ten national polls registered for 13 to 15 October and the first second-round debate, on 15 October."

methodology: "AFOS does not produce a weighted average and does not arbitrate between sources. It publishes the three signals side by side, contract price, registered poll and news flow, and reports the distance between them when it exists. A market price goes live only after two independent readings eight minutes apart that agree within 0.20pp, contract by contract, and the reference series are measured in the daily backup of the database and not in the public interface. The week runs from Monday to Friday: the published value is Friday's close, the path is the five daily closes, the Δ is Friday minus Monday, and the gap is measured at paired instants, both sides with the same timestamp.\n\nThe polls cited are those registered with the Superior Electoral Court, with protocol, sample, margin and field dates declared, and in the second round AFOS compares the price with each pollster's total vote, not with valid votes. The code and the method are open under the Apache 2.0 licence. Every factual claim in this issue links to its primary source."
