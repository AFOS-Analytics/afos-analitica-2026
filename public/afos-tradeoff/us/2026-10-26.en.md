methodology: "Prices read on AFOS's own route with the country parameter and an uncached reading, and confirmed by the capture lock: two readings 8 minutes apart, released only if they agree within 0.20pp, with the two timestamps required to be distinct. This issue's certified reading is from 26 October 2026 at 04:05:50 UTC, and the real interval between the two timestamps was 8.0 minutes. Each contract's series is read from the database backup and never from the history route, because that route caps the window at 90 days and in the Senate contract that would hide the start of the series, which is 14 April. The piece of series the daily backup does not yet cover is closed with the route's intraday points and checked by a positive control, series by series, comparing the two sources over the window in which both have data: in this issue all five series closed with identical points on both sides, and that is why every superlative here holds for the whole series and not only for what the backup already had. Closes are the last recorded point of each day, and top and floor are intraday, because a daily close hides extremes. The week's delta is anchored to the previous Friday's close, 16 October, which is the convention of this United States series, and the tool that calculates it was required to reproduce the numbers published in the previous issue before being used here. A band distribution is cited only when the book's sum falls between 95% and 105% at the edges used, and it is cited as a share of the book, normalised by the sum at that edge. A band book summing above 100% carries overpricing, and this house tests where it sits by comparing the normalised share of the book's centre against the raw sum across the series: if the centre's share falls when the sum rises, the excess is in the tail; if it rises with it, the excess is in the centre; with no clear relationship, it is approximately uniform. Measured across 671 captures of the Senate seats book, the correlation is -0.40, inside the range this house treats as no clear relationship, between -0.5 and +0.5. In that case normalising preserves the internal proportion, and that is why the distribution here supports statements about SHAPE and never about level, and why no share of it is compared with the price of a binary contract. The generic ballot average is a simple arithmetic mean, unweighted, in a 30 day rolling window, with one round per pollster per wave by the hierarchy likely voter, registered voter, adults, and with a wave published under another instrument, such as the ballot with the names of the district's candidates, kept out of the calculation and declared with the pollster's own wording. Book depth is not cited in this brief, by an editorial decision of May 2026."
