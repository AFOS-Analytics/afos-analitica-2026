sinalDaSemana: "The week of 21 to 25 September was a round trip in the [presidential contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election). At 18:00 UTC on Monday the gap between **Flávio Bolsonaro** and **Lula** reached **23.80pp**, the largest in the entire paired series recorded since 14 April, 7.50pp above any earlier instant. Over the next four sessions it gave ground and closed Friday at **14.90pp**. Within the week, from Monday's close to Friday's, that is **5.80pp** less, the seventh largest shift in absolute value of the 24 weeks with four or more sessions. Against the close that issue №18 published, 14.00pp on 18 September, the distance ended only **0.90pp** higher.\n\nThe pullback is not an effect of the pair's sum. The sum stayed almost still, from 99.70 to 99.90, and the normalized reading gives **2.92pp** to each side: Flávio Bolsonaro from 60.20% to 57.40% and Lula from 39.50% to 42.50%. The intra-week low of the gap, **10.70pp**, came at 15:30 UTC on 24 September, the day six national polls were released, and the range between high and low was **13.10pp** in four days.\n\nThe polls moved much less than the contract. The ten national polls in the window repeat the previous week's split in the second round, with **six putting Flávio Bolsonaro ahead, three putting Lula ahead and one tie**, but the interval between the extremes fell from 14.3 to **7.7 points**. Six pollsters published in both windows, and none moved more than **1.3 point** against itself: three moved up to one point in Lula's direction, two stayed still and one moved 1.3 point in Flávio Bolsonaro's direction. These are different units, a point of voting intention against a percentage point of probability, and the comparison holds as an order of magnitude. This is the last full week before the first round, on 4 October, and the calendar registers seventeen national polls through 2 October."

trackRecord: "Issue №18 assigned about 55% to the base case, in which the national polls of 21 and 24 September would keep the split, with a simple majority giving the second round to the price leader, dispersion between pollsters would stay above 10 points and the gap would settle between 12pp and 20pp. The split held, six to three with one tie. The dispersion did not: it fell to 7.7 points. And the band got four of the five closes right, between 14.00pp and 17.20pp, with Monday's at 20.70pp, above the ceiling; in the recorded readings the gap left the band on both sides, at 23.80pp on Monday and at 10.70pp on Thursday. Of that issue's calendar, nine of the ten pollsters listed published in the window, and the national label of Real Time, which the issue gave as under review, was confirmed at the release of 24 September."

methodology: "AFOS does not produce a weighted average and does not arbitrate between sources. It publishes the three signals side by side, contract price, registered poll and news flow, and reports the distance between them when it exists. A market price goes live only after two independent readings eight minutes apart that agree within 0.20pp, contract by contract, and the reference series are measured in the daily backup of the database and not in the public interface, which caps the window at ninety days. The gap in this issue is measured at paired instants, both sides with the same timestamp, and every claim of an extreme declares the window in which it was checked.\n\nThe polls cited are those registered with the Superior Electoral Court, with protocol, sample, margin and field dates declared, and national scope is a condition of entry into the comparative table. When the scope label depends on a text field that the house repeats without distinguishing, the issue says so in the line instead of publishing the label as fact. The code and the method are open under the Apache 2.0 licence. Every factual claim in this issue links to its primary source."
