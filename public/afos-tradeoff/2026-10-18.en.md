sinalDaSemana: "The week of 12 to 16 October gave back everything the gap in the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) had opened the week before, and more. It went from **74.10pp** at Monday's close to **55.20pp** at Friday's, with **Flávio Bolsonaro at 77.70%** and **Lula at 22.50%**. That is **-18.90pp** in five sessions, the largest weekly drop among the 13 weeks that closed toward Lula since 14 April and the second-largest move in absolute value among 27, behind only the +25.30pp of the week of 7 September. Counted from the 78.50pp close of 9 October, published in №21, it is 23.30pp in seven calendar days.\n\nThe path went almost entirely one way: 74.10, 72.70, 69.00, 59.00 and 55.20pp. The largest step was Thursday's, 15 October, -10.00pp, and the intraday floor, **49.80pp**, came at 16:30 UTC that day, with Lula at 25.50%. On Thursday Datafolha, Palver and Indexa were released, and Lula gave an interview to Jornal Nacional that night; AFOS measures price and fact with each one's timestamp and does not attribute the contract's move to either of them.\n\nThe polls moved much less. The eleven national polls of the week that published total votes gave Flávio Bolsonaro **0.9 to 9 points**, with a simple average of 2.9, against 3.2 the week before. What changed was within each pollster: the three that measured twice during the week, Gerp, Futura/100% Cidades and PoderData/Aya, all moved toward Lula.\n\nThis time the contract that received the most new money was the one that gained price: **USD 2.85M** in Lula and USD 1.66M in Flávio Bolsonaro, in a book that received **USD 4.51M** during the week and reached **USD 184.64M** accumulated. In the [2026 inflation](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-annual-inflation-2026) book, the 5.00% to 5.49% band rose 16.60pp, to 62.60%, with USD 2,071 of new money, in the week in which the Senate approved and Congress promulgated the end of the 6x1 work schedule."

execSummaryIntro: "The theme of the week is the gap between the speed of the two instruments. The contract took 18.90pp off the gap in five sessions and ended the week paying about three and a half times for Flávio Bolsonaro what it pays for Lula, against more than eight times the week before. The polls barely changed on average, from 3.2 to 2.9 points, but they changed within each pollster: those that measured twice saw the distance shrink. None of the eleven national polls with total votes put Lula ahead during the week; the next one to do so was Vox Brasil, on 18 October. methodology: "AFOS does not produce a weighted average and does not arbitrate between sources. It publishes the three signals side by side, contract price, registered poll and news flow, and reports the distance between them when it exists. A market price goes live only after two independent readings eight minutes apart that agree within 0.20pp, contract by contract, and the reference series are measured in the daily backup of the database and not in the public interface. The week runs from Monday to Friday: the published value is Friday's close, the path is the five daily closes, the Δ is Friday minus Monday, and the gap is measured at paired instants, both sides with the same timestamp.\n\nThe polls cited are those registered with the Superior Electoral Court, with protocol, sample, margin and field dates declared, and in the second round AFOS compares the price with each pollster's total vote, not with valid votes. The code and the method are open under the Apache 2.0 licence. Every factual claim in this issue links to its primary source."
