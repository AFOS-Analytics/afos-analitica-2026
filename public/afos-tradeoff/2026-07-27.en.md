In the [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election), Flávio Bolsonaro went from **26.90%** on Monday to **23.10%** on Friday, a fall of **−3.80pp**, giving back with interest the 3.20pp the previous issue reported as a rise with no polling backing. Lula rose 1.00pp, from 60.50% to **61.50%**, and the gap widened from **+33.60pp to +38.40pp**, up 4.80pp in the week. Four national polls were published in the window and all four pointed the same way as the price: Real Time Big Data and Indexa on July 21, Gerp on July 22 and the **Datafolha on July 24 (BR-01166/2026, n=2,004, conf. 0.90)**, which measured a first round of **40 × 32** and a runoff of 48 × 43. In the same week, Flávio's [second place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-2nd-place) fell **−6.00pp**, from 80.00% to 74.00%, while his [third place contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election-first-round-3rd-place) rose **+4.00pp**, from 4.60% to 8.60%. On the other side of that ledger is Renan Santos, formalized as a candidate by Missão on **July 20**, the first day of the window: he rose **+3.30pp** in the winner contract, from 8.50% to 11.80%, and **+6.00pp** in third place, to 67.00%, while three of the four national polls in the week measured him at **3%**. methodology: "AFOS Tradeoff aggregates three signals **without averaging them into a composite**: [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) (USD-denominated, ~30min latency), polls registered with the [TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) (declared voting intention, variable frequency) and 400+ press sources (event flow). When the three diverge, the divergence *is* the signal, not the consensus. USD volume is reported alongside implied probability to separate conviction from artificial spikes. Liquidity (book depth) is not cited inline: low liquidity on Polymarket does not mean the price is wrong, the market is continuously arbitraged.\n\nWeekly deltas in this issue come from snapshots persisted in our own database, not from retroactive reconstruction, and use the **last point of each day**. That produces differences of hundredths relative to the AFOS Daily, which reports the **intraday capture** of the moment it is written: the July 25 gap appears here as +39.60pp and in that day's Daily as +39.65pp, and both readings are correct within their own convention. We record this because the alternative is the reader discovering it alone and not knowing which to believe.\n\nA methodological note on superlatives, valid for the whole series: the public history API caps queries at 90 days, so any 'widest of the cycle' checked through it measures 90 days. The maximum claims in this issue were checked directly against the database and are written as **widest of the series**, with the window declared, never as widest of the cycle."
