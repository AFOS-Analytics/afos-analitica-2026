methodology: "Polymarket prices are read on AFOS's own route with the country parameter and without cache, and confirmed by the capture lock: two readings 8 minutes apart, released only if they agree within 0.20 points. This issue's certified reading is from November 26, 2026, at 18:24:25 UTC, 8.0 minutes after the first. The win and first-round books are certified as whole books, because they are mutually exclusive; the runoff and ballot books are sets of independent contracts and are certified contract by contract, and only certified contracts are cited. Kalshi enters at the price of the hour whenever its reading passes this brief's checks (the book sums between 85 and 120 and the leader has both a bid and an ask); this issue uses the reading of 18:15 UTC. Prices are in points out of 100. The weekly series are read directly from the database, so there is no blind tail between the last backup and now. They start on November 8, 2026, at 19:30 UTC on Polymarket and at 18:25 UTC on Kalshi, and every superlative here says since November 8. A day's close is the last point recorded that day, and tops and floors are intraday. The week's change is anchored to the last point of the previous Thursday. Polls come from the open Mieux Voter base (MIT licence), and each list is checked against the official notice listing of the Commission des sondages. Only lists whose notice matches, which sum to between 97 and 103 and have the shape of a presidential list are used. Averages are simple, unweighted means over lists with fieldwork ending in the last 30 days; counts of places and matchups use 45 days, cut by UTC date; the tool that computes the week of polls was required to reproduce the averages of the AFOS Weekly France No. 1 first. No poll number is typed by hand. Volume is notional, contracts traded times USD 1, as both platforms display it. Book depth is not cited in this brief."
