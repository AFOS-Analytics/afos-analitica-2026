sinalDaSemana: "**The Aug 10-14 window produced seven national polls and the widest dispersion this brief has ever recorded.** The first-round gaps between the top two were **0pp, 4pp, 4.7pp, 5pp, 6pp, 7pp and 13.7pp**. The average comes to **5.77pp**, and none of the seven measured that. On **Aug 11 three national polls came out on the same day**, and the distance between the widest and the narrowest was **13.7pp**: [CNT/MDA](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) printed 42.4% x 28.7% and Gerp printed 38% x 38%, a tie. In the runoff the dispersion is just as wide and more informative: Gerp records a **statistical tie with Flávio numerically ahead, 43% x 45%** inside a 2pp margin, and CNT/MDA puts Lula **+9pp**, 48% x 39%. Eleven points of amplitude on the same question, in the same week.\n\n**The market did not move.** Lula's [winner contract](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) closed **all five sessions of the window at 63.50%**, without a single change in the close, and the gap oscillated inside a **1.30pp** band, between 35.50pp and 36.80pp, ending at **35.60pp** against 37.50pp at the Aug 7 close. Seven polling measurements with 13.7pp of amplitude and a motionless price is the crossing this brief exists to show, and it has two possible readings that AFOS does not choose between: either the book had already priced the whole range, or it does not read polling at that granularity at all.\n\n⚠️ **This issue also scores the scenarios from №12**, which projected exactly this window. The base scenario got the **three market conditions** right and the **two polling conditions** wrong. It is the inverted mirror of №11, whose base got the polling right and the market wrong. Two consecutive issues in which the half of the scenario that speaks about price and the half that speaks about polls do not land together."

methodology: "AFOS Tradeoff aggregates three signals **without averaging them into a composite**: [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/event/brazil-presidential-election) (USD-denominated, ~30min latency), polls registered with the [TSE](https://divulgacandcontas.tse.jus.br/divulga/) and 400+ press sources. When the three diverge, the divergence *is* the signal, and that is what this issue publishes instead of an average gap.\n\nA price only enters after **two independent readings taken eight minutes apart** agree within 0.20pp; a reading that does not confirm is not published. Closing values are the last point recorded each day in BRT, and series are measured on the live window at every issue, never inherited from the previous one. Code and method are open under Apache 2.0 on [GitHub](https://github.com/AFOS-Analytics), and the dataset of validated elections carries a permanent DOI at the Harvard Dataverse."
